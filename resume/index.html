Case Studies

Business-readable case studies

These are intentionally compact and confidentiality-safe. The goal is to show how I frame product questions, choose methods, and translate research into decisions.

AI trust / measurement

Quantifying distrust in an AI-assisted reasoning system

Problem
The team needed a defensible way to evaluate distrust, control, and acceptance, not just whether users found the system useful.
Method
Trust-scale design, Likert measurement, ordinal modeling, user-type comparisons, and public-safe synthesis.
What I did
Turned distrust from a vague concern into measurable constructs that could guide evaluation and product decisions.
Decision value
Helped teams reason about where AI assistance increased confidence, where it triggered suspicion, and what to measure next.

Segmentation / product strategy

Building a persona architecture tied to product decisions

Problem
Static personas were not specific enough to guide roadmap, onboarding, messaging, or measurement decisions.
Method
Mixed-method synthesis across interviews, preference evidence, workflow observations, and behavioral segmentation logic.
What I did
Designed a layered segmentation system connecting behavioral roles, motivations, constraints, and product implications.
Decision value
Moved the work from persona storytelling into an operating model for prioritization and post-launch analysis.

Survey + behavioral analytics

Diagnosing a multi-step product journey with high drop-off

Problem
A product flow was losing users early, but the team lacked a shared explanation for the behavioral and experiential causes.
Method
Journey framing, behavioral baseline design, survey measurement, scorecards, and product analytics interpretation.
What I did
Connected observed drop-off patterns to research questions about trust, clarity, friction, and decision confidence.
Decision value
Created a reusable baseline for prioritization, benchmarking, and evaluating whether future changes improved the experience.
