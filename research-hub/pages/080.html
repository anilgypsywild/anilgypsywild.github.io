Welcome to the Compassion Causes Research Hub.

Welcome to the Compassion Causes Research Hub.

This repository documents ongoing and completed user experience (UX) research activities supporting the Causes platform Compassion International’s digital experience for donors who want to support specific causes with meaningful, direct impact.

This section consolidates all research initiatives related to the Causes platform and Neighbor-to-Neighbor (N2N) giving experiences at CVG. It includes usability benchmarks, donor engagement insights, heuristic evaluations, and email optimization efforts that inform design, messaging, and product development. For N2N, you'll find survey instruments, personas planning, AB testing results, and strategy documents tailored to peer-to-peer fundraising experiences. Each item here serves as a modular reference point to advance our understanding of supporter behavior and improve digital tools that drive mission impact.

Product Overview

Compassion Causes empowers supporters to fund high-impact initiatives across critical focus areas such as clean water, education, disaster relief, and maternal health. The platform highlights transparency, emotional connection, and ease of giving - key factors in deepening donor trust and long-term engagement.

Research Goals

Our Research efforts aim to:

  • Benchmark usability across key donor tasks including discovery, navigation, donation, and post-donation experience.
  • Understand donor motivations and willingness to support cause-based initiatives.
  • Evaluate emotional resonance and clarity of communication across touchpoints such as emails, landing pages, and impact timelines.
  • Refine and validate features that improve engagement, conversion, and retention for both current-gen and next-gen donors.

Research Projects Included

📄 Causes Usability Benchmark Proposal (July 2025)
Defines success metrics, usability tasks, and scoring frameworks to evaluate end-to-end product experience.

🧪 Usability Benchmark Task Set
Detailed walkthrough of the scenarios used to measure performance, satisfaction (SUS), and cognitive load (TLX).

📨 Causes Email Engagement UXR (May 2025)
Assesses comprehension, visual clarity, and emotional impact of marketing and re-engagement emails.

📊 Supporter Motivations Survey Plan
Outlines survey design and segmentation strategy to capture supporter values, trust, and marginal willingness to pay (MWTP).
