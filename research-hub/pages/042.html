Compassion Church-to-Church Partnership Insights (Qualitative Survey)

Prepared for: @AThompson8

Objective

This research aims to understand the language, motivations, and partnership dynamics that shape how Regional Managers (RMs) and Relationship Specialists (RSs) engage with Support Church Partners (SCPs).

The goal is to surface themes, challenges, and opportunities in how Compassion supports church partnerships, that is, to inform both strategic engagement and a subsequent quantitative MaxDiff study.

Insights from this survey may directly inform the factor list used in the Church-to-Church Partnership Motivations (MaxDiff) study.

Key Research Questions

  1. What currently works well in developing and maintaining SCP partnerships with Compassion?
  2. What are the most compelling selling points that drive SCP leaders to partner with Compassion?
  3. What aspects of the partnership excite or motivate SCPs most — and which needs remain unmet?
  4. What support or innovations could make it easier for RMs and RSs to connect SCPs with FCPs (manual or digital)?
  5. What would sustain long-term SCP engagement and inspire continued partnership over time?
  6. How should churches be categorized (small, medium, large), and how do RMs/RSs measure “investment level”?
  7. What offerings or experiences are most valuable for highly invested or large SCPs?
  8. How do RMs/RSs handle cases where SCPs prefer check-based giving over individual sponsorship?
  9. What measurement or signals indicate a deeply invested SCP partnership?
  10. What hasn’t been asked that RMs/RSs believe could improve the Church-to-Church partnership experience?

Methodology: Open-Ended Survey

Approach

A lightweight open-ended Qualtrics or Lyssna survey targeting Compassion’s RMs and RSs.
This qualitative instrument emphasizes guided reflection and narrative responses to identify emotional and operational drivers behind partnership success.

Rationale

  • Captures authentic RM/RS language and terminology.
  • Reveals unprompted themes for quantitative testing.
  • Encourages idea generation beyond predefined choices.
  • Minimizes analysis overhead while enabling downstream quant synthesis.

Survey Design

  • 10–12 guided open-ended prompts (based on the question set above).
  • Some include contextual hints such as “Think of a recent success…” or “Describe an example.”
  • Optional numeric or multiple-choice probes for segmentation (e.g., church size or engagement level).
  • Estimated Time: 10–12 minutes per respondent
  • Respondents: RMs and RSs across multiple regions (target n ≥ 30)
  • Output: Narrative responses coded into themes and potential MaxDiff factors

Preliminary Thematic Areas

  1. Partnership Drivers: What motivates SCPs to partner and stay.
  2. Emotional Resonance: How stories, impact, and mission alignment influence decisions.
  3. Operational Friction: Gaps in support, communication, or resources.
  4. Measurement Frameworks: How “investment” is defined by staff.
  5. Scalability and Recognition: Differentiation by church size and engagement depth.
  6. Innovation Opportunities: Digital tools, new resources, or streamlined processes.

Research Task Breakdown

Scoping and Design

  • Review existing interview questions for clarity and overlap.
  • Add guided hints for more concrete, actionable responses.
  • Program survey in Qualtrics or Lyssna.
  • Align participant list with RM/RS population.

Execution

  • Field survey over a two-week window.
  • Encourage complete, thoughtful responses with clear instructions.
  • Monitor completion rate and response quality.

Analysis

  • Code responses into thematic clusters.
  • Extract 12–15 high-frequency, high-impact factors for the MaxDiff phase.
  • Identify recurring wording used by RMs/RSs to preserve authenticity.

Synthesis and Deliverables

  • Qualitative summary report or wiki page with coded themes.
  • Preliminary factor list for MaxDiff testing.
  • Recommendations for immediate partnership improvements.

Success Metrics

  1. Completion of qualitative dataset (≥ 30 respondents, balanced across regions).
  2. Identification of 10–15 key partnership motivators and barriers.
  3. Validated factor list approved by [name omitted] and Anil for MaxDiff testing.
  4. Shared insight summary to guide product, design, and content strategy.

Appendix:

A1. Companion Notes: Summary of Revisions and Alignment Plan

  • Document: Compassion Church-to-Church Partnership Insights (Qualitative Survey)
  • Purpose: Clarify original objectives, summarize proposed revisions, and define next steps for integration with the follow-up quantitative MaxDiff study.

1. Original Objectives

The initial question set aimed to explore how Regional Managers (RMs) and Relationship Specialists (RSs) perceive and manage Support Church Partner (SCP) relationships.
Key intents included:

  • Identifying what works well in developing SCP partnerships
  • Understanding key motivators and selling points for church leaders
  • Clarifying definitions of church size, engagement, and investment
  • Exploring unmet needs, barriers, and opportunities for better support

The original framing was conversational and qualitative in nature—effective for open dialogue or interviews but requiring adaptation for structured, self-administered survey use.

2. Proposed Revisions

The revised version converts the discussion guide into a structured qualitative survey that maintains the exploratory spirit but adds consistency, analysis readiness, and scalability.

Summary of Key Adjustments

Area Original Focus Proposed Revision Rationale
Structure Conversational flow for live discussion Organized 10–12 open-ended questions with light segmentation Enables self-guided online participation and easier coding
Overlap Multiple questions repeated similar ideas (e.g., excitement vs. desires) Consolidated into laddered prompts combining excitement and unmet needs Reduces redundancy while preserving meaning
Measurement Clarity “Small,” “medium,” and “large” churches undefined Added prompt for unit (attendance, sponsorship count, giving volume) Improves comparability across regions
Behavioral Detail Conceptual discussion Added cues like “Think of a recent success” and “Share one example” Grounds responses in observed behavior
Flow and Grouping Mixed themes throughout Reordered into sections: Motivation → Measurement → Support/Ideas Provides logical progression for respondents
Analysis Readiness No coding structure specified Added analysis plan to code and extract factors for MaxDiff phase Prepares for quantitative follow-up

3. Optional Elements to Retain or Simplify

Element Recommendation Notes
Segmentation prompts (church size, region, etc.) Optional Include only if cross-tab analysis is desired; otherwise keep purely qualitative
Example prompts Recommended Adds context and improves response quality
“Three Wishes” item Optional Useful for generating visionary ideas; can be retained or removed based on timing
“Check vs. sponsorship” item Recommended Highlights a recurring strategic tension worth quantifying later
Final open-ended wrap-up (“What did we not ask…”) Optional Retain if survey duration remains under 12 minutes

4. Rationale for Revision

  1. Scalability: The structure now supports asynchronous survey deployment in Qualtrics or Lyssna without needing a moderator.
  2. Analytical Clarity: Responses can be coded consistently into themes suitable for MaxDiff factor generation.
  3. Continuity: The conversational tone is preserved, maintaining the trust and openness needed for honest RM/RS feedback.
  4. Strategic Value: Creates a direct pipeline between this qualitative phase and the planned quantitative validation study, minimizing redundancy.

5. Integration with Quantitative Phase

Following completion of the qualitative survey:

  • Responses will be coded to identify high-impact themes and candidate partnership factors.
  • These factors will form the input list for the Church-to-Church Partnership MaxDiff Study, which will quantify the relative importance of each.
  • The combined outcome will link qualitative narratives (“why and how”) with quantitative prioritization (“what matters most”).
  • Estimated additional workload for this integration is approximately 10% beyond standard qualitative analysis.

A3. Proposed Next Step: Overview of MaxDiff Extension

Purpose
Quantify which partnership factors matter most, using a short MaxDiff survey that complements the qualitative survey. This follows the same structure and reporting approach used in the Donor Motivations MaxDiff brief.

Scope of what carries forward from the qualitative survey

  1. Code open ended responses to surface a clean list of candidate factors
  2. Consolidate to about twelve to fifteen single idea items, written in the same language used by respondents
  3. Remove overlaps and merge near duplicates
  4. Keep a few stretch ideas for learning value

Respondents
Regional Managers and Relationship Specialists. Optionally include a small set of Support Church Partner leaders for an external view.

Survey design in brief

  1. Use MaxDiff best worst scaling with sets of four or five items per screen
  2. Each respondent selects the most impactful and the least impactful item per set
  3. Balanced design to ensure fair exposure across items
  4. Field in Qualtrics or Lyssna and export for R based analysis, following the prior MaxDiff workflow and reporting format.

Segmentation to plan for

  1. Church size definition and unit used such as attendance, sponsorship count, or giving volume
  2. Region
  3. Role such as RM versus RS
  4. Observed investment level where available

Primary outputs

  1. Ranked importance scores for each factor
  2. Top drivers overall and by segment
  3. Pared list of three to five recommendations tied to the top drivers
  4. Visuals and structure that mirror the Donor Motivations MaxDiff readout for easy leadership review.

Data quality and risk controls

  1. Keep item wording simple and single idea
  2. Pilot with a small internal set to confirm timing and comprehension
  3. Target at least forty to fifty completes to allow stable ranks and segment views

Effort and timing
This is an incremental layer on top of the qualitative survey. Programming and analysis are a light lift using the established MaxDiff template and R scripts. Estimated additional effort is about ten percent beyond coding the qualitative results.

Hand off and ownership
The qualitative survey produces the factor list. The MaxDiff phase quantifies priorities. The combined readout integrates narrative insights with a defensible priority ranking, consistent with prior Compassion MaxDiff practice.
