Study Overview

Objective

  • This diary study aims to capture real-world, high-level insights from multi-millionaire High-Impact Donors (HIDs) engaging with the Supporter Platform Beta.
  • The focus is on critical usability, engagement, and trust indicators, ensuring streamlined and impactful feedback.
  • This approach ensures minimal participant effort, maximum impact insights, and a research structure that prioritizes what truly matters to highly affluent HIDs.

Key Research Questions

  1. Adoption & Engagement: Does the platform provide immediate, clear value?
  2. Time-to-Value (TTV): How quickly can HIDs find relevant content?
  3. Trust & Credibility: Does the platform inspire confidence in donation impact?
  4. Feature Relevance: Which features are essential vs. unnecessary?
  5. Barriers to Use: What are the biggest friction points?

Study Design

Participants

  • 8–10 High-Impact Donors (HIDs) selected from the beta cohort.
  • Strict screening to ensure active engagement in philanthropy & digital platforms.

Duration

  • 2 weeks per participant (shorter, focused engagement).
  • Alternate-day diary entries + one final debrief interview.

Methodology

  • Simple, structured diary prompts (mobile-friendly format).
  • Weekly check-ins replaced with one final high-impact discussion.
  • Minimal burden, maximum insight approach.

Simplified Diary Entry Template

1️⃣ Access & Usage

  • Did you log in today? (Yes/No)
  • What was your primary reason for logging in? (Multiple choice: Check donation impact, Explore new content, Engage with community, Other)

2️⃣ Feature Use & Engagement

  • Which features did you use? (Checkbox: Dashboard, Reports, Community, Notifications)
  • How valuable was your experience today? (1-5 scale)

3️⃣ Trust & Impact

  • Do you feel your donations are making a measurable impact? (1-5 scale)
  • What specific information would increase your confidence? (Open-ended)

4️⃣ Barriers & Friction

  • Did anything frustrate or slow you down today? (Yes/No)
  • If yes, what? (Multiple choice: Navigation, Content clarity, Feature usability, Other)

5️⃣ Final Reflection

  • Would you recommend this platform to another donor? (Yes/No)
  • One change that would make this platform better for you? (Open-ended)

Final High-Impact Discussion

  • What was the most valuable part of the platform?
  • What would make you log in more often?
  • What needs immediate improvement?
  • Would you continue using this long-term? Why or why not?

Analysis & Reporting

  • Quantitative insights: Aggregated scores from diary responses.
  • Qualitative insights: Key trends from final debrief interviews.
  • Feature impact vs. actual engagement: What donors say vs. what they do.
  • Platform trust score: HID confidence in donation transparency.

Outcome & Deliverables

By the end of the Beta, this study will provide:

  • ✅ Concise, executive-level insights into HID engagement behaviors.
  • ✅ Red-flag usability issues identified with clear recommendations.
  • ✅ Trust and credibility benchmarks for donor confidence.
  • ✅ Strategic improvements to optimize the HID experience before full-scale launch.
