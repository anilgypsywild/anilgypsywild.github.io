Research article

Author: Anil Shankar | April 2025 | UX

🎯 Purpose

This proposal outlines a UX Scorecard framework to longitudinally measure and improve the Compassion Causes platform using standardized and emotionally resonant metrics.

It supports the April 2025 post-production benchmark initiative and complements the Causes deck prepared for [name omitted] and [name omitted] (PM Lead for Causes).

We aim to track core donor workflows using SUPR-Q and HEART (Happiness, Engagement, Attention, Retention, Task-Metrics) frameworks, aligned with Compassion's mission to maximize trust, emotional resonance, and generosity.

Our goal is to support the creation of a Maximum Lovable Product—not just usable or functional, but deeply inspiring and friction-free for donors.

⏳ Core Workflows to Benchmark

The following user journeys have been identified as critical to user engagement, trust, and donation conversion. They were derived from mixed-methods testing, stakeholder discussions, and site analytics:

  1. Homepage Exploration and Cause Discovery
  2. Viewing a Cause Details Page
  3. Adding a Cause to the Donation Cart
  4. Completing the Donation Checkout Flow
  5. Multi-Cause Donation Adjustment
  6. Urgent Cause Identification and Engagement
  7. Cultural Context Exploration
  8. Donation Confirmation and Post-Donation Feedback
  9. (Future) Fundraiser Discovery (P2P)
  10. (Future) Fundraiser Setup (P2P)

❤️ How We Measure Maximum Lovable Product-ness (MLP-ness)

We combine SUPR-Q and HEART metrics to evaluate the following product qualities:

Quality Metric Examples
Emotional Resonance Appeal ratings, happiness scale, qual quotes
Ease and Joy of Use Usability, task success, error rate
Trust and Confidence Trust score, NPS, “Would donate again?” survey
Community Energy Social shares, referrals, email opt-ins
Flexibility and Control Multi-cause giving metrics, slider interaction rate

📊 Tracking Plan (Quarterly and Release-Based)

Release Stage Metrics Focus
Q2 (Baseline) All live-site workflows from Home to Confirmation
Q3 (Enhancements) Add A/B test metrics for Urgent Tags, etc.
Q4 (Growth Loop) Loyalty triggers, emotional impact, personalization UX

💲 Strategic Outcomes

By implementing this UX scorecard, the Causes team can:

  • Monitor donor journeys across releases and A/B tests
  • Identify which parts of the experience drive donation intent, trust, and retention
  • Share clear KPIs with leadership that tie back to donor experience and platform ROI
  • Build a repeatable and defensible UX measurement system that scales with product growth

Next Steps

  1. Align metrics with product analytics (Optimizely, Mixpanel) to capture live task data
    1. Define Events to track based on the above tasks
    2. Convert the flows into tasks on UserZoom, test the tasks, launch in segments of interest
  2. Define survey questions for SUPR-Q and emotional appeal benchmarks
  3. Launch Q2 baseline testing and establish quarterly reporting cadence
