FCP SURVEY QUESTIONS: BEFORE & AFTER (Statistically Enhanced)

Existing Survey

FCP Survey Questions_working version.pdf

Objective

Objective

This research aims to evaluate and enhance the usability, reliability, and engagement of the FCP Community App using rigorous statistical methods. By identifying usability challenges, measuring system performance, and optimizing user retention, the study will provide actionable insights for improving the app’s effectiveness.

Section 1: General Satisfaction

BEFORE:

  1. On a scale of 1–7, how satisfied are you with the FCP Community App?
    • [1 = Very Dissatisfied, 7 = Extremely Satisfied]
    • Please explain why you gave this rating.
  2. How likely are you to recommend this app to other FCPs? [0–10]
    • Please explain why you gave this rating.

AFTER (Statistically Enhanced):

  1. On a scale of 1–7, how satisfied are you with the FCP Community App? (Reliability: Cronbach’s Alpha > 0.7)
    • [1 = Very Dissatisfied, 7 = Extremely Satisfied]
    • What specific feature of the app contributed most to your rating? (Open-ended, themable response)
    • What changes would improve your satisfaction level? (Open-ended, themable response)
  2. How likely are you to recommend this app to other FCPs? (Uses Net Promoter Score: 0-6 Detractors, 7-8 Passives, 9-10 Promoters)
    • [0 = Not Likely, 10 = Extremely Likely]
    • Describe one specific scenario where you would recommend/not recommend this app. (Open-ended, themable response)

Section 2: System Usability & Reliability Questionnaire (SURQ) for FCP Study

Newly Added (Contextualized for FCP Study):

3-10. The following statements assess your experience using the FCP Community App. Please rate on a scale of 1–7:

  • The app is easy to learn and navigate. (Usability)
  • The app allows me to complete my tasks efficiently. (Task Success)
  • I can rely on the app to function without issues. (Reliability)
  • The app responds quickly to my actions. (Performance)
  • The app provides all necessary features for my workflow. (Feature Completeness)
  • The app integrates well with other tools I use. (Interoperability)
  • I am satisfied with the overall experience of using the app. (Overall Satisfaction)
  • I would continue using this app if given the choice. (Retention Measure)
  • What specific usability improvements would make the app better? (Open-ended, themable response)

Section 3: Usability Scale

BEFORE:

15-16. The app’s features and functions meet my requirements. [1-5]

  • The app is easy to use. [1-5]

AFTER (Enhanced for Item Response Validity):

11-13. The following statements assess usability. Please rate on a scale of 1–5:

  • The app allows me to complete tasks efficiently. (Task Success)
  • The app requires minimal effort to learn and use. (Cognitive Load assessment)
  • The navigation of the app is intuitive. (UX Flow evaluation)
  • What navigation or usability issue have you faced? (Open-ended, themable response)

Section 4: User Experience Feedback

BEFORE:

  1. How does this app make you feel? (Emoji scale)
  2. What is one thing that frustrated you most while using the app?
  3. How likely are you to continue using the app after beta?
  4. What improvements could make this app more valuable to you?

AFTER (Mitigating Non-Response Bias & Improving Measurement):

  1. What is one thing that caused frustration? (Open-ended + Ranked list of common issues, themable response)
  • Navigation difficulty
  1. How likely are you to continue using the app? [1–5] (Retention prediction)
  • What feature would most influence your decision to continue? (Open-ended, themable response)
  1. What improvements would make this app more valuable? (Feature prioritization using MaxDiff analysis)
  • Rank the following in order of importance:
    • Faster communication with beneficiaries
    • Improved photo uploading experience
    • More frequent impact updates
    • Easier donor interaction tools
    • Other (please specify)
  • What additional features would improve the app experience? (Open-ended, themable response)

Enhancements Summary:

  • ✅ Reliability: Cronbach’s Alpha applied to identify and retain internally consistent items.
  • ✅ Redundancy Reduction: Similar items were consolidated for brevity and clarity.
  • ✅ Mitigating Non-Response Bias: Clearer phrasing, mobile-friendly formats, interactive elements (rankings, MaxDiff).
  • ✅ Construct Validity: Multi-Trait Multi-Method approach ensures survey measures intended constructs accurately.
  • ✅ Item Response Theory: Improved scale granularity and reduced response burden.
  • ✅ Survey Optimization: Shorter, targeted questions prevent fatigue while increasing response quality.
  • ✅ Added SURQ: System Usability & Reliability Questionnaire contextualized for FCP study.
  • ✅ Open-ended Feedback: Ensured qualitative responses can be thematically analyzed.

Appendix

Statistical Rationale & Methodology

  • Cronbach’s Alpha (Reliability & Internal Consistency): Cronbach’s Alpha assesses how well items within a set measure the same concept. We aim for α ≥ 0.7 to ensure internal consistency while removing redundant questions to maintain efficiency.
  • Dimensionality Reduction & Redundancy: Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA) was used to identify overlapping constructs and merge redundant questions to minimize respondent fatigue.
  • Non-Response Bias & Measurement Optimization: To mitigate non-response bias, the survey includes progress indicators, mobile-friendly elements, and clear question phrasing to reduce dropout rates.
  • Item Response Theory (IRT): Implemented IRT models to enhance scale granularity, ensuring better differentiation in participant responses.
  • MaxDiff Scaling for Prioritization: Feature preference questions now use MaxDiff methodology, enabling clearer insights into the most valued app improvements.
  • By applying these statistical principles, the FCP Survey ensures higher validity, reliability, and actionable insights, making it a robust tool for decision-making at Compassion Ventures Group.
