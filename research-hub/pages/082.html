Next Gen vs Current Gen

Generational UX and Motivation Report

Prepared for @AThompson8
Sources: CVG Persona System 2025 and N2N Survey Dec 2024

Executive Takeaways

  1. Next Gen
    • “Show me what is changing right now.”
    • “If the experience slows down, I leave.”
    • “Authenticity matters more than institutional tone.”
    • “Let me see it, not read about it.”
    • “Trust starts only after I see transparency.”
  2. Current Gen
    • “Demonstrate stewardship and long term commitment.”
    • “Faith alignment sets the foundation for trust.”
    • “I stay when reporting stays consistent.”
    • “Urgency is persuasive when it is honest and grounded.”
    • “Clarity, simplicity, and continuity matter most.”

1. Motivation

Next Gen Current Gen
Driven by emotion, urgency, and authenticity Driven by faith alignment, stewardship, and responsible impact
Responds to transformation images Responds to mission rooted narratives
Wants clear measurable goals Wants long term continuity
Follows peer momentum Follows mission consistency

Takeaway
Next Gen responds to immediacy
Current Gen responds to integrity

2. Trust

Next Gen Current Gen
Low initial trust High initial trust
Needs transparency and visual evidence Needs mission credibility
Rejects formal institutional tone Rejects youth tone and hype
Trust increases with proof Trust increases with continued reporting

Takeaway
Next Gen seeks proof
Current Gen seeks continuity

3. UX Expectations

Next Gen Current Gen
Fast, mobile first, visual experiences Calm, structured, predictable experiences
Minimal reading required Will read to confirm understanding
Real time progress feedback Stable periodic updates
Emotionally authentic design Pastoral tone and reassurance

Takeaway
Design fast for Next Gen
Design clear for Current Gen

4. Emotional Resonance

Next Gen Current Gen
Strongest with transformation images Strongest with personal stories
Motivated by measurable goals Motivated by urgency framed through stewardship
Faith resonance grows later Faith resonance is immediate
Responds poorly to long text Accepts narrative depth

Takeaway
Next Gen responds to visuals
Current Gen responds to people

5. Giving Behavior

Next Gen Current Gen
Small and frequent contributions Larger and planned contributions
Shares campaigns often Shares sparingly
Prefers goals under 500 Comfortable with moderate to high goals
Uses analytics tools frequently Uses analytics moderately

Takeaway
Next Gen fuels momentum
Current Gen sustains impact

6. Social Behavior

Next Gen Current Gen
Social proof matters Social proof has minimal effect
Heavy use of group chats and short video Use of email and church channels
Motivated by group challenges Motivated by collective purpose
Shares updates often Shares updates rarely

Takeaway
Next Gen follows community energy
Current Gen follows mission alignment

7. Messaging Strategy

Next Gen Current Gen
Lead with visuals Lead with clarity
Provide minimal text upfront Provide full narrative context
Delay explicit faith messaging Begin with faith alignment
Highlight transparency and proof Highlight stewardship and trust

Takeaway
Next Gen needs to feel confident through transparency
Current Gen needs to feel grounded through clarity

8. Product Implications

Next Gen Current Gen
Requires speed, visual confirmation, and transparency Requires stability, clarity, and responsible reporting
Leaves quickly if friction appears Leaves when complexity grows
Wants to act immediately Wants to understand then act
Needs instant share tools Needs dependable update tools

Takeaway
Both groups punish friction, but in different ways

9. N2N Implications

Next Gen Current Gen
Prefers team challenges and countdowns Prefers steady multi week campaigns
Goals under 500 work best Larger goals are acceptable
High sharing volume Moderate sharing volume
Peer energy drives participation Purpose and mission drive participation

Takeaway
Next Gen campaigns scale quickly
Current Gen campaigns sustain outcomes

10. Recommendations for 2026 Planning

  1. Build two parallel messaging paths: visual to narrative, and faith to stewardship
  2. Create Next Gen rapid flow modes with instant proof
  3. Strengthen Current Gen continuity messaging in email and post gift sequences
  4. Use age based segmentation to trigger appropriate onboarding, tone, and update cadence
  5. Prioritize transparency features for Next Gen and reporting quality for Current Gen
  6. Adjust N2N tools to support group challenges for younger users and structured campaigns for older users
