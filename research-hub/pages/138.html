Mission Statement

SERVE HIM

Guided by our calling to serve Jesus Christ, research at CVG anchors Christian principles into our mission is to elevate user experience by fostering empathy, integrity, and service in all our endeavors. Inspired by the teachings of Jesus, we aim to create meaningful, inclusive, and ethical digital experiences that reflect God’s love and care for all people.

Vision Statement

To become stewards of compassionate design, transforming lives by ensuring that every digital interaction reflects the values of love, humility, justice, and service. Rooted in Scripture and dedicated to serving users with honesty and patience, we strive to make every touchpoint a testament to God’s grace and our commitment to His call to serve others.

Context of the STARS Model for CVG's UX Team:

Our UX team at CVG l is currently positioned between the Start-Up (S) and Turnaround (T) phases in the STARS model, having recently navigated through a Realignment (R) phase. This transitional stage provides a unique opportunity to leverage our foundational improvements while addressing emerging challenges and scaling our processes.

  1. Start-Up Elements:
    • Developing and implementing core UX processes.
    • Establishing trust with engineering and other cross-functional teams.
  2. Turnaround Elements:
    • Overcoming initial skepticism of UX research value.
    • Building credibility through quick wins and data-driven results.
  3. Post-Realignment Insights:
    • Ensuring alignment of UX efforts with organizational goals.
    • Addressing lessons learned from the realignment phase to refine strategies for user engagement and product improvement.

SERVE HIM: The CVG Research Philosophy

S – Seek Understanding

We begin with humility and a listening heart, ensuring that every decision is grounded in the real needs of donors, supporters, and front-line partners.

"Let the wise hear and increase in learning, and the one who understands obtain guidance."
— Proverbs 1:5 (ESV)

E – Engage with Empathy

True research is not just about numbers—it’s about people. Every donor, partner, and recipient has a story. Our job is to listen deeply and design in ways that reflect Christ’s love.

"Rejoice with those who rejoice, weep with those who weep."
— Romans 12:15 (ESV)

"Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ."
— Galatians 6:2 (ESV)

R – Research to Reveal, Not Just Confirm

We do not use research to justify what we already believe. Instead, we ask, listen, and learn—seeking truth rather than validating assumptions.

"The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps."
— Proverbs 14:15 (ESV)

"Teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe in your commandments."
— Psalm 119:66 (ESV)

V – Validate with Wisdom

Every decision must be tested and refined, but we do not let data override faithful impact. Mission over metrics.

"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given him."
— James 1:5 (ESV)

"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is insight."
— Proverbs 9:10 (ESV)

E – Elevate Kingdom Impact

User experience at CVG is not just about usability—it’s about spiritual, emotional, and relational engagement. Our research must help people give joyfully, serve effectively, and grow in faith.

"As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace."
— 1 Peter 4:10 (ESV)

"Let all that you do be done in love."
— 1 Corinthians 16:14 (ESV)

H – Honor the User’s Journey

Every donor, church partner, and recipient is on a spiritual journey. Our research must support this journey, ensuring our tools do not just increase donations but deepen faith and connection.

"Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding."
— Proverbs 3:5 (ESV)

"And let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works."
— Hebrews 10:24 (ESV)

I – Iterate with Integrity

We refine, improve, and test—but always with honesty and humility. We never manipulate behavior; instead, we equip people to give and serve authentically.

"Better is a little with righteousness than great revenues with injustice."
— Proverbs 16:8 (ESV)

"Whoever walks in integrity walks securely, but he who makes his ways crooked will be found out."
— Proverbs 10:9 (ESV)

M – Measure What Matters

Not all success is measured in clicks, engagement rates, or revenue. True impact is found in lives changed, faith deepened, and burdens lifted.

"So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to everyone, and especially to those who are of the household of faith."
— Galatians 6:10 (ESV)

"By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another."
— John 13:35 (ESV)
