🚆 Events to Track for the Supporter Platform (Beta Launch) in Mixpanel – Focused on High-Impact Donors (HIDs)

Goal: Track engagement behaviors of High-Impact Donors (HIDs) who are less focused on donations through the platform and more interested in impact statements, visual updates, and their role in driving change.

1. Onboarding & First-Time Engagement

Why? Ensure HIDs complete the onboarding process and set up their preferences for tracking impact.

✅ Events to Track:

  • Onboarding Started → HID begins the onboarding process.
  • Onboarding Completed → HID completes the onboarding flow.
  • Profile Created → HID personalizes impact tracking preferences (e.g., causes of interest).
  • Tutorial Skipped → HID bypasses onboarding guidance.

KPIs:

  • Completion rate of onboarding.
  • % of HIDs customizing impact preferences.

2. Impact Tracking & Engagement

Why? Understand how HIDs interact with content that showcases their philanthropic impact.

✅ Events to Track:

  • Impact Dashboard Viewed → HID checks their impact overview.
  • Impact Statement Opened → HID reads a detailed impact report.
  • Impact Video Watched → HID watches a story/video related to their funding.
  • Before & After Comparison Viewed → HID engages with content showing transformation over time.
  • Impact Metrics Expanded → HID drills down into granular donation allocation and results.

KPIs:

  • % of HIDs regularly engaging with their impact dashboard.
  • % of HIDs who view visual proof of change (videos, before/after).
  • Time spent engaging with impact statements & success stories.

3. Recognition & Social Influence

Why? Track how HIDs engage with recognition and peer networks.

✅ Events to Track:

  • Leaderboard Viewed → HID checks their standing among top supporters (if available).
  • Donor Spotlight Opened → HID reads a feature on a fellow philanthropist.
  • Cause Shared → HID shares their impact story via email or social media.
  • Recognition Badge Earned → HID receives acknowledgment for funding milestones.

KPIs:

  • Engagement rate with leaderboards & recognition features.
  • % of HIDs sharing their impact to encourage peer giving.

4. Relationship Management & Organizational Engagement

Why? Ensure HIDs maintain an ongoing relationship with the organization.

✅ Events to Track:

  • Private Message Sent → HID contacts a Compassion Representative.
  • Invite to Private Event Accepted → HID agrees to attend an exclusive gathering.
  • Annual Report Opened → HID reviews detailed financial and impact reports.
  • One-on-One Impact Call Scheduled → HID requests a direct conversation with leadership.

KPIs:

  • % of HIDs actively communicating with Compassion staff.
  • % engaging with annual reports & in-depth updates.
  • Retention rate of repeat HID engagement year-over-year.

5. Portfolio & Multi-Cause Engagement

Why? Track how HIDs engage with multiple causes and diversify their philanthropic contributions.

✅ Events to Track:

  • New Cause Followed → HID expresses interest in additional causes.
  • Multi-Cause Dashboard Viewed → HID tracks multiple impact areas at once.
  • Cause Portfolio Report Opened → HID reviews how their funds are distributed across causes.

KPIs:

  • % of HIDs engaging with more than one cause.
  • Average number of causes followed per HID.

6. Retention & Churn Prevention

Why? Ensure ongoing engagement and prevent drop-offs.

✅ Events to Track:

  • 30-Day Inactivity → HID has not engaged in a month.
  • Impact Alert Opened → HID reads a personalized update (email, app notification).
  • Re-engagement Action Taken → HID returns after a nudge (e.g., seeing a new impact update).

KPIs:

  • Retention rate of high-value HIDs.
  • Effectiveness of personalized re-engagement efforts.

Implementation Priorities in Mixpanel

  1. Define Event Properties (e.g., HID type, preferred impact categories).
  2. Create Funnels for HID journeys from onboarding → impact tracking → long-term engagement.
  3. Set Up Dashboards to monitor recurring engagement vs. drop-offs.
  4. A/B Test how different impact storytelling methods (videos vs. data-heavy reports) drive engagement.

Outcome:

By tracking HID engagement with impact updates, recognition, and ongoing involvement, the platform can refine its approach to keeping multi-millionaire donors engaged and invested in long-term philanthropy. 🚀
