Research Brief

Closed Card Sort Validation for Outcome Based Cause Categorization

  • Date: February 2, 2026
  • Status: Follow up validation study

1. Background and Context

Item Description
Prior research Open card sort has been completed, synthesized, and delivered
🔥 Core insight Supporters group causes by human outcome rather than operational category
Gap Research interpretation and validation have not yet been formally completed
Purpose of follow up Validate whether proposed categories remain intuitive under constrained conditions

This study builds directly on the completed open card sort and is intended to stabilize category decisions that are already under consideration.

2. What Do We Know Now

Based on the delivered open card sort results, the following has been established:

Confirmed Insight Evidence Source
Outcome driven grouping Repeating supporter groupings across participants
Category candidates Stable outcome themes emerged without imposed structure
Language patterns Supporter authored descriptions explain why causes belong together
⚠️ Friction points Existing functional categories fail to communicate impact clearly
🧨 Decision risk Category directions have been discussed without validation under constraint

The closed card sort tests whether these insights hold when supporters must choose among predefined options.

3. Problem Statement

Problem Risk if Unaddressed
Outcome based categories not validated Ambiguous or overlapping IA
Category labels not stress tested Misclassification during discovery
⚠️ Decisions moving faster than research Reduced traceability to supporter data
🧨 Exploratory findings treated as final Weak evidence for downstream product work

This study addresses a risky transition from exploration to confirmation.

4. Why This Study Is Happening Now

Factor Context
🧩 Timing Research results requested by Friday
Turnaround Approximately four days
Product state Category directions already being reviewed
⚠️ Research role Introduce validation before decisions are finalized

This timing necessitates a focused, confirmatory method rather than additional discovery.

5. Research Objectives

Objective Type Objective
Primary Validate supporter ability to classify causes into proposed categories
Primary Standard card sort goal: Identify confusion, overlap, or breakdowns
Primary Standard card sort goal: Assess semantic clarity of category labels
Secondary Reinforce outcome language associated with categories

6. Key Research Questions

Question Area Research Question
Classification Can supporters consistently assign causes to predefined categories?
Agreement Which categories show strong alignment versus disagreement?
Friction Where do supporters hesitate or misclassify?
Semantics Do labels clearly communicate outcomes without explanation?

7. Test Artifacts

Artifact Source
Cause list From completed open card sort
Category labels Derived from open card sort synthesis
Category descriptions Neutral, outcome focused
Instructions Emphasize meaning and outcomes

8. Data Collected

Data Type Measures
Quantitative Assignment frequency, agreement rates
Quantitative Overlap and misclassification patterns
Qualitative Outcome language per category

9. Outputs and Use of Results

Output Used For
Validated category structure Information architecture
Label performance evidence Navigation and filtering
Misclassification insights Category refinement
Traceable documentation ⚠️ Product and leadership decisions
