Research article

Objective

Develop a structured research framework to measure the ongoing experience of high-impact (net-worth) donors (HIDs) using the Supporter Platform, ensuring that insights inform engagement, retention, and long-term platform value while respecting their expectations and behavior.

Research Approach

1. Onboarding & Early Experience

Assess how donors experience their first interactions with the platform, ensuring that it immediately communicates value and drives early engagement.

2. Core Platform Engagement & Feature Use

Measure how donors interact with key platform features over time, identifying which elements sustain engagement and which present friction points.

3. Ongoing Experience & Retention Indicators

Track longitudinal engagement patterns to understand what motivates donors to stay engaged or disengage, ensuring the platform maintains relevance.

4. Feedback Collection & Continuous Optimization

Implement structured, event-driven feedback mechanisms to collect insights without disrupting the donor experience, ensuring an ongoing refinement process.

Implementation Considerations

  • Research must be integrated into the donor experience seamlessly.
  • Feedback collection should be lightweight, strategic, and positioned as exclusive.
  • Passive behavioral tracking should supplement direct user feedback.

Next Steps

✅ Refine detailed methodology and measurement approach (Effort for the next two days).
✅ Align ongoing experience research with platform evolution priorities.
✅ Define structured feedback collection methods for long-term insights.
✅ Prepare initial stakeholder materials for review.
