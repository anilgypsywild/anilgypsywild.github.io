Causes Usability Benchmark: Summary & Insights

DataSet: CausesBCData_ForR.csv

Lysnna Results are here: [reference omitted]

Q1: If you were looking for Causes to donate again, would you follow the same path or try a different way?

Quantitative Results

  • Current-Gen:
    • Mean Difficulty Rating: 1.6 (SD 0.9), Median: 1
    • % "Very Easy" (1–2): 83%
    • Mean Time on Task: 29s (Median: 25s)
  • Next-Gen:
    • Mean Difficulty Rating: 1.4 (SD 0.7), Median: 1
    • % "Very Easy" (1–2): 93%
    • Mean Time on Task: 26s (Median: 23s)

Insights

  • Nearly all users in both cohorts would repeat their path due to ease and directness.
  • Next-Gen users found the process marginally easier and faster, with less rating variance.
  • A minority in both groups wanted more advanced search or filtering.

Recommendations

  • Retain a clear, direct discovery path.
  • Surface advanced filters and suggested searches for targeted seekers.
  • Enable users to save preferred navigation sequences.

Q2: How confident are you that your donation will be used as described?

Quantitative Results

  • Current-Gen:
    • Mean Confidence Rating: 5.4 (SD 1.6), Median: 6
    • % High Confidence (6–7): 60%
    • % Low Confidence (≤4): 21%
  • Next-Gen:
    • Mean Confidence Rating: 6.2 (SD 0.7), Median: 6
    • % High Confidence (6–7): 78%
    • % Low Confidence (≤4): 2%

Insights

  • Next-Gen trust is significantly higher, with almost no low scores.
  • Current-Gen confidence is moderate; a notable minority remains uncertain.
  • Transparency and impact proof remain key needs for both groups.

Recommendations

  • Add visual allocation indicators and testimonials near donation CTAs.
  • Use real-time banners (“Your gift just helped X”) to reinforce trust.

Q3: What motivates you most when deciding to donate to a cause?

Quantitative Results

  • Top motivator (both groups): Cause impact/transparency (Current-Gen: 77%, Next-Gen: 69%)
  • Faith as motivator: Current-Gen: 60%, Next-Gen: 53%
  • Personal connection: Current-Gen: 64%, Next-Gen: 65%
  • Emotional content: Current-Gen: 30%, Next-Gen: 39%

Insights

  • Impact and transparency are primary drivers.
  • Next-Gen is more motivated by emotional storytelling and social proof.
  • Faith remains important, especially for Current-Gen.

Recommendations

  • Emphasize impact stats and follow-up stories.
  • Include optional faith alignment features and personal connection modules.

Q4: When you donate, how much do you typically give at one time? (Marginal Willingness to Pay)

Quantitative Results

Donation Range Current-Gen (%) Next-Gen (%)
10–24 17 39
25–49 32 24
$50+ 40 20
Less than $10 11 12
  • Current-Gen Median Gift: $35
  • Next-Gen Median Gift: $20

Insights

  • Current-Gen donors give more per donation; Next-Gen is more price-sensitive.
  • Both groups respond to “anchor” amounts but Next-Gen is more budget-conscious.

Recommendations

  • Present $50+ options for Current-Gen, 10–25 for Next-Gen.
  • Use micro-goal framing to link small gifts to tangible outcomes.

Q5: How clear and trustworthy is the donation information?

Quantitative Results

  • Clarity (6–7 on 7-pt scale): Current-Gen: 79%, Next-Gen: 86%
  • Trust (6–7 on 7-pt scale): Current-Gen: 59%, Next-Gen: 64%

Insights

  • Both groups generally trust the information, but mid-range (4–5) ratings indicate some skepticism.
  • Visual clarity and consistent messaging increase perceived trust.

Recommendations

  • Add impact summaries, badges, and source references.
  • Standardize presentation of allocation and outcome data.

Q6: How likely are you to recommend Compassion Causes to a friend or colleague? (NPS)

Quantitative Results

Segment Current-Gen (%) Next-Gen (%)
Promoters 35 36
Passives 25 36
Detractors 33 28
NPS 2 8
  • Mean Rating: Current-Gen: 6.96, Next-Gen: 7.55

Insights

  • Next-Gen has higher NPS and fewer detractors.
  • Key opportunity: Move passives to promoters with emotional impact and referral nudges.

Recommendations

  • Trigger referral prompts after high satisfaction.
  • Use testimonials and shareable impact stories.

Q7: Marginal Willingness to Pay (MTWP) Open

Quantitative Results

  • Current-Gen: Mode and median at $25; 10% gave $50+; 30% gave $20 or less
  • Next-Gen: Mode and median at $25; 30% gave $20 or less; very few gave over $25

Insights

  • $25 anchoring is effective; Current-Gen shows more “stretch” to higher gifts.
  • Next-Gen more budget-conscious and rationale-driven.

Recommendations

  • Test higher anchors for stretch; offer “round up” and “add $5” nudges.

Q8: Findability of a Specific Cause

Quantitative Results

  • “Very Easy” Rating: Current-Gen: 72%, Next-Gen: 86%
  • Mean Time on Task: Current-Gen: 18s, Next-Gen: 16s

Insights

  • Both groups found causes like Education/Water with ease; Next-Gen slightly faster and more confident.
  • Labeling and categories worked well.

Recommendations

  • Maintain clear, consistent category labels and visual cues.
  • Offer recent searches and favorites.

Q9: Catch Trial – Finding a Nonexistent Cause

Quantitative Results

  • “Very Easy” (1): Current-Gen: 30%, Next-Gen: 43%
  • “Very Difficult” (7): Current-Gen: 23%, Next-Gen: 8%

Insights

  • Current-Gen had higher frustration and abandonment when causes were missing.
  • Next-Gen used broader terms and found alternatives more easily.

Recommendations

  • Design “no results” fallback with suggested similar causes.
  • Enable user requests for missing types.

Q10: Satisfaction with Confirmation/Follow-up (CSAT)

Quantitative Results

  • Mean Satisfaction: Current-Gen: 4.1, Next-Gen: 4.6 (on 7-point scale)
  • % Highly Satisfied (6–7): Current-Gen: 29%, Next-Gen: 42%

Insights

  • Next-Gen more satisfied overall; both groups want more emotional and personalized confirmation.
  • Absence of clear thank-you and follow-up is a key pain point.

Recommendations

  • Add warm, personalized confirmation and thank-you messaging.
  • Offer clear receipts, impact stories, and follow-up updates.

Executive Summary

  • Ease of use is high for both cohorts, but Next-Gen consistently rates tasks as easier and faster.
  • Trust and transparency remain essential, especially for Current-Gen.
  • Donation habits differ: Current-Gen is more generous per gift; Next-Gen responds to lower anchors and emotional cues.
  • Refinements: Focus on emotional engagement, clear impact, and robust fallback experiences to convert satisfaction into advocacy and higher giving.

End of Report
