🎯 Objective

Establish a multi-method, multi-stage research strategy to support the Supporter Platform Beta rollout to High-Impact Donors (HIDs), ensuring that user experience is measured, optimized, and refined using quantitative rigor and qualitative depth.

🔍 Research Overview

The March–June 2025 Beta is a critical period for testing how HIDs interact with the Supporter Platform.

To drive data-backed decision-making, this research strategy integrates:
✔ Survey-based statistical design for scalability & reliability.
✔ SUPR-Q's 8 dimensions of usability, credibility, design, and loyalty.
✔ Behavioral analytics for engagement, retention, and feature adoption tracking.
✔ Qualitative UX and HCI methodologies for deep user insights.
✔ A/B testing & experimental design to optimize feature-level performance.

This multi-method approach ensures that insights translate into real platform enhancements before full-scale deployment.

🛠️ Research Framework: Multi-Stage Approach

This research is divided into four progressive stages, each building upon prior insights to ensure a comprehensive understanding of user behaviors and pain points.

1️⃣ Phase 1: Onboarding & Early Experience (March – April 2025)

📌 Objective: Assess first-time user experience to ensure seamless adoption and engagement.

🔹 Methods:
✔ SUPR-Q Survey (Usability & Appearance)
✔ Usability Testing (Moderated) of Onboarding Flows
✔ Behavioral Analytics: Drop-Off & Time-to-Value (TTV) Tracking

🔹 Key Metrics:
📊 Time-to-Value (TTV): Time from login → first engagement.
📊 Drop-Off Rate: % of users abandoning onboarding.
📊 SUPR-Q Usability Score: Ease of navigation & task completion.
📊 SUPR-Q Appearance Score: Perceived design appeal & clarity.

🔹 Research Outputs:
✅ Onboarding UX optimization recommendations.
✅ TTV benchmarks for later comparison.

2️⃣ Phase 2: Core Platform Engagement & Feature Adoption (April – May 2025)

📌 Objective: Understand what features sustain engagement and what causes friction.

🔹 Methods:
✔ SUPR-Q Survey (Credibility & Loyalty)
✔ A/B Testing: Personalized Notifications vs. Standard Alerts
✔ User Interviews: Feature Use & Perceived Value
✔ Behavioral Analytics: Interaction Frequency & Click-Through Rates (CTR)

🔹 Key Metrics:
📊 CTR on Personalized Notifications vs. generic alerts.
📊 Feature Utilization Rate: % of HIDs engaging with core features.
📊 SUPR-Q Credibility Score: Trust in platform content & donor updates.
📊 Qualitative Sentiment: Which features are resonating and which aren’t?

🔹 Research Outputs:
✅ Feature engagement insights to refine notification & personalization strategies.
✅ Data-driven prioritization of features.

3️⃣ Phase 3: Ongoing Experience & Retention (May – June 2025)

📌 Objective: Identify long-term engagement drivers & attrition risks.

🔹 Methods:
✔ Longitudinal Survey: Tracking Satisfaction Over Time
✔ Behavioral Cohort Analysis: Retention Trends
✔ CSAT Tracking: Perceived Impact of Donations
✔ Think-Aloud Sessions: Assessing Emotional Connection to Platform

🔹 Key Metrics:
📊 Retention Rate: % of users returning weekly/monthly.
📊 CSAT (Customer Satisfaction) Score: Confidence in donation impact.
📊 Engagement Fatigue Signals: Drop in activity over time.
📊 SUPR-Q Loyalty Score: Willingness to continue using & recommending platform.

🔹 Research Outputs:
✅ Predictive indicators of donor churn.
✅ Recommendations for engagement nudges (reminders, content recommendations, etc.).

4️⃣ Phase 4: Beta Retrospective & Optimization (June 2025)

📌 Objective: Synthesize insights from the entire beta to prepare for full-scale launch.

🔹 Methods:
✔ Post-Beta SUPR-Q Survey: Full User Experience Assessment
✔ Final Behavioral Audit: Comparing Pre vs. Post Engagement Metrics
✔ Stakeholder Workshops: Translating Research into Design Priorities

🔹 Key Metrics:
📊 Net Promoter Score (NPS): % of users likely to recommend.
📊 Final Retention Rate: % of HIDs still active by Beta end.
📊 SUPR-Q Aggregated Score: Overall platform usability, credibility, design, & loyalty.

🔹 Research Outputs:
✅ Beta Report: Comprehensive findings + UX/design recommendations.
✅ Prioritized Feature Fixes & Enhancements.

📈 Research Design Considerations: UX & HCI Best Practices

To ensure rigor and methodological validity, this strategy incorporates:

🔹 Statistical Survey Design (Ref: Survey Stat Design Doc)

  • Reliability assurance (Cronbach’s Alpha ≥ 0.7) & bias reduction techniques.
  • SUPR-Q percentile benchmarking to measure UX performance against industry standards.

🔹 Mixed-Methods Approach (Ref: Quant & Qual Research Methods Doc)

  • Quantitative Analysis: Behavioral analytics, SUPR-Q benchmarking, A/B testing.
  • Qualitative Depth: In-depth interviews, diary studies, usability heuristics testing.

🔹 UX Metrics & Validity (Ref: Supporter Beta Research Strategy Doc)

  • Multi-Trait Multi-Method (MTMM) testing to validate constructs.
  • SUPR-Q for trend analysis to identify UX improvements over time.
