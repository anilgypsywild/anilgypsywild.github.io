Introduction (Moderator or Automated Script)

Usability Testing Script for UserZoom – Supporter App

Study Type: Moderated or Unmoderated Usability Test
Platform: UserZoom
Participants: Supporters (High Impact Donors)
Estimated Duration: 20-25 minutes
Devices: Mobile (iOS/Android)

Introduction (Moderator or Automated Script)

"Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God’s grace in its various forms." – 1 Peter 4:10

Welcome, and may God bless you for your generosity and heart for giving.

Thank you for taking the time to participate in this usability study. Your role as a Supporter is essential, and we want to ensure that the Supporter App provides you with a seamless, impactful, and deeply engaging experience.

This is not a test of you, but rather a test of the platform. There are no right or wrong answers—just share your honest thoughts as you complete the tasks.

We ask that you think out loud while navigating the app. If anything is confusing, frustrating, or unexpected, please let us know. Your feedback will help us improve the experience so that you can see, track, and experience the fruits of your giving.

When you are ready, please begin.

Task 1: First Impressions & Navigation (3-5 min)

Scenario:

As a Supporter, you regularly contribute to meaningful causes. You have just logged into the Supporter App for the first time and want to see how effectively it surfaces impact insights and ways to engage.

Instructions:

  1. Explore the homepage for about 30-60 seconds.
  2. Without tapping anything, describe what you think you can do on this screen.
  3. Now, try to find where you can track your past donations and see the direct impact they’ve made.

Follow-up Questions:

  • What are your first impressions of the homepage?
  • Was it immediately clear where to find information about your giving impact?
  • Did you feel that the app respected your time and surfaced the most important information upfront?
  • Was there anything that felt missing or unclear?

Data Collection:

  • Screen recording (UserZoom)
  • Open-ended verbal feedback
  • Click paths & time-on-task tracking

Task 2: Viewing Personalized Impact Stories (3-5 min)

Scenario:

You want to see how your specific contributions are changing lives. You open the Impact section to view the latest updates.

Instructions:

  1. Navigate to the Impact section in the app.
  2. Scroll through the updates and select a story that you feel most connected to.
  3. Read the details and describe whether the information was compelling and relevant to you as a significant contributor.

Follow-up Questions:

  • Did the impact stories feel personalized to your giving or too generic?
  • Was the content emotionally compelling and informative, or did it lack depth?
  • Were the numbers and statistics presented in a way that made you feel your contributions truly made a difference?
  • Would this kind of reporting encourage you to give more or engage further?

Data Collection:

  • Screen recording (UserZoom)
  • Emotional response ratings
  • Task completion success

Task 3: Notifications & Engagement (2-4 min)

Scenario:

You receive frequent updates from the Supporter App about impact stories, donor engagement, and responses to your comments. You want to check whether these updates add value or feel overwhelming.

Instructions:

  1. Navigate to the Notifications tab.
  2. Review your recent notifications.
  3. Identify which notification feels most relevant and valuable to you.

Follow-up Questions:

  • Were the notifications high-value, or did they feel excessive?
  • Did they make you feel recognized and connected to the causes you support?
  • Would you prefer a different way to receive updates (e.g., weekly digest, real-time, email, or in-app summaries)?
  • What information do you wish was included in these notifications?

Data Collection:

  • Click tracking & time spent per notification
  • Open-ended responses

Task 4: Commenting & Community Engagement (3-5 min)

Scenario:

As a Supporter, you want to encourage and engage with communities, ministries, or other donors by commenting on impact updates. However, your comment is unexpectedly blocked.

Instructions:

  1. Navigate to the Impact section and select a post.
  2. Attempt to leave a comment that expresses encouragement or engagement.
  3. If your comment is not posted, review the moderation message.

Follow-up Questions:

  • Did the moderation policy make sense to you as a high-value donor?
  • Were the moderation reasons clear and fair, or did they feel restrictive?
  • Would this discourage you from interacting further, or would you try another comment?

Data Collection:

  • Task completion rate
  • User frustration points

Task 5: Closing & Final Thoughts (2-3 min)

Scenario:

You’ve now explored the core features of the Supporter App. Before finishing, we’d like your final thoughts.

Instructions:

  1. Give the app a rating from 1 to 10 on usability.
  2. Share one thing you liked the most about the experience.
  3. Share one thing you would improve to better serve Supporters.

Follow-up Questions:

  • Did this app make you feel more connected to the causes you support?
  • Would you continue using this app to track impact and engage further?
  • Would you recommend this app to other Supporters? Why or why not?

Data Collection:

  • Final user rating (scale 1-10)
  • Open-ended feedback

Closing Message:

"Whoever is generous to the poor lends to the Lord, and He will repay him for his deed." – Proverbs 19:17

Thank you for your time and valuable insights. Your generosity fuels meaningful change, and your feedback helps us ensure that this platform honors your contributions, respects your time, and deepens your connection to the work you support. May God bless you abundantly.
