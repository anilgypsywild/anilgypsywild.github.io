Assessing Core Navigation and Intuitiveness: Enhancing Content Moderation, Notification Feed Comprehension, and Impact Screen Expectations

Research Problem to Solve:

Major donors to Compassion International desire timely and meaningful updates that clearly demonstrate the impact of their contributions. The current communication system, however, is affected by delays, inefficiencies, and limitations that undermine donor trust, transparency, and connection to the cause.

Key Pain Points:

  1. Communication delays hinder timely updates to donors.
  2. Manual processes for translation and staff coordination are time-consuming and inefficient.
  3. Relationships with donors feel isolated and lack a personal touch.
  4. Restricted freedom of expression limits the richness and authenticity of updates.

Why Solve This Problem Now?

  1. The FCP Community app, beta launched last year, is expanding to additional countries within the next 1-2 months.
  2. Adding donors to this pilot is critical to supporting and motivating FCP staff who are actively sharing updates.
  3. To successfully onboard new donors, it is essential to test a mid-fidelity prototype to evaluate and refine the navigation and clarity of the current wireframes.

What Do We Know About the Problem? 

Insights from Data, Analytics, and Feedback

  1. In July 2022, a Net Promoter Survey conducted by Compassion USA highlighted child communication as a top area for improvement.
  2. A 2020 segmentation study revealed that 83% of U.S. supporters preferred receiving photos as an alternative form of communication.
  3. Recent donor interviews with those participating in the 1:Many sponsorship model, where a single donor supports an entire FCP, revealed dissatisfaction with the lack of personal updates, typically provided quarterly via email. These donors expressed a strong desire for deeper connections with the FCPs they support, wishing to encourage and pray for them but feeling uncertain about how to do so effectively.

Research Goals

Primary Goals

  1. Evaluate the efficiency and clarity of navigation.
  2. Assess the ease of locating essential information.
  3. Measure user satisfaction with the information architecture (IA).
  4. Identify usability challenges and areas of friction.

User-Testing Tasks

Scenario description

App Context: Compassion Community is an app where major donors are able to see and learn about the communities they are supporting across the world. Though this functions similarly to a social media app, it is designed to be more intentional and engaging in meaningful moments. It’s a way to share real stories and engage with real neighbors. 

Supporter Context: 

You are a major donor, giving $25k to Compassion yearly. You want to be able to see, engage with, and respond to church partners that you are supporting. 

Task 1: Evaluate the Onboarding Screens

Instruction: Imagine you have just downloaded the app for the first time. Walk through the introduction screens and think aloud about your experience. Focus on clarity, intuitiveness, and overall messaging.

Metrics to Capture:

  • Success Rate: Percentage of participants completing the onboarding without confusion.
  • Difficulty: Perceived ease of the process (1-5 Likert scale).
  • Qualitative Feedback: Spontaneous comments on visuals, content, and flow.

Task 2: Login and Create Your Profile

Instruction:Using the provided credentials, log in to the app and proceed to create your profile. Share your thoughts aloud about each step, focusing on clarity and usability.

Metrics to Capture: same as Task 1

Task 3: Evaluate Notification Feed

Instruction: Imagine you’ve received a notification and are checking it for the first time. Explore the Notification Feed and share your thoughts about its organization and clarity.

Metrics to Capture: same as Task 1

Task 4: Content Moderation Feed

Instruction:You’ve received a notification that a comment was not posted. Explore the Content Moderation Feed to understand why. Share your expectations and thoughts about the process.

Metrics to Capture: same as Task 1

Task 5: Explore Impact Timeline

Instruction:Navigate to the Impact Timeline page and explore its features. Share your thoughts on its purpose, organization, and interactivity.

Metrics to Capture: same as Task 1

RITE UX Metrics

  1. System Usability Scale (SUS): Participant's usability rating (standardized score).
  2. Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT): Satisfaction with the app on a 1-5 Likert scale.
  3. Net Promoter Score (NPS): Likelihood of recommending the app to others.

UserZoom : Research Insights

Executive Summary

The usability assessment of the Supporter app demonstrates strengths in simplicity and user engagement but highlights areas for improvement in clarity, trust, and emotional connection. Key metrics indicate:

  • CSAT (Customer Satisfaction): 4.4 / 7 — Moderate satisfaction, with nearly half of users expressing positive sentiment but room for improvement in key areas.
  • SUS (System Usability Score): 59.2 / 100 — Below the industry average of 68, indicating usability challenges.
  • NPS (Net Promoter Score): -64 — A high percentage of detractors reveals dissatisfaction and a lack of advocacy for the app.

Task 4 - Content Moderation Feed

Question: How much do you trust the process of involving a Relationship Manager to resolve comment-related issues?

Trust in the Relationship Manager Process

  • Trust Score Distribution:

    • 41% rated their trust as Neutral (3).
    • 47% expressed low trust (1 or 2).
    • Only 12% reported Complete Trust (5).

    Theme

    Feedback

    Actionable Insights for Designers

    Lack of Clarity in Moderation

    Many participants felt the process was unclear or vague, especially regarding why comments weren’t posted.

    Provide detailed explanations of why comments are flagged, using plain language and examples to make the process more transparent.

    Confusion About Resolution

    Several users were unsure how their issues would be resolved or if follow-up communication would occur.

    Add clear messaging about next steps after a comment is flagged and ensure users are updated on the resolution status.

    Objection to “Relationship Manager” Terminology

    Some participants found the term confusing or inappropriate, likening it to a dating site.

    Replace "Relationship Manager" with a more neutral and descriptive title, such as "Support Team" or "Moderation Team."

    Positive Respectful Tone

    A few participants appreciated the respectful handling of moderation issues.

    Maintain the respectful tone of moderation interactions while improving transparency and clarity.

    Outlier Experiences

    A small number of participants did not encounter or notice the moderation process in their interaction.

    Review navigation and visibility of moderation banners to ensure all users can easily engage with the process.

    Representative Feedback Quotes

    • Lack of Clarity:
      • "To me it wasn’t very clear why it wasn’t posted. Sure they explained it but it didn’t really come through very clear to me." (C6790S124_16)
      • "A little vague on reason not posted, but assume there will be a follow-up communication coming." (C6790S124_48)
    • Confusion About Resolution:
      • "No, it was not clear on how my issue would be resolved." (C6790S124_47)
      • "No it didn’t. I really don’t know how this was resolved." (C6790S124_35)
    • Terminology Concerns:
      • "Don’t like the use of the word 'Relationship Manager.' That word has many different interpretations." (C6790S124_26)
      • "It sounds like a title that would be used on a dating site." (C6790S124_36)
    • Positive Feedback:
      • "Respectful and I appreciate how that’s handled." (C6790S124_34)
      • "Yes. Very good." (C6790S124_39)

Expectations After a Comment is Flagged

Theme

Feedback

Actionable Insights for Designers

Desire for Detailed Explanations

Many participants expect clear, specific reasons why their comment was flagged.

Provide detailed explanations for flagged comments, including the exact reason and how the post violated guidelines.

Opportunity to Modify Comments

Some users want the ability to review and edit their flagged comments to align with guidelines.

Add an option for users to edit flagged comments with direct links to guidelines to help them resolve the issue.

Expectation for Notification

Several users expect to be notified when their comment is flagged, often with follow-ups from a Relationship Manager.

 Ensure immediate notifications are sent for flagged comments, with clear next steps and the option to contact support.

Timely Follow-ups

 Users expect follow-ups within a reasonable timeframe (e.g., 1–2 days) for resolution.

Establish a clear SLA (service level agreement) for follow-up communications, ensuring timely responses to flagged issues.

Mixed Opinions on Relationship Manager Role

 Some users prefer no person involved, while others want personal communication.

Offer both automated explanations and optional human follow-up for users who need additional clarification.

Representative Feedback Quotes

  • Desire for Detailed Explanations:
    • "Notify me in exact and clear language why my comment was flagged." (C6790S124_35)
    • "Exactly what I saw, however I want detail as to why it was flagged." (C6790S124_10)
  • Opportunity to Modify Comments:
    • "Moderators notify the poster of the violation. Poster should be able to modify the post to meet guidelines." (C6790S124_30)
  • Expectation for Notification:
    • "Notify me. Follow-ups from Relationship Manager." (C6790S124_24)
    • "You should contact me." (C6790S124_38)
  • Timely Follow-ups:
    • "I would hope I’d get an answer to my post within 1 to 2 days." (C6790S124_26)

Suggestions to Improve the Comment Moderation Experience 

Theme

Feedback

Actionable Insights for Designers

Desire for Detailed Explanations

Many participants emphasized the need for more detail on why comments were flagged.

 Provide specific, user-friendly explanations for flagged comments, highlighting the exact guideline violated.

Direct Guideline References

Some users found it cumbersome to click links to review guidelines and figure out violations.

Display the specific guideline directly in the notification instead of requiring users to click external links.

Support for New Users

A few participants noted that the phrasing felt more suited to experienced users than newcomers.

Tailor messaging to be accessible for new users, including step-by-step guidance for resolving flagged comments.

Avoid Escalation for Simple Issues

One participant felt escalation to a Relationship Manager was unnecessary for minor comment issues.

 Provide automated solutions or self-service options before escalating to a Relationship Manager.

Representative Feedback Quotes

  • Desire for Detailed Explanations:
    • "Detail on why it was flagged." (C6790S124_10)
    • "It could be more thoroughly explained as to why it was not posted." (C6790S124_20)
    • "Give more information as to why the comment needed moderation." (C6790S124_36)
  • Direct Guideline References:
    • "A direct comment of the guidelines would be helpful." (C6790S124_30)
  • Support for New Users:
    • "Explain in more detail because this person is new to the app and probably doesn’t really understand why it was not posted." (C6790S124_16)

Clarity of the Impact Timeline Page

Actionable Insights for Designers

Maintain Strengths:

  • The majority (70%) found the purpose of the Impact Timeline clear or very clear. Retain the current structure and information hierarchy.

Understanding and Expectations of the Impact Timeline Page 

Question: What do you believe the Impact Timeline page is for? Does it meet your expectations?

Theme

Feedback

Actionable Insights for Designers

Showcasing Impact

Many participants believe the page is meant to show the results or impact of donations.

Ensure the page clearly highlights the connection between donations and measurable outcomes (e.g., direct beneficiary stories or statistics).

Lack of Clarity

Several participants felt the page lacked clear explanations of what it represents or how to interpret the data.

Add a brief introductory section or tooltips to explain the purpose of the page and the meaning behind key metrics.

Unmet Expectations

Some users were disappointed, mentioning the lack of meaningful or detailed statistics about projects.

 Include specific, actionable statistics and project outcomes to provide more value to users seeking granular details.

Desire for Community-Level Impact

Some participants wanted insights at a community or collective donor level, emphasizing teamwork and shared impact.

Add a section showcasing the collective impact of donor contributions to emphasize community-driven outcomes.

Representative Feedback Quotes

  • Showcasing Impact:
    • "So the donors can see what is being done with the donated money, how it is supporting the community." (C6790S124_35)
    • "To show the impact on the target groups that the work of the organization has brought about." (C6790S124_48)
  • Lack of Clarity:
    • "It does not and again there needs explanations as to what is going on." (C6790S124_29)
    • "I guess it’s to view the accomplishments of this organization. I don’t know if it meets my expectations because I’m new." (C6790S124_16)
  • Unmet Expectations:
    • "These are not meaningful statistics." (C6790S124_23)
    • "I would like to see statistics organized in a better format." (C6790S124_47)
  • Positive Engagement:
    • "It was great interacting, engaging, personable." (C6790S124_34)
  • Desire for Community-Level Impact:
    • "I thought this page would be for all of the donors for a particular project and the impact we all have because we are all in this together." (C6790S124_30)

Expectations When Clicking on Events in the Timeline 

Question: What do you expect to happen when you click on events in the timeline?

Theme

Feedback

Actionable Insights for Designers

Desire for Detailed Information

Most participants expect to see more detailed information about events, including specifics like dates, metrics, or outcomes.

Provide a drill-down feature that reveals detailed event information, such as names, dates, descriptions, and outcomes (e.g., impact stories).

Expectations for Storytelling

A few participants wanted in-depth narratives or articles about the events to explain their significance.

 Include engaging narratives or summaries that highlight the story behind each event, emphasizing the impact and relevance.

Anticipation of Event Context

Participants expected context, such as program details, the beneficiaries reached, and specific activities performed.

 Add contextual descriptions that explain the event’s purpose, beneficiaries, and impact (e.g., "This project provided 500 meals").

Suggestions to Make the Impact Timeline More Useful or Engaging

Theme

Feedback

Actionable Insights for Designers

Label Events Clearly

Users suggested naming projects to better connect the impact to specific initiatives.

 Add project names to timeline events to provide clear attribution and context for accomplishments.

Emphasize Mission Over Metrics

A participant preferred focusing on the mission rather than growth-related data.

Highlight mission-driven outcomes alongside metrics to create a balance between emotional engagement and data.

Visual Enhancements

Suggestions included adding photos, graphics, or celebratory elements to make the timeline more inviting.

 Incorporate visual storytelling, such as beneficiary photos, icons, or celebratory elements (e.g., progress markers).

Clarify Symbols and Metrics

Ambiguity in symbols like “X” caused confusion for users.

Replace unclear symbols (e.g., "X") with intuitive icons like checkmarks or explanatory labels to ensure clarity.

Provide Meaningful Details

Users wanted deeper insights into what the events represent and why they matter.

 Include detailed descriptions for each event to explain its significance and the specific outcomes achieved.
