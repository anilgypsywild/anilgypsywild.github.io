High-Level Summary (For [name omitted] + Exec)

Personas that most strongly drive Conversion Rate (CVR):

  • Prospect Supporter
  • Clarity Seeker
  • Next-Gen Emotional-Authentic

Personas that most strongly drive Recurring Revenue / LTV:

  • Regular Supporter
  • Impact Believer
  • Faith-Aligned Steward
  • High Impact Donor

Personas that most strongly drive Virality (K-factor):

  • Influencer / Advocate
  • College Mobilizer
  • Next-Gen Emotional-Authentic

Personas that most strongly drive Community Platform efficiency:

  • FCP Director
  • FCP Health Worker
  • FCP Accountant
