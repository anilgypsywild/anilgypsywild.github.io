Supporter Beta Research: Test Participant Tasks for [name omitted]

📌 Optimized for Mobile – WhatsApp Voice Notes, Text, & Video

👋 Quick Intro Message (Send This First)

📩 “Hey [name omitted]! Thanks for helping test this. Just go through these quick tasks and share your thoughts however feels easiest—voice note, text, or video. No pressure, just your natural reactions. Appreciate it!”

📌 Task 1: Initial Login & First Impressions

  • 1️⃣ Log in to the Supporter platform (or test environment).
  • 2️⃣ Look around for 30 seconds before clicking anything.
  • 3️⃣ WhatsApp Prompts:
    • 🎤 Voice Note: “Tell me your first impression. What stood out?”
    • 📩 Text: “Did you find what you expected? If not, what were you looking for?”
    • 📹 Video: “Record a quick screen scroll and describe your first thoughts.”

📌 Task 2: Navigation & Feature Discovery

  • 1️⃣ Explore the main menu & browse sections (Home, Impact Reports, Community, etc.).
  • 2️⃣ Find & interact with at least one feature (e.g., view an impact report, comment on a post).
  • 3️⃣ WhatsApp Prompts:
    • 🎤 Voice Note: “How easy was it to navigate? (1-7 scale, 1 = Very Difficult, 7 = Very Easy)”
    • 📩 Text: “What, if anything, felt confusing or unclear?”
    • 📹 Video: “Show me anything that felt unclear or awkward while navigating.”

📌 Task 3: Engagement & Content Interaction

  • 1️⃣ Find and engage with content (impact report, donation update, or community post).
  • 2️⃣ Comment or react to a post if possible.
  • 3️⃣ WhatsApp Prompts:
    • 🎤 Voice Note: “How relevant did the content feel? (1-7 scale)”
    • 📩 Text: “What type of content would you prefer more of? (Reports, Photos, Videos, Data, Interactive)?”
    • 📹 Video: “Show me a post or report that stood out to you and explain why.”

📌 Task 4: Notifications & Alerts

  • 1️⃣ Look for any notifications or updates about donations and engagement.
  • 2️⃣ WhatsApp Prompts:
  • 🎤 Voice Note: “Did you receive any useful alerts? (Yes/No) If yes, which ones?”
  • 📩 Text: “How often would you prefer updates? (Real-time, Weekly, Monthly, Only Major Updates)?”
  • 📹 Video: “Show me a notification you received and share if it was useful or unnecessary.”

📌 Task 5: Drop-Off & Final Feedback

  • 1️⃣ Reflect on your experience after 10-15 minutes of using the platform.
  • 2️⃣ WhatsApp Prompts:
    • 🎤 Voice Note: “Would you return to the platform? Why or why not?”
    • 📩 Text: “What was the biggest friction point in your experience?”
    • 📹 Video: “Record a short summary of what worked well and what frustrated you.”

📌 🔹 Be as honest as possible—if something felt off, let me know!

✅ Wrap-Up Message

📩 “Thanks, [name omitted]! This was super helpful. If anything else comes to mind later, just drop me a quick note. Appreciate it!”
