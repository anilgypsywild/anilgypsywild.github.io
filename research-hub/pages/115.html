🔭 UX 4D View – May 2025

We map validated Community product hypotheses to the four UX pillars defined in the 4D Scorecard: , Doability, Dependability, Delight, and Desire

  1. ⚙️ | Doability | Behavioral UX – Can users do what we designed?
  2. 🛡️ | Dependability | Attitudinal UX – Do users trust and recommend it?
  3. 🌟 | Delight | Emotional UX – How does the experience feel?
  4. 💡 | Desire | Adoption UX – Will they come back? Do they want more?
