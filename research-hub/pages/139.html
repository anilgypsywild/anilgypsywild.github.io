What is Research? (User eXperience Reseach? (UXR, UR, UER))

User (experience) Research or Research, is a scientific practice of inquiry into user behaviors, their needs, and their motivations.

This behavioral research includes analyzing the experience of tasks from a user-centric perspective for completeness, efficiency, and even delight. For more on Usability, see its definition here at ISO standards.

Research triangulates various data sources such as qualitative user interviews, quantitative surveys, analytics from product usage, etc. to enable data-informed decisions for our partners in Product, Design, Engineering, and Marketing.

Research Collaborators

We are co-founders working in close partnership with almost every function.

  • Designers
    • Help with UI decision making by validating decision choices starting with prototypes through high-fidelity implementation
    • Shape and facilitate design sprints by highlighting research needs
  • Product Managers
    • Provide competitor insights with comparative product research
    • Discover unmet user needs for Day-0 products with Jobs-To-Be-Done, Choice-Modeling with ranking such as Max-Diff, KANO, etc
    • Quantiatively prioritize user needs for roadmaps
    • Help define UX and Product success metrics
  • Developers
    • Rank and prioritize product issues severely affecting product usage, users, security, trust, etc
    • Provide user-centric insights to influence early engineering-design choices

What do Researchers do?

Researchers translate user needs and expecations for these three core teams to support product success

  • Immerse ourselves into our customers' lives to build deep empathy for their experiences. (e.g. User Report here )
  • Partner on Day-0 from pre-ideation to post-launch to influence user-centric thinking and goals into roadmaps
  • Uncover potential opportunities to expand the product experience to suit the needs of international customers
  • Run tactical, pathfinding, validation research using a mixture of methodologies to smooth product launch at alpha, beta, and general-audience stages
  • Track product usage, user behavior, longitudinally with surveys and data-analytics to increase user-retention and their engagement with the product by helping shape delightful, efficient experiences
  • Democratize research insights with an easily accessible transparent repository
