Empowering Compassion's Mission

A User-Centric Strategy for Platform Optimization

Date: Dec 23, 2024

Overview

This strategy document leverages user research insights derived from Community team onboarding materials to enhance the functionality, usability, and emotional engagement of Compassion’s platforms. It aligns platform development with Compassion’s mission to release children from poverty in Jesus’ name, focusing on four key personas: Frontline Church Partners (FCPs), Donors/Supporters, Neighbor-to-Neighbor Users, and Global Partners.

Personas and Their Ecosystem Roles

1. Frontline Church Partners (FCPs)

  • Role: Local implementers of Compassion’s programs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Trustworthy community anchors, deeply embedded in local cultures.
    • Overwhelmed by administrative tasks, requiring streamlined tools.
  • Needs:
    • Simplified workflows.
    • Secure, offline-capable reporting tools.
  • Relevant References:
    • “National Office Structure.docx” – Describes FCP operational roles.
    • “FCP onboarding wireframe (March 2024)” – Highlights early-stage solutions.
  • User Story:
    • "As an FCP, I need an intuitive platform that simplifies reporting and aligns with Compassion’s requirements, so I can focus on nurturing the children in my care."

2. Donors/Supporters

  • Role: Financial supporters of Compassion’s programs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Diverse demographics, motivated by emotional connection to impact.
    • Require transparency and tangible proof of their contributions.
  • Needs:
    • Real-time impact visualization.
    • Trust-building narratives and transparency.
  • Relevant References:
    • “Supporter App Prototype (October 2023)” – Demonstrates early engagement tools.
    • “Compassion Moments PRFAQ” – Highlights storytelling efforts.
  • User Story:
    • "As a donor, I want real-time updates on the children or programs I support, along with visual proof of impact, so I feel confident and connected to my contributions."

3. Neighbor-to-Neighbor Users (Compassion Causes)

  • Role: Grassroots community participants addressing localized needs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Familiar with local challenges but varied in technical literacy.
    • Highly motivated to collaborate for impactful change.
  • Needs:
    • Collaborative tools and accessible platforms.
  • Relevant References:
    • “Neighbor Feedback videos (March 2023)” – Highlights usability challenges.
    • “Neighbor Feedback - Global Partners” – Identifies accessibility gaps.
  • User Story:
    • "As a community member, I want a simple platform to coordinate resources and track progress so I can contribute effectively to meaningful causes."

4. Global Partners (NGOs and Supporting Churches)

  • Role: Regional or international collaborators facilitating Compassion’s programs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Responsible for strategic implementation and monitoring.
    • Require scalable and culturally sensitive solutions.
  • Needs:
    • Real-time monitoring tools.
    • AI-driven localization and translation capabilities.
  • Relevant References:
    • “Translation Screenshots” – Highlights localization needs.
    • “Community Platform demo (July 2024)” – Demonstrates monitoring features.
  • User Story:
    • "As a global partner, I need scalable tools with robust monitoring and localization features so I can ensure program success across diverse contexts."

Pain Points and Opportunities

Pain Points

  1. Fragmented Reporting Systems:
    • Highlighted in “Epics and Features for Community app for FCPs (GitLab).”
    • Causes delays and inefficiencies for FCPs.
  2. Emotional Disconnect for Donors:
    • “Compassion Messenger Test Closeout Summary (March 2023)” reveals gaps in maintaining donor engagement.
  3. Accessibility Challenges:
    • “Neighbor Feedback videos (March 2023)” underscore the need for low-tech solutions.
  4. Cultural Sensitivity for Global Partners:
    • “Content Reservoir PRFAQ v1.0” shows the difficulty of scaling localized content.

Opportunities

  1. AI-Powered Impact Stories:
    • Leverage AI to create personalized donor updates using FCP reports.
  2. Mobile-First Design:
    • Ensure platforms are optimized for low-bandwidth mobile environments.
  3. Gamification for Donors:
    • Introduce achievement milestones (e.g., "You supported 100 children this year!").
  4. Collaborative Tools for Grassroots Users:
    • Simplify resource coordination features for Compassion Causes participants.

Strategic Next Steps

For Frontline Church Partners

  • Action: Enhance “FCP onboarding wireframe (March 2024)” with offline capabilities and contextual tutorials.
  • Rationale: Address workflow inefficiencies and ensure usability in low-connectivity regions.

For Donors/Supporters

  • Action: Refine “Supporter App Prototype (October 2023)” to include real-time impact timelines and personalized stories.
  • Rationale: Build stronger emotional connections by addressing storytelling gaps noted in “Compassion Moments PRFAQ.”

For Neighbor-to-Neighbor Users

  • Action: Simplify navigation within the Neighbor-to-Neighbor Platform and conduct usability testing.
  • Rationale: Align design with insights from “Neighbor Feedback videos (March 2023).”

For Global Partners

  • Action: Expand “Content Reservoir PRFAQ v1.0” with AI-driven localization tools.
  • Rationale: Improve scalability and cultural sensitivity to enhance program execution.

Connecting Strategy to Compassion’s Mission

This strategy directly supports Compassion’s mission by:

  1. Empowering FCPs: Providing tools that reduce administrative burdens and focus on child development.
  2. Engaging Donors: Creating emotionally resonant experiences that deepen trust and loyalty.
  3. Strengthening Communities: Enabling grassroots participants to collaborate effectively.
  4. Supporting Global Partners: Enhancing scalability and cultural sensitivity for global program success.

Conclusion

By addressing the identified pain points and leveraging opportunities with contextually informed actions, Compassion’s platforms can transform into powerful tools for collaboration, transparency, and emotional engagement. These improvements ensure alignment with Compassion’s overarching vision, fostering a unified, impactful community.

Personas and Design Implicaitons

1. Frontline Church Partners (FCPs)

  • Role: Local implementers of Compassion’s child development programs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Highly focused on child development, with limited time for administrative tasks.
    • Operate in regions with varying connectivity and technological literacy.
  • Design Priorities:
    • Simplified workflows for data reporting.
    • Offline-capable tools with clear, intuitive interfaces.
  • Relevant References:
    • “National Office Structure.docx” – Describes FCP responsibilities.
    • “FCP onboarding wireframe (March 2024)” – Provides early-stage design insights.
  • Design Opportunity:
    • "Create dashboards that highlight actionable insights, such as upcoming deadlines or flagged cases, enabling FCPs to prioritize tasks efficiently."

2. Donors/Supporters

  • Role: Individuals or entities funding Compassion’s programs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Diverse demographic profile with varying motivations for giving.
    • Require emotional connection and proof of impact.
  • Design Priorities:
    • Immersive, visually engaging impact narratives.
    • Easy access to program updates and personalized donor journeys.
  • Relevant References:
    • “Supporter App Prototype (October 2023)” – Demonstrates donor engagement tools.
    • “Compassion Moments PRFAQ” – Highlights storytelling efforts.
  • Design Opportunity:
    • "Incorporate dynamic visualizations and interactive timelines that allow donors to explore their impact stories."

3. Neighbor-to-Neighbor Users (Compassion Causes)

  • Role: Grassroots participants addressing localized community needs.
  • Characteristics:
    • Varied technical literacy levels.
    • Operate in resource-constrained environments.
  • Design Priorities:
    • Accessible, mobile-first platforms.
    • Simplified navigation and collaboration tools.
  • Relevant References:
    • “Neighbor Feedback videos (March 2023)” – Highlights usability barriers.
    • “Neighbor Feedback - Global Partners” – Identifies accessibility challenges.
  • Design Opportunity:
    • "Develop low-bandwidth-friendly interfaces with guided tutorials to support first-time users."

4. Global Partners (NGOs and Supporting Churches)

  • Role: Strategic collaborators overseeing regional or international program implementation.
  • Characteristics:
    • Require scalable, culturally adaptable solutions.
    • Manage program compliance and monitoring.
  • Design Priorities:
    • Tools that support real-time monitoring and localization.
    • Scalable features for global deployment.
  • Relevant References:
    • “Community Platform demo (July 2024)” – Demonstrates early monitoring features.
    • “Translation Screenshots” – Highlights localization needs.
  • Design Opportunity:
    • "Integrate AI-powered localization and intuitive dashboards for program monitoring."

Key Design Challenges and Opportunities

Challenges

  1. Fragmented User Journeys:
    • Highlighted in “Epics and Features for Community app for FCPs (GitLab)”—users experience inconsistent navigation and workflows.
  2. Emotional Disconnect for Donors:
    • “Compassion Messenger Test Closeout Summary (March 2023)”—gaps in storytelling reduce donor engagement.
  3. Accessibility Barriers:
    • “Neighbor Feedback videos (March 2023)”—interfaces need to account for diverse literacy and connectivity levels.

Opportunities

  1. Unified Design System:
    • Develop a consistent, scalable design system to unify user experiences across platforms.
  2. Storytelling Innovations:
    • Use interactive media (e.g., videos, maps, and timelines) to create immersive narratives for donors.
  3. Usability Testing Frameworks:
    • Leverage insights from “Design Sprint Interviews” to iteratively improve interfaces.

Strategic Design Initiatives

1. Enhance FCP Tools

  • Action: Redesign the “FCP onboarding wireframe” to focus on offline capabilities and actionable insights.
  • Execution:
    • Conduct field usability tests in regions with poor connectivity.
    • Prioritize simple data entry workflows.
  • Outcome: Reduced administrative burden and improved reporting efficiency.

2. Create Donor-Centric Experiences

  • Action: Build upon the “Supporter App Prototype” by adding real-time impact visualizations and donor milestones.
  • Execution:
    • Integrate feedback loops to gather donor preferences.
    • Use AI to generate personalized impact stories.
  • Outcome: Increased donor engagement and retention.

3. Optimize Neighbor-to-Neighbor Platforms

  • Action: Simplify navigation and implement guided tutorials.
  • Execution:
    • Conduct usability testing with first-time users.
    • Design for low-bandwidth environments.
  • Outcome: Improved accessibility and user satisfaction.

4. Scale for Global Partners

  • Action: Expand “Community Platform” localization tools with AI-powered translation.
  • Execution:
    • Collaborate with global partners to refine localization requirements.
    • Develop modular, scalable design components.
  • Outcome: Enhanced scalability and cultural adaptability.

Design Metrics and Evaluation

  1. For FCP Tools:
    • Measure reduction in reporting time.
    • Track adoption rates in offline environments.
  2. For Donor Engagement:
    • Monitor donor retention and satisfaction scores.
    • Assess engagement with interactive narratives.
  3. For Neighbor-to-Neighbor Platforms:
    • Evaluate ease of use through task completion rates.
    • Gather feedback on accessibility improvements.
  4. For Global Partners:
    • Analyze effectiveness of localization tools.
    • Measure program compliance rates across regions.
