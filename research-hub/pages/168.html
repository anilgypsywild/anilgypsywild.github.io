🚧 User Journey: Adoption & Engagement

This structured document enables actionable, data-driven decision-making based on user needs and Suporter platform performance.

User Story:

As a High-Impact Donor (HID), I want to regularly engage with the Supporter Platform so that I can stay informed and feel connected to the causes I support.

Hypotheses to Verify:

  • If the platform provides relevant and timely updates, then supporters will engage more frequently (views, reactions, comments, shares).
  • If notifications are personalized and meaningful, then the click-through rate will increase.
  • If supporters understand the impact of their donations, they will be more likely to return to the platform.

Testing Methods:

  • Survey Questions: How often do you log into the platform? What motivates you to engage?
  • UserTesting Task: Track behavior in an unmoderated session with content interaction prompts.
  • Analytics Tracking: Views as a percentage of posts, reaction rates, and notification CTR.

User Journey: Time-to-Value (TTV) & Content Effectiveness

User Story:

As a Supporter, I want to quickly find valuable content so that I can engage with the platform without unnecessary effort.

Hypotheses to Verify:

  • If content is easily accessible and well-categorized, then Supporters will engage faster.
  • If supporters receive curated recommendations, their Time-to-Value (TTV) will decrease.
  • If content matches Supporters’ expectations and interests, satisfaction and retention will improve.

Testing Methods:

  • Survey Questions: How quickly do you find relevant content? How satisfied are you with the recommendations?
  • UserTesting Task: First-time user flow to determine where drop-offs occur.
  • Analytics Tracking: Time taken from login to first engagement action.

User Journey: CSAT & Trust in Platform

User Story:

As a Supporter, I want to feel confident that my donations are making an impact so that I can continue contributing without hesitation.

Hypotheses to Verify:

  • If supporters receive clear and authentic impact stories, they will report higher CSAT scores.
  • If supporters can see real-time updates on their contributions, trust will increase.
  • If supporters believe their donations are used wisely, their long-term retention will improve.

Testing Methods:

  • Survey Questions: Do you feel your contributions are making an impact? How satisfied are you with the transparency of fund usage?
  • User Interview Questions: What makes an impact story compelling to you? What additional details would improve your trust?
  • Analytics Tracking: CSAT correlation with engagement behaviors.

User Journey: Content Engagement & Satisfaction

User Story:

As a Supporter, I want to engage with meaningful stories so that I feel emotionally connected to the people I am helping.

Hypotheses to Verify:

  • If content includes personal success stories and milestones, then engagement will increase.
  • If content includes multimedia formats (videos, images, testimonials), then Supporters will find it more compelling.
  • If translations and communication quality are high, international Supporters will feel more engaged.

Testing Methods:

  • Survey Questions: How engaging do you find the content? What type of content resonates most with you?
  • UserTesting Task: Compare engagement levels between different content formats.
  • Analytics Tracking: Reactions per post, video completion rates, content shares.

Next Steps

  • Convert these hypotheses into research tasks for usability testing, surveys, and analytics tracking.
  • Prioritize hypotheses based on impact on user experience and business goals.
  • Align testing roadmap with Supporter Platform milestones for continuous learning.
