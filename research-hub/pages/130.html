CVG User Stories for User Research – Workshop Brief

  • Date: Second Week of March
  • Duration: 45 minutes
  • Objective: Aligning on how user stories contribute to user research, usability testing, and product validation within the Supporter Platform and Causes App.

1. Workshop Overview

User stories are often seen as a product team tool, but they play a critical role in user research as well. This workshop focuses on how user stories can be structured and utilized to drive research objectives, usability testing, and data-driven decision-making.

The goal is to ensure that PMs, researchers, and designers align on a structured approach that integrates user stories into the research process to improve baseline metrics, usability scorecards, and overall product insights.

2. Workshop Agenda

Introduction (5 min)

• Purpose of the workshop

• The role of user stories beyond product development

• How they support research, usability testing, and validation

User Stories in the Context of User Research (10 min)

• Using user stories to define research baselines and usability testing frameworks

• How they help in content comprehension, engagement, and donor impact validation for CVG products

• Example use cases from Supporter Platform & Causes App

Open Discussion & Alignment (20 min)

• Identifying gaps in current user stories

• Addressing misalignment between product and research teams

• How PMs, designers, and researchers can collaborate effectively

Next Steps & Takeaways (10 min)

• Action items for refining user stories for research

• Structured ownership: Who writes, updates, and validates user stories?

• Defining next steps for integration into upcoming MVP testing & beta research

3. Key Content & Discussion Points (20 min Open-Ended Format)

Why User Stories Matter in Research

• They guide usability test scenarios, benchmark measurements, and qualitative insights

• A well-defined user story ensures research validates actual user needs rather than assumptions

• User stories help researchers communicate test goals effectively to stakeholders

Applying User Stories to Supporter Platform & Causes App

• How can we use them to test donor engagement, content clarity, and impact perception?

• Ensuring user stories align with actual user behaviors and not just product assumptions

• Real examples: Testing comprehension and engagement in the Supporter Platform

Research vs. Product Ownership: Defining Roles

• Why PMs need to provide research-informed user stories

• The role of research in refining and improving user stories

• Setting expectations: User research ≠ Product user stories

Collaboration & Structuring the Process

• Who defines the initial user stories?

• How does research validate or refine them?

• When do user stories transition from product focus to research focus?

Immediate Actions

1. Schedule follow-ups for aligning product and research-driven user stories.

3. Define a tracking mechanism for integrating user stories into ongoing research.

For Research & PMs Moving Forward

• PMs should define initial user stories based on personas and product needs.

• Research will validate and refine user stories through testing and data-driven insights.

• A clear handoff process should be established between PMs → Research → Testing.

5. Timeline & Execution Plan

Week 1 of March:

• Prepare user stories for research-driven testing in Supporter & Causes projects.

• Define content-related research questions based on personas and impact metrics.

Week 2 of March (Workshop Date TBD):

• Conduct CVG User Stories for User Research workshop.

• Align PMs, designers, and researchers on user story integration in research.

Post-Workshop:

• Track implementation of research-driven user stories.

• Establish a repeatable process for incorporating user stories into future usability studies.

6. Conclusion

This workshop is meant to close the gap between product and research by ensuring user stories are not just written, but actually inform usability testing, baseline research, and scorecard development.

By structuring how user stories support CVG Supporter Platform and CVG Causes App, we ensure that research is driven by real user behaviors and needs, rather than internal product assumptions.
