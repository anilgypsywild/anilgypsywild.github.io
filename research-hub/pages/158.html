❓ Research Questions

Usertesting.com test plan

❓ Research Questions

Problem

The current content review process doesn’t work well for sponsors because they lack direct contacts—such as Relationship or Content Review Managers—to reach out to when their comments are rejected. (link to full epic)

This research aims to improve the sponsor experience when their comment is rejected by testing a clearer, more actionable content review flow. Previous research focused on app notifications, not the content review experience, and didn’t test this new design. This study reuses elements from that test—like recruiting criteria and questions about content moderation trust & clarity—for consistency and to allow comparison with baseline metrics.

Goals

This study will test a new sponsor content review workflow to:

  1. Evaluate the clarity and emotional impact of the "Your comment wasn’t posted" landing page.
  2. Assess whether sponsors understand why their comment was rejected after reading the landing page content.
  3. Test if sponsors can find and understand the Community Guidelines from the landing page.
  4. Explore where and when sponsors expect to see Community Guidelines (e.g., during onboarding, after rejection, or elsewhere).
  5. Identify what sponsors want to do next after a comment is rejected and what features would support that.
  6. Understand how a rejected comment affects sponsors’ motivation to engage again.

Research Method

We will conduct unmoderated remote usability tests using usertesting.com, where participants will interact with a lo-fi prototype of the updated content review experience.

✅ What we’re testing:

Participants will go through three screens (link to prototype):

  1. Lock screen notification about their comment being rejected.
  2. Landing page: “Your comment wasn’t posted,” including a brief explanation and a link to Community Guidelines.
  3. Community Guidelines page, accessed via the landing page.

Participants (Users)

🎥 Persona

  • We will reuse recruiting criteria from the Hifi UX evaluation test as the persona is the same (sponsors) and this will allow us to more effectively compare the results of this test to the baseline.
  • Recruited faith-driven donors and supporters based on screener criteria.
  • Participants segmented by giving frequency and technology familiarity.
  • Total: 5 participants

Screener criteria

  1. Have you ever donated to a charitable organization or nonprofit before?
    1. Yes
    2. No (disqualify)
  2. How important is faith or spirituality in your life?
    1. Very important
    2. Somewhat important
    3. Not very important
    4. Not important (disqualify)
  3. Which of the following best describes your faith tradition?
    1. Christianity
    2. Islam (disqualify)
    3. Judaism (disqualify)
    4. Hinduism (disqualify)
    5. Buddhism (disqualify)
  4. What is your gender identity?
    1. Male
    2. Female
    3. Prefer not to say (disqualify)
  5. How comfortable are you with using apps or technology to engage with charitable causes?
    1. Very comfortable
    2. Somewhat comfortable
    3. Neutral (disqualify)
    4. Somewhat uncomfortable (disqualify)
    5. Not comfortable (disqualify)

🚧 Tasks

Welcome page

Hi!

Thanks for your interest in this study. Before we get started, let’s review some key points and tips.

1. Make sure you’re in a quiet environment.
2. If required for the test, make sure your audio is turned on to record your comments.
3. We will ask you to verbally express your thoughts, to think out loud, while you are experiencing a website or an interactive prototype.

There are no wrong answers- we want your honest and thoughtful feedback. It’s what helps us improve, so be loud and proud!

Tip: Prototypes are concepts that are not live websites yet. Some links and areas may not be fully functional, but do not dwell too much on these.

This study should take about 10-15 minutes.

Ready? Click "Agree and Continue" to start!

Task 1: Open the Notification

Purpose: See your first reaction to the notification.
Instruction: You just received a notification from the Compassion Community app. This app helps donors connect with the churches they support. Please tap the notification to open it. As you do, say what you’re thinking out loud.
When you’re done reviewing the notification, click “Next” to continue.

Follow-up Questions:

  • What’s your first impression of the notification?
  • How clear was the message at first glance?
  • How did this notification make you feel about your comment and the app?

Task 2: Review the Landing Page (“Your comment wasn’t posted”)

Purpose: Understand clarity of the rejection message, user sentiment, and expected next steps.
Instruction: Review the rejection message, explanation, and next steps. Then, answer the following questions based on your experience.
When you’re ready, click “Next” to continue.

Follow-up questions:

  1. How easy or difficult was it to complete this task?
    1 = Very Difficult
    2 = Difficult
    3 = Neutral
    4 = Easy
    5 = Very Easy
  2. How clear was the reason for your comment not being posted?
    • Very clear, I understand why it was rejected.
    • Somewhat clear, but I have some questions.
    • Neutral, I don’t have a strong opinion.
    • Somewhat unclear, I need more explanation.
    • Very unclear, I don’t understand why my comment was rejected.
  3. Do you agree with the reason given for the rejection?
    • Yes, I fully agree.
    • Somewhat agree, but I think more context would help.
    • Neutral, I don’t feel strongly either way.
    • Somewhat disagree, I think my comment was fine.
    • Strongly disagree, I think my comment should not have been rejected.
  4. How would you expect to appeal or get more clarification about your rejected comment?
    • There should be an option to request a review.
    • I should be able to contact support for clarification.
    • The rejection should include more details on what exactly triggered it.
    • I don’t think an appeal process is necessary.
    • Other: _______
  5. What action would you take next after seeing this message? (Select all that apply)
    • Edit and repost my comment to follow the guidelines.
    • Abandon the comment and move on.
    • Contact support for more clarification.
    • Avoid posting similar comments in the future.
    • Other: _______
  6. How does this rejection message affect your willingness to continue engaging in the community?
    • No impact, I will continue engaging as before.
    • Slightly discouraged, but I will keep participating.
    • Neutral, I don’t feel strongly about it.
    • Somewhat discouraged, I might hesitate to post again.
    • Very discouraged, I may stop engaging in the community.
    • Other: _______
  7. What improvements would make this experience better? (Select all that apply)
    • A clearer explanation for rejection.
    • An option to appeal or request clarification.
    • More specific examples of acceptable comments.
    • A way to edit and resubmit directly from this screen.
    • Nothing, this process works fine as is.
    • Other: _______

Task 3: View the Community Guidelines

  • Purpose: Assess how easily users find and understand the community guidelines, and whether the content helps clarify posting expectations.
  • Instruction: Please find the community guidelines from this page and take a moment to read them. Share your thoughts as you explore. When you’re finished, click “Next” to continue.
  • Follow up questions
    • Were you able to find the community guidelines? If yes, where?
    • Was this where you expected to find them?
    • How clear and easy to understand are the guidelines?
    • Did they help clarify why comments are rejected?
    • Would you prefer to see these guidelines earlier (like during onboarding) or only after a comment is rejected? Why?

Task 4: Decide your next step

Purpose: Understand what users want to do next and how confident they feel after reading the rejection and guidelines.
Instruction: Based on what you’ve seen so far, explain what you would do next and explain why.
When you’re done, click “Next” to move on.

Follow-up questions:

  • What helped you decide what to do next?
  • What would make you feel more confident about posting comments in the future?

Post task questionnaire

  1. What would make this experience better for you?
  2. How does this experience affect your motivation to keep participating in the community?
