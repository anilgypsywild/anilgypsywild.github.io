Research article

We will categorize all data gathered during observational usability studies into the following four categories.

Categories

Acronym

Category

Description

Planned Next Steps
SS

Show Stopper

 Blocking issue causing task failure; affected CSAT score Change commitment: Tag key neighbors to resolve this issue
IS

Issue

 Caused multiple users to struggle and reduced CSAT or SUS scores Plan for change: Tag key neighbors to come up with a resolution plan
HL

Highlight

 High-quality experience appreciated by users; should be retained Celebrate: Highlight and preserve this for future experiennces
OB

Observation

 A datapoint, Does not immediately affect the UX but needs further investigation Investigate: Include this follow-up in subsequent research
