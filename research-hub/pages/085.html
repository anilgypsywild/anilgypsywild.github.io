Persona → UX Risk Mapping Table

Which UX failures or design issues disproportionately harm each persona?

GitLab-compatible, ASCII-only.

TABLE: PERSONA → UX RISK MAPPING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Persona                     | UX Risks That Disproportionately Harm Them
                           | Why It Matters / Behavioral Evidence
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.1 Prospect Supporter    | - Any checkout friction
                           | - Missing transparency details
                           | - Poor mobile optimization
                           | - Hidden fees or unclear gift routing
                           | Prospect motivation = Clarity + Simplicity.
                           | Deep Contrast shows urgency and faith framing
                           | too early lowers conversion.
                           | Evidence: :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.2 Regular Supporter     | - Infrequent updates
                           | - Generic thank-you messages
                           | - Low specificity in impact
                           | - Confusing recurring settings
                           | They need continuity to reinforce trust.
                           | Poor reporting causes “reversion to Prospect.”
                           | Evidence: :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.3 High Impact Donor     | - Vague multi-year reporting
(HID)                      | - Lack of financial governance clarity
                           | - Disorganized PDF reports
                           | - Inconsistent key metrics
                           | HIDs require governance-level clarity.
                           | Small UX errors = massive perceived risk.
                           | Evidence: HID brief + MaxDiff motivation
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.4 Influencer/Advocate   | - Slow loading share screens
                           | - Image upload issues
                           | - Poor mobile layouts
                           | - No preview of share content
                           | They run on speed, momentum, social feedback.
                           | Any friction reduces virality.
                           | Evidence: MaxDiff (Next-Gen urgency)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.5 College Mobilizer     | - Non-mobile flows
                           | - No team views or leaderboards
                           | - Delayed progress updates
                           | - Unclear goal setting
                           | Social and time-bound nature requires
                           | real-time clarity + low lift.
                           | Evidence: MaxDiff Next-Gen motivations
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.6 Supporting Church     | - Complex group workflows
                           | - Ambiguous communication
                           | - No high-level dashboards
                           | - Multi-user conflict
                           | Needs hybrid: pastors want high-level,
                           | youth want emotional + urgency.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.7 Social Circle         | - Slow fundraiser creation
Fundraiser                  | - Confusing share links
                           | - “Cold start” with no templates
                           | - Low visibility progress bar
                           | They depend on social trust, not scale.
                           | Template absence = abandoned campaigns.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.2.1 Clarity Seeker        | - Dense text blocks
                           | - Vague descriptions
                           | - Emotional overload too early
                           | - Missing proof of use of funds
                           | Prospect conversion collapses under
                           | ambiguity. Transparency score = 100.
                           | Evidence: :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.2.2 Impact Believer       | - Weak or infrequent stories
                           | - No continuity thread
                           | - Low personalization
                           | They need “your gift → this impact.”
                           | No personal connection = flatline giving.
                           | Evidence: :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.2.3 Emotional-Authentic   | - Institutional tone
                           | - Text-heavy content
                           | - Slow videos
                           | - Low-emotion visuals
                           | They read emotion before cognition.
                           | Emotion first, clarity second.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.2.4 Faith-Aligned Steward | - Unclear spiritual framing
(Current-Gen)              | - Urgency overemphasis
                           | - Missing stewardship language
                           | They interpret trust through spiritual,
                           | long-term stewardship lenses.
                           | Evidence: :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.3.1 FCP Director          | - Redundant tasks
                           | - Errors with no explanation
                           | - Missing confirmations
                           | - No audit trail
                           | They manage large coordination loads.
                           | Clarity = survival.
                           | Evidence: :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.3.2 FCP Health Worker     | - Photo upload failures
                           | - No offline support
                           | - Confusing instructions
                           | - Low resilience to poor networks
                           | Their workflows are mobile, field-based,
                           | and time-sensitive.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.3.3 FCP Educator          | - Long free-text fields
                           | - No writing structure
                           | - Unclear photo criteria
                           | Low time, high demand = needs templating.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.3.4 FCP Accountant        | - Mislabelled uploads
                           | - No reconciliation view
                           | - Complex navigation
                           | Accuracy = identity. Bad UX = audit risk.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.3.5 Pastor / Church       | - Overly technical content
Leader                      | - Missing high-level snapshots
                           | - Hard-to-find spiritual context
                           | They need “child impact” at a glance.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.4.1 Next-Gen Overlay      | - Slow mobile loading
                           | - Non-visual flows
                           | - Lack of emotional payoff
                           | High urgency + emotion = low patience.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.4.2 Current-Gen Overlay   | - Overly youthful tone
                           | - Missing stewardship signals
                           | - Too much urgency
                           | They abandon quickly when tone mismatches.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

💵 Executive Summary

Personas with the highest UX risk sensitivity:

  • Prospect (A.1.1) — any clarity failure kills conversion
  • Influencer (A.1.4) — any friction kills virality
  • FCP Health Worker (A.3.2) — any image upload issue blocks field reporting
  • HID (A.1.3) — any governance ambiguity damages trust

Personas with the broadest cross-platform impact:

  • Clarity Seeker (A.2.1) — top funnel
  • Impact Believer (A.2.2) — retention
  • Emotional-Authentic (A.2.3) — N2N shareability
  • FCP Director (A.3.1) — Community adoption
