Causes Baseline UX Research Brief

Understanding Donor Intent Formation and Pre Checkout Hesitation

Product: Compassion Causes Web

Related Analytics Artifact
Causes Donation Funnel Analysis (GA4)
April 1, 2025 – January 15, 2026

1. Background and Context

  • Compassion Causes serves donors primarily through organic, social, and community driven acquisition channels. Recent GA4 funnel analysis across a nine month period establishes a clear behavioral baseline for the platform’s core donation workflow.
  • The analytics show that while Causes successfully attracts and engages users, the majority of visitors disengage before forming donation intent, and a secondary but meaningful drop occurs before checkout, particularly on desktop.

This research brief is designed to investigate why these behaviors occur, building directly on the GA baseline rather than assumptions or anecdotal feedback.

2. Analytics Driven Problem Statement

GA4 funnel analysis reveals:

  • Only 7.33 percent of users progress from page view to Donation Intent
  • Over 92 percent of users disengage before intent formation
  • Checkout completion is relatively strong once users commit
  • Mobile users are significantly more decisive than desktop users

These patterns indicate that the primary barrier to donation is not transactional usability, but pre commitment hesitation related to meaning, trust, clarity, or emotional resonance.

Without understanding the drivers of this hesitation, UX and product improvements risk optimizing the wrong layer of the experience.

3. Why This Research Now

The GA baseline represents:

  • Stable behavior across nine months
  • Real production traffic and donors
  • Clear, repeatable drop off patterns

This creates a timely opportunity to shift from observing what happens to understanding why it happens, ensuring that future UX changes meaningfully address the highest leverage problem in the funnel.

4. Research Objectives

This study aims to:

  1. Understand why users do not progress from viewing causes to expressing donation intent
  2. Identify trust, clarity, or emotional gaps in the cause discovery and detail experience
  3. Explore why desktop users hesitate more than mobile users after intent is formed
  4. Identify experience patterns that correlate with higher intent formation
  5. Provide actionable insights to inform future UX prioritization and experimentation

5. Primary Research Questions

Grounded in the GA baseline, this research seeks to answer:

  1. What questions or doubts do users have before deciding to donate?
  2. What information or reassurance are users seeking but not finding?
  3. How do users interpret urgency, impact, and transparency signals?
  4. What differentiates users who form donation intent from those who disengage?
  5. Why does device context influence decisiveness and follow through?

6. Research Scope and Focus

This study will focus on pre checkout experiences, including:

  • Cause discovery and navigation
  • Cause detail comprehension
  • Language and framing of impact and urgency
  • Trust and legitimacy cues
  • Decision making moments prior to donation intent

Checkout mechanics will be examined only insofar as they relate to hesitation after intent, not as a primary usability problem.

7. Methodology

Primary Method

Usability Testing with Think Aloud

  • Moderated or unmoderated sessions
  • Desktop and mobile participants
  • Tasks focused on:
    • Exploring causes
    • Interpreting impact and urgency
    • Deciding whether to donate

Supplementary Methods

  1. Post task surveys (confidence, trust, clarity)
  2. Behavioral tagging of hesitation moments
  3. Comparative analysis of intent forming vs non intent forming users

8. Target Participants

  1. First time or low frequency donors
  2. Mix of mobile and desktop users
  3. Users arriving through direct and social channels
  4. Faith aligned but not necessarily existing Compassion donors

9. Key Metrics and Signals

This research will connect qualitative findings to baseline analytics by examining:

  1. Intent formation likelihood
  2. Perceived clarity of cause and impact
  3. Trust and confidence ratings
  4. Emotional resonance indicators
  5. Self reported reasons for hesitation or abandonment

10. Expected Outcomes

This study will deliver:

  • Clear explanations for pre intent abandonment observed in GA
  • A map of trust and clarity gaps in the current experience
  • Evidence based recommendations for improving intent formation
  • A foundation for future A B testing and experimentation
  • Qualitative context to interpret ongoing funnel metrics

11. Relationship to Analytics Baseline

This research explicitly builds on the documented GA funnel baseline:

  • Analytics identified where users disengage
  • Research will uncover why they disengage
  • Future experiments will test how to improve outcomes

This ensures a disciplined, evidence first approach to UX improvement.

12. Deliverables

  • Research summary with key findings
  • Annotated user journey highlighting hesitation points
  • Prioritized UX recommendations tied to funnel stages
  • Clear linkage between research insights and GA benchmarks

13. Next Steps

Upon approval:

  1. Finalize study design and tasks
  2. Recruit participants
  3. Conduct research sessions
  4. Synthesize findings
  5. Share results with product, design, and leadership

Summary

This research brief represents the natural next step after establishing a behavioral baseline. By grounding UX research in observed donor behavior, Compassion Causes can steward donor trust responsibly and focus improvement efforts where they matter most.
