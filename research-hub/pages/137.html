🗺️ Defining Research Strategy

Objectives: focus on understanding user needs, validating assumptions, and ensuring that the app is user-friendly and effectively meets its mission. 

This strategy plan creates a user-centered peer-to-peer donation app that effectively serves its mission. By understanding the needs and motivations of both donors and beneficiaries, we can design an app that fosters engagement, trust, and ultimately helps release children from poverty in Jesus' name.

1. Define Research Objectives

  • Understand User Needs: Identify the motivations and pain points of potential donors and beneficiaries.
  • Evaluate App Usability: Assess how easily users can navigate and interact with the app.
  • Validate Mission Alignment: Ensure that the app's features and messaging resonate with the target audience’s values and beliefs.

2. Identify Target Users

  • Donors: Individuals motivated to contribute, including families, church members, and community organizations.
  • Beneficiaries: Children and families in need of assistance, and potentially their guardians.
  • Stakeholders: Non-profit organizations, church groups, and volunteers involved in child poverty alleviation.

3. Select Research Methods

  • Surveys: Distribute surveys to gather quantitative data on user demographics, motivations, and preferences.
  • Interviews: Conduct one-on-one interviews with potential users to dive deeper into their experiences, beliefs, and needs.
  • Focus Groups: Organize sessions with diverse user groups to discuss the app's concept, features, and messaging.
  • Usability Testing: Create prototypes of the app and observe users as they complete specific tasks to identify usability issues.
  • Field Studies: Observe users in real-world settings to understand their behaviors and interactions related to charitable giving.

4. Develop Research Instruments

  • Survey Questionnaires: Create surveys with a mix of multiple-choice, rating scale, and open-ended questions.
  • Interview Guides: Prepare structured guides for interviews, focusing on key topics such as motivations for donating and barriers to using the app.
  • Usability Testing Tasks: Define specific tasks for users to complete in the prototype (e.g., making a donation, finding a child to support).

5. Recruit Participants

  • Identify and Reach Out: Use social media, community organizations, and churches to recruit participants that fit your target user profile.
  • Incentivize Participation: Offer incentives (e.g., small donations to charity, gift cards) to encourage participation.

6. Conduct Research

  • Gather Data: Execute surveys, interviews, focus groups, and usability tests.
  • Record Observations: Take detailed notes and, if possible, record sessions (with consent) for later analysis.

7. Analyze Data

  • Quantitative Analysis: Use statistical methods to analyze survey results, identifying trends and common themes.
  • Qualitative Analysis: Thematically analyze interview and focus group transcripts to identify key insights and user sentiments.
  • Usability Findings: Summarize findings from usability testing, highlighting common pain points and areas for improvement.

8. Synthesize Findings

  • Create User Personas: Develop detailed profiles of typical users, including their goals, motivations, and challenges.
  • Journey Maps: Visualize the user journey, highlighting key touchpoints and potential barriers.
  • Insights Report: Compile a report summarizing key findings, insights, and recommendations for app design and functionality.

9. Iterate and Design

  • Refine App Features: Use research insights to prioritize app features and design elements that resonate with users.
  • Prototype Development: Develop wireframes and prototypes incorporating user feedback and test again if needed.

10. Validate and Launch

  • Pre-launch Testing: Conduct final usability tests with a beta version of the app to ensure it meets user expectations.
  • Launch Plan: Develop a marketing and outreach plan that aligns with the mission and values of the app, targeting potential donors and beneficiaries.

11. Post-Launch Research

  • Continuous Feedback Loop: Establish mechanisms for ongoing user feedback (e.g., in-app surveys, feedback forms).
  • Monitor Key Metrics: Track user engagement, satisfaction, and donation success rates to measure app effectiveness and impact.
