Causes Conversion Funnel Baseline and Experience Diagnosis

Problem Statement:

  • We have a conversion funnel performance problem in the early Causes journey, characterized by a very high rate of early abandonment.
  • Current signals indicate that approximately 76 percent of users drop off from the Causes home page. This level of abandonment is too high to confidently invest in downstream optimizations without first understanding the underlying experience failures.
  • The intake suggests that this drop off may be driven by a combination of factors including navigation and discoverability challenges, lack of clarity or relevance in information presented, insufficient confidence or trust to proceed, and interaction friction early in the journey.

Without a clear understanding of why users disengage at these early stages, product and design decisions risk addressing symptoms rather than root causes.

Why We Are Solving This Now

  • This work is time sensitive because the product team needs a credible baseline to inform upcoming prioritization and development decisions.
  • The intake specifies a need for baseline insights by the end of Q3, in advance of Q4 development planning. Establishing this baseline now ensures that future design and engineering investments are guided by evidence rather than assumptions.

Without a shared baseline, it will be difficult to determine whether future changes meaningfully improve the user experience or simply shift metrics without reducing abandonment.

What We Know About the Problem Space

We have early directional data but lack an evidence backed explanation of user behavior.

Known signals and context include:

  • There is existing end to end conversion analysis and supporting materials already created and linked in Figma.
  • Analytics data indicates a steep drop off at the very beginning of the journey, particularly from the home page.
  • The intake explicitly calls for understanding not only where users drop off, but why they disengage.
  • There is alignment that both behavioral performance signals and user perception signals are needed to understand the problem space.
  • Preferred data sources mentioned in the intake include Google Analytics and Quantum Metrics, indicating existing instrumentation that can support baseline understanding.

At this stage, what is missing is a unified narrative that connects behavioral patterns with user understanding, confidence, and decision making.

What We Will Do With the Results

The results of this research will be used to support both near term product decisions and longer term measurement governance.

Product and Design Decisions

  • Identify the highest impact breakpoints in the early Causes journey.
  • Understand the primary drivers of early abandonment, including navigation, content clarity, and confidence related factors.
  • Translate validated drivers into concrete UX requirements that inform design and engineering prioritization for the next development cycle.

Measurement and Governance

  • Establish a baseline that can be re measured after changes are shipped.
  • Create a CVG 4D UX Scorecard baseline for the Causes journey that leadership can reference over time.
  • Enable consistent comparison across future iterations to determine whether experience quality is improving in meaningful ways.

CVG UCD Framework Placement

This project is situated within the Build phase of the CVG User Centered Design framework, with a strong emphasis on post launch measurement.

The work focuses on evaluating an existing experience, identifying experience gaps, and establishing baseline performance against defined UX outcomes.

This aligns with CVG guidance for Build phase activities that include iterative testing, post launch evaluation, and KPI tracking tied to real user behavior.

Research Questions

These questions are intentionally method agnostic and structured to support scorecard ready outcomes.

  1. Journey and Drop Off
    1. Where in the visit to conversion journey do users disengage?
    2. What patterns of abandonment are most common across user groups?
  2. Navigation and Findability
    1. Can users efficiently locate causes and critical information needed to continue?
    2. qWhich navigation or content elements introduce confusion or unnecessary detours?
  3. Decision Clarity and Confidence
    1. What information do users need at the list and detail stages to feel confident proceeding?
    2. Which aspects of language, structure, or presentation reduce confidence or trust?
  4. Baseline and Success Criteria
    1. What are the current baseline values for key UX and behavioral metrics so that meaningful change can be detected over time?

Appendix A: CVG 4D UX Scorecard Mapping

This research is structured to directly support creation of a CVG 4D UX Scorecard baseline.

Doability

  • Task success rate
  • Time on task

Click efficiency indicators such as unnecessary navigation or repeated actions

Dependability

  • CSAT
  • UMUX Lite
  • NPS

User confidence ratings related to understanding and trust

Delight

  • Lightweight emotional response signals or qualitative reactions where appropriate

Indicators of frustration or positive surprise

Desire

  • Signals of continued intent, willingness to proceed, or likelihood of return
  • Behavioral indicators of motivation to engage further with Causes

Deliverables

  1. Research brief (this document)
  2. Baseline insights aligned to CVG 4D UX Scorecard dimensions
  3. Clear articulation of experience risks and prioritization guidance for next phase work
  4. Scorecard ready outputs that can be reused for future benchmarking
