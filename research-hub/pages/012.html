🎨 Crazy-8 Workshop Guide

Theme: Designing for Sponsor Motivation & Trust
Source: Prospects Motivations MaxDiff Study (CVG Causes Research 2025)

🧭 Purpose

Generate eight distinct design ideas per designer that visualize how clarity, transparency, and tangible impact can be expressed in the Compassion Causes Prospects journey.

👥 Personas to Anchor

1. The Proof-Seeker (NextGen / First-Time Prospects)
Values clarity, autonomy, transparency.
Wants to see where money goes and control how much to give.

2. The Faithful Steward (CurrentGen / Repeat Prospects)
Values faith alignment, trust, and gratitude.
Seeks reassurance and mission-driven reinforcement after donating.

3. The Pragmatic Planner (Mid-life Individual Giver)
Balances rational stewardship with subtle spirituality.
Needs a simple process and visible local authenticity.

🧩 Key Insights to Translate into Design Prompts

Insight Design Opportunity
Prospects want to clearly understand fund flow (78 % of preference share) Visual impact dashboards, fund-tracking animations, “where your money goes” modules
Tangible proof > emotion (clarity → trust → emotion) Replace generic hero imagery with evidence tiles (“Your $40 = clean water for 2 weeks”)
Trust = prerequisite, not differentiator Auto-generate receipts with transparent line items; integrate third-party verification
Urgency works only when specific and local Location-tagged causes, authentic community faces, countdowns tied to real projects
First-time → Repeat = shift from proof to purpose Two-stage UX: clarity first, faith later; dynamic interface that evolves after first gift
74 % prefer individual autonomy over group giving Personal dashboards, self-directed cause selection
<$50 donors = micro-decisions under scrutiny Simplify choices: one-click 10/20 options + ROI micro-copy
35-54 dominant age = rational-spiritual balance Blend crisp data visuals with faith-based affirmations in thank-you moments

✏️ Workshop Structure (60 minutes)

1️⃣ Set Up (10 min)

  • Moderator introduces purpose: “We’re designing for clarity, transparency, and tangible impact.”
  • Display the three personas.
  • Show one slide summarizing the top 5 motivators from the MaxDiff study.

🗒️ Moderator Note: Emphasize that every sketch must visually prove stewardship before spirituality—clarity before emotion.

2️⃣ Crazy-8 Sketching (8 × 1 min + 2 min setup = 10 min)

Each designer folds a sheet into 8 frames.

For every frame, sketch one fast idea responding to a single prompt.

Example Prompts (per Persona)

For Proof-Seeker (NextGen):

  1. One-screen “Where your money goes” visualization.
  2. Progress bar from donation → delivery.
  3. Toggle to choose impact type (water vs education).
  4. Local photo proof card.
  5. Instant receipt with map.
  6. “Before you give” clarity widget.
  7. Micro-trust badge explaining fund allocation.
  8. Feedback loop survey after first gift.

For Faithful Steward (CurrentGen):

  1. “Faith Impact Timeline” overlaying Scripture verses.
  2. Gratitude story carousel from field partners.
  3. Prayer request integration post-donation.
  4. Personalized thank-you video from pastor or child.
  5. Faith-driven monthly impact newsletter layout.
  6. Re-engagement banner (“Continue your story of giving”).
  7. Church community impact dashboard.
  8. Legacy donation builder for older sponsors.

For Pragmatic Planner (Mid-life):

  1. Budget planner slider showing impact per dollar.
  2. Side-by-side comparison of causes for clarity.
  3. Simplified checkout with real-time impact preview.
  4. Donation breakdown modal with plain-language labels.
  5. Local authenticity card (featuring community worker).
  6. “Your impact this week” digest email mock-up.
  7. Impact ROI calculator.
  8. Cause explorer map by region and theme.

🗒️ Moderator Note: Time each frame exactly one minute. Encourage speed and volume over perfection.

3️⃣ Share & Cluster (15 min)

  • Designers pin sketches to the wall.
  • Group by theme: Transparency, Trust, Faith Connection, Local Authenticity, Autonomy.
  • Identify patterns and innovative outliers.

🗒️ Moderator Note: Ask “Which idea would a first-time sponsor trust immediately?” and “Which idea invites a repeat donor to stay for purpose?”

4️⃣ Prioritize and Next Steps (10 min)

  • Each participant receives 3 dot votes.
  • Mark ideas with highest alignment to the top motivators (clarity, impact, autonomy).
  • Capture the top 3 concepts per persona for prototyping.

🗒️ Moderator Note: Remind the team to link every winning concept to a measurable UX metric (“Perceived Transparency”, “Trust Score”, “Comprehension”).

📊 Deliverables Post-Session

  • Photos of Crazy-8 sheets.
  • Clustered themes board.
  • Top concepts matrix (Persona × Motivator × Concept).
  • Follow-up tasks for lo-fi wireframes or Lyssna A/B testing.

🕊️ Quote

“Prospects don’t give because they feel; they feel after they see clarity. Design the clarity first—emotion and faith will follow.”
