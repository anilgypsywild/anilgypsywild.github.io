Executive Insights

High-priority features shared across multiple personas

The strongest convergence is around:

  • Fast mobile checkout
  • Impact dashboards
  • Before/after visuals
  • Transparent use of funds

High Impact Donors & Current-Gen donors

Strong alignment on:

  • Governance
  • Stewardship
  • Long-form reporting
  • Faith-centered storytelling

Next-Gen personas (Emotional-Authentic, College, Influencer)

Depend heavily on:

  • Visual media
  • Real-time feedback
  • Viral mechanics
  • Fast mobile pathways

FCP personas

Uniquely require:

  • Offline-first
  • Photo compression
  • Bulk workflows
  • Clear financial labeling

These features directly influence Community App adoption, which then drives child update quality for donors.
