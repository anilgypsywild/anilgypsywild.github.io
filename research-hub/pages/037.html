Causes: UsabilityTest, 2024 - Nov

Summary of Research Goals

The primary goal of this research is to assess and improve the usability of the Causes app by evaluating user journeys, identifying key pain points, and establishing a baseline UX scorecard with metrics for task success, customer satisfaction, trust, and emotional appeal. This study will guide actionable improvements to enhance donor engagement and support for causes.

1. Research Overview

1a. Problem Statement

We aim to baseline the core user experience of the Causes app to gather data on:

  • Frequently encountered UX issues or bugs.
  • Common stumbling blocks in locating content.
  • Baseline UX metrics related to task success, customer satisfaction, persuasiveness, emotional appeal, and trust.

1b. Hypotheses

  • H1: The System Usability Scale (SUS) score for the population is hypothesized to be 72.6 or above (Grade B-), with a sample mean of 70.
  • H2: The Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) score will be above 6.1 (on a scale of 1 to 7).

1c. Why Solve This Problem Now?

  • Enhance sponsorship outcomes for the general audience (GA release) on Giving Tuesday.
  • Prepare for a high-fidelity UX evaluation by Support-Ops (Nov 11–17).
  • Create a quantitative UX scorecard to track future iterations.

1d. Existing Research Insights

  • Motivation: Donors are driven by knowing the number of children affected by a cause.
  • Key Categories: Health and Education are the top categories for participants.
  • Urgency: Participants prefer identifying urgent causes with clear reasons.
  • Navigation: Improvements needed for intuitive, visual navigation to causes.

1e. What Will We Do With the Research Insights?

Insights will be categorized into actionable follow-ups:

  • Critical: Blocking issues causing task failure (requires immediate fixes).
  • High: Issues causing multiple user struggles (requires resolution plans).
  • Medium: Positive experiences to celebrate and retain.
  • Low: Data points for further investigation in subsequent studies.

2. Research Goals and Metrics

Goals

  • Validate baseline UX metrics (SUS, CSAT, task success rates).
  • Assess navigation and exploration workflows.
  • Create a UX scorecard with actionable insights.

Metrics

  • By Task:
    • Task Success Rate: Percentage of users completing tasks.
    • Task Time: Average time spent on tasks.
    • Ease of Use: User ratings of effort or difficulty.
    • Open-Ended Feedback: Qualitative insights on usability.
  • Overall:
    • System Usability Scale (SUS): Standardized usability score.
    • Customer Satisfaction (CSAT): Overall user satisfaction rating.
    • Persuasiveness: Measured via Net Promoter Score (NPS).
    • Emotional Appeal: Captured using a smiley scale.
    • Trust: Measured via a trust evaluation scale.

3. Research Methods

Approach

  • Conduct task-based usability tests to evaluate critical user journeys and capture relevant UX metrics for the Causes experience.

Sampling Strategy

  • Participants:
    • Target 39 participants for statistical validity (90% confidence level, 80% power).
    • Include a mix of donors, fundraisers, and support-ops staff.
  • Recruitment Strategy:
    • Leverage existing donor and staff pools for diverse representation.
    • Balance demographics (age, experience level, donation habits).
  • Randomization: Ensure representative sampling across participant groups.

Task Scenarios

  • Task 1: Explore Cause Selection
    • Objective: Evaluate how easily users can find and select a cause to support.
    • Instructions: Navigate to the main screen, browse causes, and select one.
    • Metrics: Task success rate, ease of use.
  • Task 2: Evaluate Cause Details
    • Objective: Assess the clarity and appeal of cause descriptions.
    • Instructions: Review details of a selected cause and decide if it’s donation-worthy.
    • Metrics: Emotional appeal, persuasiveness.
  • Task 3: Proceed to Checkout
    • Objective: Analyze the ease of navigating from cause selection to checkout.
    • Instructions: Complete a mock donation process.
    • Metrics: Task time, trust.
  • Task 4: Evaluate Additional Features
    • Objective: Test social sharing and customization options.
    • Instructions: Explore settings for sharing donations and customizing contributions.
    • Metrics: Open-ended feedback.

Risks and Mitigation

  • Risk: User behavior may not represent real-world scenarios.
  • Mitigation: Conduct follow-up studies with more organic, exploratory tasks.

4. Hypotheses

  • Design Hypothesis: Simplified workflows will reduce errors and improve efficiency.
  • Task-Based Hypothesis: Enhancements to navigation will increase task success rates above 90%.
  • Overall Hypothesis: Visual impact tracking will improve both trust and emotional appeal ratings.
