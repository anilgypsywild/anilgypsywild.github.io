Rapid Iterative Testing and Evaluation (RITE)

  • Is a research methodology to gather user feedback quickly and iteratively
  • Helps evaluate the effectiveness of the process and the usability of a product.
  • Metrics aim to capture
  • Task level
  • Overall-exprience level
  • User-level information (Persona)
  • Metrics are explained in detail here [reference omitted]

CVG RITE UX Metrics

#

 Metric Description Comments
1a

Task: Success Rate
  • Percentage of tasks completed successfully by users
  • Helps identify tasks that users struggle with in the current design and allows you to measure improvements with each iteration
1b

Task: Total Time
  • Average time users take to complete each task
  • Shorter completion times can indicate more intuitive navigation and task flow improvements as the study progresses.
1c

Task: Difficulty
  • Asks paticipants to rate their perceived sense of ease of use of the task. It is measured on a Likert Scale from 1 to 5 (or 7)
  • Provides a subjective measure of user confidence and contentment, capturing early feedback on design effectiveness.
1d

Task: Error Rate
  • Frequency and types of errors users encounter per task.
  • Tracking specific error types (e.g., misclicks, incomplete steps) reveals areas for UI or instructional improvement in subsequent iterations.
1e

Task: Number of User-Assistance Requests
  • Number of times users require assistance or guidance during tasks.
  • Indicates areas where the interface is unclear, helping refine elements that users don’t immediately understand.
2a

Overall: System Usability Scale (SUS)
  • Baseline SUS score for the initial design, repeated periodically.
  • Measures perceived usability over time to gauge user-perceived improvements.
2b

Overall: Completion Rate for Core Workflows
  • Percentage of participants who complete core workflows (end-to-end user journeys).
  • Helps assess overall design usability and functionality at each iteration, particularly for complex, multi-step processes.
2c

Overall: Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
  • Standardized score measured on a Likert Scale from 1 to 7 that tracks customer-satisfaction
2d

Overall: Net Promoter Score (NPS)
  • Industry standardized score that measures a customer's loyalty and adoption
2e

Overall: Emotion Smiley Scale
  • Captures a user's emotional reaction to the evaluated experience.
2f

Overall: Trust Score
  • Trust or Mistrust or Both measured with the Automated Systems Trust Scale
  • Helps to indentify issues of trust or mistrust with different experience touchpoints
2g

Overall: User Comments and Observations

|Record spontaneous feedback, observations, and verbalized frustrations or compliments.

Qualitative feedback offers insights into specific pain points and initial reactions to design elements, guiding focused iterations.
3a

Who: Demographic Data

 Collecting and analyzing data about user demographics can help ensure that insights are representative and applicable to the target audience or target personas

Issue Categorization

  • We will categorize the data observed during a RITE study with this template into the following four categories.

    Category

    Description

    Show Stopper

    		 Blocking issue causing task failure; affected CSAT score

    Issue

    		 Caused multiple users to struggle and reduced CSAT or SUS scores

    Highlight

    		 High-quality experience appreciated by users; should be retained

    Observation

    		 A datapoint, Does not immediately affect the UX but needs further investigation
