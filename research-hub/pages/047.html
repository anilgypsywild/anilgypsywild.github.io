Causes Category Validation

🥙 Study Overview

A closed card sort was conducted in a single day, Feb-3 2026 to evaluate whether Causes items align with the intended outcome-based categories. Follow-up open-ended questions were used to confirm participant understanding and interpret category boundaries.

Please see Lyssna Results in-depth here: [reference omitted]

🪣 Card Sort Results

The overall category structure is sound and aligns with participant mental models.

  1. Safety and Basic Needs show the strongest agreement, with minimal ambiguity.
  2. Training & Skill Building clusters cleanly around applied, practical causes.
  3. Education is well understood as foundational, with some expected adjacency to Training.
  4. Income Generation shows the lowest agreement, with cards frequently dispersed across other categories.

The dispersion in Income Generation reflects how participants conceptualize it as a downstream outcome rather than a primary cause type.

🌻 Qualitative Insights

🎓 Education

Participants describe Education as building early cognitive foundations and supporting long-term development, especially when delivered in safe and supportive environments.

Representative quotes:

  • “It starts the kids off very young, and they can build upon that foundation.”
  • “They develop their thought process at an early age.”

🪓 Training & Skill Building

Training is consistently understood as hands-on, practical learning that gives children usable skills and future agency.

Representative quotes:

  • “They learn skills they can actually use.”
  • “These are skills they can turn into a job or career.”

🪴 Income Generation

Income is viewed as the result of education and skills rather than a direct child-level activity, often framed in terms of family or community benefit.

Representative quotes:

  • “They apply their skills and monetize their education.”
  • “Income comes once they have something to sell.”

⚠️ Safety

Safety is treated as a non-negotiable prerequisite that enables learning, play, and normal childhood development.

Representative quotes:

  • “Before learning and gaining skills, safety comes first.”
  • “No one is putting their children where safety is compromised.”

📚 Basic Needs

Basic Needs are understood as essential for survival, health, and the ability to focus on any other activity or program.

Representative quotes:

  • “Without clean water and food, they suffer.”
  • “These are necessary for the other categories to exist.”

Exec. Summary (tldr;)

  1. The category model reflects a clear mental sequence: foundational needs first, followed by development, skills, and outcomes.
  2. Safety and _Basic Needs fu_nction as trust and legitimacy anchors.
  3. Training & Skill Building supports clarity and applied understanding.
  4. Income Generation is best framed as an outcome rather than a peer entry category.
