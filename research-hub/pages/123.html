Data-Driven Personas Research for CVG Supporter & CVG Causes App

By adopting these data-driven methods, CVG can ensure that its supporter personas are actionable, evolving, and truly representative of its diverse user base.

🏅 Key Insights from ACH CHI Data-Driven Personas

1. Data-Driven Personas vs. Traditional Personas

  • Traditional personas often rely on surveys, interviews, and self-reported data, which may not accurately reflect actual user behavior.
  • Data-driven personas use real behavioral data (clickstreams, telemetry) to identify distinct user workflows and archetypes.
  • Key Takeaway: Personas must be living, evolving models that are continuously refined with actual user behavior data, rather than static marketing constructs like “Donor Dave.”

2. Creating Personas from Clickstream Data

  • The research used 3.5 million clicks from 2,400 users, clustering these into 39,000 clickstreams, which were then grouped into 10 common workflows.
  • By applying hierarchical clustering and mixed models, five behavioral personas emerged.
  • Key Takeaway: User personas should be derived from actual supporter behavior in CVG applications, leveraging data from donations, engagement with content, and interaction patterns.

3. Continuous Persona Validation

  • The personas created were validated by user behavior experts, ensuring alignment with real-world usage.
  • Key Takeaway: CVG personas should be validated against actual supporter interactions and updated periodically.

🧪 Applications for Supporter & Causes Apps

1. Using Behavioral Data for Persona Development

  • CVG Supporter App: Track user behaviors such as donation frequency, cause preferences, time spent on impact stories, and community engagement.
  • CVG Causes App: Capture fundraiser activity, donor conversion rates, and interaction with campaign tools.
  • Use clustering techniques on telemetry data to derive actual usage-based personas.

2. Moving Beyond Static Personas

  • Ensure personas do not become marketing stereotypes (e.g., Donor Dave) but reflect real supporter motivations.
  • Personas should evolve over time as behavior changes.
  • Example: Instead of assuming a persona like "Generous Greg" always donates in large amounts, track if high-value donors start to engage differently over time (e.g., shifting to peer-to-peer fundraising).

3. Identifying Common Supporter Workflows

  • Similar to Platfora’s 10 common workflows, CVG Supporter App should identify key supporter workflows such as:
    • Discovery: Finding a cause, exploring impact stories.
    • Engagement: Commenting, sharing, following campaigns.
    • Action: Donating, pledging recurring support.
    • Advocacy: Starting fundraisers, inviting friends.
  • Use hierarchical clustering to determine supporter journey patterns.

4. Keeping Personas Alive with Real Data

  • Set up a feedback loop: Regularly validate personas with supporter activity logs, surveys, and interview insights.
  • Implement machine learning models that can update personas dynamically as behavior shifts.
  • Example: If a supporter frequently engages with impact updates but never donates, they might be a high-potential donor needing a different engagement strategy.

👣 Next Steps for CVG

  • Adopt a data-driven approach to persona creation, leveraging clickstream data from actual supporter interactions.
  • Implement a system for periodic persona validation to keep them relevant.
  • Align personas with behavioral insights, rather than assumptions, to optimize engagement strategies in CVG Supporter & CVG Causes App.
  • Next Steps:
    1. Extract user interaction data from CVG platforms.
    2. Apply clustering & behavioral analytics to derive supporter personas.
    3. Integrate personas into product strategy, ensuring they drive real decision-making.
