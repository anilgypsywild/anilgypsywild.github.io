Compassion Church-to-Church Partnership Insights Survey

Lyssna Survey Ready here: [reference omitted]

Introductory Message (for Respondents)

Thank you for taking time to share your experience.

This short survey is part of our ongoing effort to understand how Compassion can better support and strengthen partnerships between Support Church Partners (SCPs) and Frontline Church Partners (FCPs).

Your insights as a Regional Manager or Relationship Specialist are essential. We want to hear, in your own words, what is working well, what could be improved, and what would make your work easier and more impactful.

There are no right or wrong answers. Please be candid and specific — your honesty helps us design resources and tools that truly meet your needs.

The survey will take about 10–12 minutes. Most questions are open-ended; feel free to use as much space as you need. Thank you for helping us learn from your experience.

Section 1. Partnership Foundations

1.1 Think about a recent time when you helped develop a new or deeper partnership between a Support Church Partner (SCP) and Compassion. What made it work well?
(Open text)

1.2 What are the most compelling reasons or “selling points” that lead church leaders to commit to partnering with Compassion?
(Open text)

1.3 From your experience, what do SCPs find most exciting or motivating about partnering with Compassion?
(Open text)

1.4 In contrast, what do SCPs most often want that we are not currently able to provide?
(Open text)

Section 2. Support and Enablement

2.1 If there were no constraints, what kinds of support from Compassion would make it easier for you to connect SCPs with FCPs?
(You can include both manual and digital ideas.)
(Open text)

2.2 With unlimited resources, what would you design or provide that would encourage SCPs to stay engaged long-term and help more children?
(Think about both senior pastors and day-to-day contacts.)
(Open text)

2.3 Describe one tool, process, or resource that has been especially helpful in your work with SCPs — and why it works.
(Open text)

2.4 Describe one tool, process, or resource that has been especially difficult or time-consuming — and why.
(Open text)

Section 3. Measurement and Church Profiles

3.1 How do you usually categorize churches in your portfolio (for example, by size, engagement level, giving capacity, etc.)?
(Open text)

3.2 How would you define a small, medium, and large church in your region?
(Please include the unit you use — for example, weekly attendance, number of sponsored children, or total giving.)
(Open text)

3.3 What size church do you work with most often?
(Single choice: Small / Medium / Large / Other + text box)

3.4 What size church do you most enjoy or prefer working with — and why?
(Open text)

3.5 What do you measure to know that a church is highly invested in Compassion?
(Open text)

3.6 Please share one example of a church you consider highly invested and what made that partnership stand out.
(Open text)

Section 4. Tailoring the Partnership Experience

4.1 What do you typically offer to churches that are highly invested in Compassion?
(Open text)

4.2 What do you offer to churches that are less invested or just beginning the relationship?
(Open text)

4.3 What have you found to be most valuable for larger SCPs? What makes those offerings effective?
(Open text)

4.4 If an SCP prefers to give a single check instead of encouraging individual sponsorships, how often do you see that — and what approach or message has worked best in that situation?
(Open text)

Section 5. Looking Ahead

5.1 If you had three wishes with no constraints, what ideas or innovations would you bring forward to make Compassion’s church partnerships stronger?
(Open text)

5.2 What would make it easier for you personally to serve and support SCPs in the way you feel called to?
(Open text)

5.3 Is there anything we haven’t asked that you believe Compassion should know to improve the SCP partnership experience?
(Open text)

Closing Message

Thank you for sharing your experience and insights.
Your feedback will directly inform future partnership strategies and a follow-up study that prioritizes which factors most influence church engagement and long-term partnership success.

We deeply appreciate your openness, honesty, and the time you’ve given to help Compassion serve churches — and children — even more effectively.
