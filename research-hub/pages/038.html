1. Research Questions

What is the problem we are trying to solve?

  1. How do users interpret and interact with the "urgent" and "non-urgent" cause labels?
  2. Does badge placement on mobile affect the clarity and appeal of cause imagery?
  3. How do users perceive the map’s limited country coverage, and how can cultural representation (e.g., flags) improve engagement?
  4. Does the placement of “Donate Now” and “Share” CTAs on mobile impact usability and conversions?

Why solve this problem now?

  • Addressing usability issues improves user trust, engagement, and donations.
  • The site is mobile-first, and optimizing the mobile experience is crucial for user retention.
  • Causes is in post-production, real-world, use.

What do we know about the problem?

  • Badge placement can obscure important parts of cause imagery.
  • CTAs are below the fold on mobile, potentially lowering conversions.
  • The map lacks visual and cultural richness, which may reduce emotional connections with causes.

2. Research Goals and Metrics

Goals:

  1. Identify usability barriers related to urgency tags and badge placement.
  2. Optimize the visual and functional aspects of the map for better engagement.
  3. Improve visibility and usability of CTAs on mobile.

Metrics:

  • Task Success Rate: Percentage of users who complete usability tasks.
  • Time on Task: Time taken to complete specific tasks, such as finding CTAs or understanding urgency tags.
  • RITE UX Metrics: SUS, NPS, CSAT
  • Qualitative Feedback: Insights from think-aloud protocols during testing.

3. Research Methods

  1. Usability Testing:
    • Objective: Evaluate user interactions with urgency tags, badge placement, map features, and CTAs.
    • Format: Remote unmoderated testing using UserZoom, focusing on mobile and desktop experiences.
  2. Post-Test Surveys:
    • Collect user feedback on trust, visual appeal, and overall satisfaction.
  3. Task Scenarios:
    • Locate and interpret an “urgent” cause label.
    • Explore the map to find supported countries and cultural elements.
    • Interact with the CTAs on a cause page and provide feedback on placement.

4. Hypotheses

  1. Users who understand urgency tags will trust the platform more and are more likely to donate.
  2. Improving badge placement will enhance the visual appeal of cause imagery.
  3. Adding cultural elements (e.g., flags) to the map will increase engagement and emotional connection.
  4. Relocating CTAs above the fold will lead to higher engagement and conversion rates.

6. Key Deliverables

  1. Usability Insights:
    • Findings on urgency tags, badge placement, map interactions, and CTA usability.
  2. Recommendations:
    • Adjust badge placement to avoid obscuring imagery.
    • Add cultural elements to the map for emotional engagement.
    • Relocate CTAs to boost conversions on mobile.
  3. Final Report:
    • A concise summary with visuals, user quotes, and prioritized recommendations.
