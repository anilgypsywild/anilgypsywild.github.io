Story Behind the Story: Lyssna Test Plan

Date: 2026-03-31

Study Objective

The goal is to compare the current Causes homepage experience (Control) against the proposed SBS homepage direction (Variant) using a lightweight, directional study that is easy to review, easy to build, and easy to iterate.

Control Variant

Study Design

  • Design type: within-subjects comparative test
  • Control: current Causes experience
  • Variant: proposed SBS experience
  • Order handling: Experience two mirrored versions of the UX
    • Version A: Control first, then Variant
    • Version B: Variant first, then Control
  • Recommended completes: 18
  • Minimum viable completes: 12

Each participant sees both versions so the same person can react to both experiences. This keeps the first round directional and reduces noise from comparing two different participant pools.

What This Study Will Answer

  1. Can participants identify a cause they would consider supporting?
  2. Are the categories and filters understandable if participants choose to use them?
  3. Do participants understand the page and cause framing at a glance?
  4. Does the Variant feel at least as usable and compelling as the Control?

What This Study Will Not Answer

  1. live homepage CTR improvement
  2. live conversion improvement
  3. end-to-end donation-flow performance
  4. definitive proof that one design is universally better for all audiences

Lysnna Test Structure

We use the same sequence of tasks for both Control and Variant.

🥘 Task 1: Cause Choice

  • Lyssna section type: First Click Test
  • Purpose: directional signal on initial cause selection and page-level choice behavior

Participant instruction

Please look at this page and choose the cause you would be most likely to support. You do not need to click into a detail page. Just select the cause you would choose.

Capture

  1. first click
  2. time to first click
  3. selected cause

Follow-up

  1. Open text: Why did you choose that cause?
  2. Scale 1 to 5: How easy was it to choose a cause on this page?
  3. Open text: What on the page helped you make that choice?

👓 Task 2: Filter Clarity

  • Lyssna section type: First Click Test
  • Purpose: directional signal on whether filtering and narrowing are understandable

Participant instruction

Imagine you want to narrow the list to causes that feel most relevant to you. Please use the page to narrow the list and identify one cause you would consider supporting.

Capture (in Lyssna)

  1. First click
  2. Time to first click
  3. Whether participants notice and use the narrowing controls

Follow-up

  1. Scale 1 to 5: How clear were the categories or filters on this page?
  2. Open text: Did the categories or filters help you narrow the choices? Why or why not?
  3. Open text: What, if anything, felt unclear or confusing?

🤔 Task 3: At-a-Glance Understanding

  • Lyssna section type: Survey or Five Second Test
  • Purpose: directional signal on comprehension and outcome clarity

Participant instruction

Without clicking deeper, what do you think this page is trying to help you do?

Follow-up

  1. Open text: What does this page help a donor do?
  2. Open text: What stood out to you most?
  3. Scale 1 to 5: How confident do you feel that you understood the options on this page?

📊 Post-Version Ratings

After the participant completes all three tasks for a version, we capture these metrics:

  1. Ease of Use: Scale 1 to 5 How easy was it to use this page overall?
  2. Clarity: Scale 1 to 5 How clear were the categories and labels overall?
  3. Comprehensibility: Scale 1 to 5 How confident do you feel that you understood the available causes?
  4. NPS: Scale 0 to 10 How likely would you be to continue exploring this experience?

🆎 Final Comparison Question

After participants see both versions, ask:

  1. Single choice Which version made it easier to identify a cause you would consider supporting?
    • Control
    • Variant
    • No meaningful difference
  2. Open text Why?
