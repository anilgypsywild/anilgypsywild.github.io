🗣️ Donors Interview Script for Community Platform

This script focuses on discovering the most impactful ways to engage major donors and gather insights on preferred communication, frequency, and level of transparency. It also seeks suggestions for improvements, aiming to enrich the relationship between donors and recipients.

Introduction

  1. Introduce Yourself and Purpose
    • Thank you for meeting with me today. 
    • My name is [Your Name] from [Organization Name]. 
    • We’re looking to better understand how we can support and enhance your experience with Compassion International, especially in terms of your connection with recipients and the impact of your contributions.
  2. Set Expectations
    • This conversation should take around [30–45 minutes].
    •  We’ll discuss your preferences in engaging with recipients, your overall experience with giving, and any ideas you may have to make the experience more meaningful. 
    • Please share openly—your insights are very valuable to us.
  3. Consent to Record (if applicable)
    • To ensure I accurately capture all your insights, would it be alright if I record our conversation? Everything you share will be confidential.

Questions: Donor Experience and Motivation

  1. Motivations for Giving
    • Can you tell me about what inspired you to support Compassion International?
    • What are the most important factors for you in making the decision to support a cause?
  2. Connection to the Cause
    • How connected do you feel to the communities and individuals your donations support?
    • Are there specific stories or interactions that have deepened this connection for you?

Questions: Engagement Preferences and Experiences

  1. Preferred Ways of Engaging with Recipients
    • How would you like to engage with recipients of your donations? For example, would you prefer regular updates, personalized letters, or other forms of communication?
    • What kind of interactions or updates make you feel most connected to the impact of your giving?
  2. Frequency and Format of Updates
    • How often do you prefer to receive updates on the impact of your donations?
    • Do you have a preference for how these updates are shared—such as through emails, letters, video updates, or even in-person events?
  3. Memorable Engagements
    • Have there been any particularly memorable updates or interactions with recipients that have stood out to you?
    • What aspects of those experiences made them special or meaningful?

Questions: Communication and Impact Measurement

  1. Information and Transparency
    • How important is it for you to know how your donation is being used, down to specific details?
    • Are there certain kinds of impact data or success stories you feel are missing, or that you’d like to see more of?
  2. Challenges in Engagement
    • Are there any barriers or frustrations you’ve encountered when trying to connect with recipients or learn about the impact of your donations?
    • How could we make it easier or more effective for you to engage and feel connected with the recipients of your support?

Questions: Support Preferences and Improvement Areas

  1. Feedback on Current Engagement Options
    • How satisfied are you with the current ways we provide updates and connections to recipients?
    • Are there specific elements you feel could be improved to help you feel more engaged or connected?
  2. New Engagement Ideas
    • If you could design an ideal way of engaging with recipients or seeing the impact of your contributions, what would that look like?
    • Are there other ways we could help you connect more deeply with the people and communities you support?

Closing

  1. Additional Thoughts
    • Is there anything else you’d like to share about your experience with Compassion International, or any other ideas you have for how we can enhance the donor experience?
  2. Thank You and Next Steps
    • Thank you so much for sharing your thoughts with us. We deeply appreciate your support and are committed to making this experience as meaningful as possible for you. If we have follow-up questions, would you be open to further discussion?
