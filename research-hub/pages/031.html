Thematic Analysis

Thematic analysis is a powerful method for identifying patterns and recurring themes within qualitative data, enabling product managers to gain a deeper understanding of user needs, concerns, and behaviors. In this approach:

  • We first begin by organizing insights into clear themes by summarizing key finding and next contextualize them within the broader user journey.
    • For each theme
      • We identify its impact on the user experience (UX)
      • Assess its implications for business outcomes such as engagement or conversion rates
      • Propose actionable design improvements to address identified challenges with a research point of view (Research POV)

Thematic analysis ensures that insights are not just theoretical but translated into practical strategies to enhance both user satisfaction and business performance.

Thematic Analysis Breakdown

Theme Description User Impact Business Impact UX Tenet(s) Examples
1. Privacy and Security Concerns Users express hesitation in providing sensitive payment information due to fears related to privacy and data security, particularly during donation processes . A sense of insecurity, leading to lower completion rates in financial transactions and potential donations. Reduced trust leads to lower conversion rates, fewer donations, and long-term loss of credibility among users. Keep users in control - Empower users over their data and transactions through straightforward privacy policies and transparent data practices. Users abandon their carts due to the UI failing to provide adequate confidence in privacy and security.
2. Clarity and Transparency of Financial Information Users report confusion regarding financial communications, such as unclear donation progress indicators and minimum amounts, leading to doubts about where their money is going. Confusion erodes trust, leading to hesitance in making donations, as users want assurance that their contributions are effectively utilized. Decreased user trust leads to lower donation rates, emphasizing the need for clear and transparent financial information. Offer informative feedback and Strive for consistency - Provide clarity and meaningful updates on donations. Issues like “Questions on 90/10 vs. 95% Donation Allocation Ratios” highlight communication needs.
3. Navigation and Usability Issues Users struggle with navigating the donation platform, finding filter options, and understanding label terminology, causing frustration. Poor navigation leads to frustration, prompting users to abandon the platform, negatively affecting user satisfaction. Reduced engagement metrics and higher drop-off rates significantly impact donation growth and user retention. Seek universal usability and Strive for consistency - Foster intuitive design for easy navigation and consistent labeling. Issues like “Unintuitive Filter Placement” expose gaps in user-centered design.
4. Consistency and Professionalism in Language and Labeling Users notice inconsistencies in terminology (e.g., labels like “Add to Cart” in a donation context) and errors in text, eroding trust. Inconsistent terminology confuses users and diminishes the platform’s professionalism and trustworthiness. Decline in user confidence, leading to decreased engagement and donation rates. Strive for consistency - Maintain uniform language and terminology for clarity and professionalism. Issues such as “Add To Card” emphasize the need for Causes-specific language.
5. Detailed and Accessible Information Users seek additional details about causes, clearer distinctions of funding statuses, and better filtering options. Lack of information hinders emotional engagement and dissuades users from donating. Low conversion rates stem from inadequate information, stressing the necessity of clear communication. Offer informative feedback and Seek universal usability - Provide easy access to detailed and relevant information for informed decisions. Issues such as “Insufficient Cause Descriptions” deter donations by lacking emotional connection.

Research POV

Theme Action Proposed UX Modification
Privacy and Security Concerns Develop comprehensive privacy policies that highlight how user data (especially payment information) will be protected. Add a dedicated "Privacy & Security" section within the app that clearly outlines data handling practices.
Clarity and Transparency Create infographics or visual aids showing how donations are allocated (e.g., percentages for causes or expenses). Include real-time donation progress bars for specific projects or child sponsorships to inform users of their contributions' impact.
Navigation and Usability Issues Map out the user journey and conduct usability testing sessions with actual users to identify navigation challenges. Implement clear menu structures and use prominent call-to-action buttons for sponsorship opportunities to simplify app navigation.
Consistency and Professionalism Establish a tone of voice guide detailing preferred terminology and language style consistent with the mission and values. Use well-defined button labels consistently throughout the app and use a review to catch inconsistencies.
Detailed and Accessible Information Gather user feedback on what additional information would be helpful regarding projects and causes. Create an accessible FAQ section and ensure sponsorship descriptions include personal stories and relevant statistics to drive engagement.
