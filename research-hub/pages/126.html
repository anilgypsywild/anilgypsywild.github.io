I. CVG GitLab User Story Checklist

Use this when writing, reviewing, or iterating on user stories in issues, MRs, or epics.

📌 1. Story Structure Check

  • Follows “As a [user], I want to [action], so that [goal/motivation]” format

💡 2. Persona Alignment

  • Mapped to a validated CVG persona (e.g., Supporter, Campaign Donor, FCP Worker)

🔍 3. Signals for Research & Design

  • Suggests observable behavior (click, scroll, share, comment, etc.)

🎯 4. Outcome-Oriented

  • Outcome is user-centered (emotional gain, clarity, confidence—not just task completion)

🤝 5. Collaboration Cues

  • Comments or @mentions include input from UX, PM, and/or Research

📂 Where to Use It

  • ✅ In GitLab issues: Include this checklist as a comment or description format
  • ✅ In research briefs: Tie stories to personas and hypotheses
  • ✅ In design reviews: Ensure emotional intent + behavioral outcomes are carried through

II. CVG GitLab User Story Example: BASE-Jump Instagram Moment

📌 1. Story Structure Check

Original Story (Too Generic):

“As a user, I want to receive updates so I know what’s happening.”

Rewritten Story (Improved):

As someone who watches BASE jump and adventure reels late at night,
I want to find relevant, emotionally resonant questions surfaced in the comments,
so that I don’t feel alone in wondering if I could ever do this too.

✅ Follows format
✅ Has context ("watches reels late at night")
✅ Includes emotional motivation (validation, curiosity, community)

💡 2. Persona Alignment

Persona:

  • 🎯 “Explorer-Observer Donor” — someone emotionally moved by stories but hesitant to act until they feel seen or socially validated.

Persona Backing:

  • ✅ Interview quote: “I watch a lot of these but rarely click. Sometimes I just want to know I’m not the only one thinking about it.”
  • ✅ Behavioral trait: Scrolls → lingers → reads comments → rarely engages unless trust is established.

Mindset Trigger:

  • Awe → Question → Curiosity → Validation Need

🔍 3. Signals for Research & Design

Observable Behavior:

  • Scrolling through comments
  • Clicking on other commenters’ profiles
  • Searching for comments that match their unspoken question

Hypothesis:

If we surface emotionally resonant comments and light-profile previews, users will engage longer and follow inspiration trails more confidently.

Design Implications:

  • Add "comment highlight" logic driven by NLP for resonance
  • Enable hover previews or trust badges in comment threads

Research Cue:

  • Track dwell time and click-throughs from emotionally tagged comments

🎯 4. Outcome-Oriented

User Outcome:

  • Emotional clarity
  • A sense of shared curiosity or belonging
  • Builds confidence that this dream isn’t just fantasy

CVG Metric Hook:

  • H.E.A.R.T:
    • ✅ Engagement (thread dwell)
    • ✅ Retention (returning to similar content)
    • ✅ Trust signal interaction (profile clicks)

🤝 5. Collaboration Cues

  • @UX tagged to review emotional comment surfacing options
  • @Research linked to thread analysis study
  • Story linked to Figma prototype showing “comment trust cues”
  • Input from storytelling team on matching comment tone to donor archetypes
