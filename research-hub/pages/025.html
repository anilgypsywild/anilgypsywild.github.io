Introduction

Additional Survey Questions for Compassion Causes

Introduction

These additional survey questions are derived from project research files to further refine our understanding of users' needs, engagement behaviors, trust perceptions, and motivations related to Compassion Causes.

These questions can complement the N2N Persona Survey and allow for deeper insights into donor behaviors, peer-to-peer fundraising engagement, and usability concerns.

1️⃣ Giving & Impact Preferences

  1. What is the most important factor when selecting a cause to support? (Dropdown)
    • The urgency of the need
    • Personal connection to the cause
    • Transparency of fund usage
    • Faith-based alignment
    • Recommendations from friends/family
    • Other (please specify)
  2. How do you decide how much to donate? (Dropdown)
    • Suggested donation amounts
    • My personal budget at the time
    • The total funding goal of the cause
    • Matching gift opportunities
    • Other (please specify)
  3. Which type of fundraising campaign most resonates with you? (Dropdown)
    • Emergency relief efforts
    • Education and child sponsorship
    • Healthcare and medical aid
    • Community development projects
    • Other (please specify)
  4. Do you prefer to see suggested donation amounts listed in: (Dropdown)
    • Ascending order (smallest to largest)
    • Descending order (largest to smallest)
    • No preference
  5. Would you like to receive impact updates after making a donation? (Dropdown)
    • Yes, via email
    • Yes, via SMS
    • Yes, via both email and SMS
    • No, I do not need updates

  2. What would encourage you to participate in peer-to-peer fundraising? (Select all that apply)

  3. Have you ever been inspired to give or fundraise based on an influencer’s recommendation? (Dropdown)
    • Yes, frequently
    • Yes, occasionally
    • No, never
  4. How important is gamification (badges, progress bars, rewards) in a fundraising platform? (Dropdown)
    • Very important
    • Somewhat important
    • Not important
  5. Would you be more likely to donate if there was a time-limited matching campaign (e.g., "Your donation is doubled for the next 24 hours")? (Dropdown)
  • Yes, absolutely
  • Maybe, depending on the cause
  • No, it wouldn't influence my decision
