HID User Journeys & Jobs to be Done (JTBD)

  • By mapping these Supporter Journeys and their Jobs to Be Done, we can optimize engagement, retention, and impact across different supporter types (Personas)
  • This structured approach ensures that Compassion International provides meaningful experiences tailored to each user’s motivations and behaviors.

1. New HID Onboarding Journey

User Journey Steps:

  1. Discovery: Learns about the platform through marketing, word of mouth, or Compassion International’s website.
  2. Account Creation: Registers and sets up their profile.
  3. Onboarding Experience: Walks through a guided tutorial, understanding the platform’s mission and functionalities.
  4. First Interaction: Browses stories, projects, or donation opportunities.
  5. Initial Engagement: Saves or follows causes, interacts with content.
  6. First Contribution: Makes a first donation or pledges support.
  7. Post-Donation Experience: Receives confirmation, emotional reinforcement (e.g., impact stories, thank-you messages).
  8. Continued Engagement: Receives notifications, sees project updates, engages with the community.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Understand the platform’s purpose and how their contributions make an impact.
  • Feel confident that their donation is used effectively.
  • Seamlessly sign up and onboard without friction.
  • Receive immediate emotional gratification from their first action.
  • Know what to do next after their first engagement.

2. Recurring HID Journey (Engaged Donors)

User Journey Steps:

  1. Regular Logins: Visits the platform consistently.
  2. Cause Tracking: Monitors the progress of past donations.
  3. Engagement with Updates: Reads stories, watches videos, and sees real-time impact reports.
  4. Community Interaction: Comments on posts, shares stories, and connects with other supporters.
  5. Additional Contributions: Participates in fundraising, recruits others, increases donation amount.
  6. Advocacy Role: Becomes an ambassador, sharing success stories and advocating for Compassion International.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Stay emotionally connected with the impact of their support.
  • Have clear visibility into how funds are used.
  • Engage with like-minded supporters for a sense of community.
  • Receive gentle nudges to contribute more when needed.
  • Feel valued and recognized for their continuous support.

3. High-Value HID Journey (Major Donors / Supporters of Large Projects)

User Journey Steps:

  1. Exclusive Onboarding: Personalized welcome experience.
  2. Direct Engagement: Direct contact with account managers or representatives.
  3. Personalized Updates: Receives tailored impact reports, video messages, and high-touch engagement.
  4. Custom Project Funding: Has the ability to choose specific projects to fund.
  5. Exclusive Events & Recognition: Invited to special donor events, behind-the-scenes looks.
  6. Long-Term Relationship Building: Maintains a close connection with Compassion International.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Receive personalized attention and a high-touch experience.
  • Understand large-scale impact beyond standard reports.
  • Directly influence projects based on their values.
  • Get insider access to exclusive updates and invitations.
  • Be recognized as an elite supporter to build a legacy.

4. Lapsed HID Journey (Re-Engagement Flow)

User Journey Steps:

  1. Inactivity Detected: The system flags supporters who haven’t engaged in a set timeframe.
  2. Re-Engagement Messaging: Supporter receives personalized messages about past contributions and recent impact.
  3. Special Incentives: Limited-time matching funds, exclusive updates to reignite interest.
  4. Survey & Feedback: Encourages supporters to share why they stopped engaging.
  5. Simplified Re-Onboarding: Offers an easy way to return to donating or engaging.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Be reminded of the importance of their past contributions.
  • Receive a compelling reason to return and re-engage.
  • Experience a frictionless return path to contributing again.
  • Feel that their absence was noticed and valued.

5. One-Time HID Journey

User Journey Steps:

  1. Immediate Need Recognition: The donor sees a compelling story or campaign.
  2. Quick Contribution Flow: Fast, seamless donation experience.
  3. Instant Gratification: Receives confirmation and an impact preview.
  4. Delayed Follow-Up: Receives updates on how their donation was used.
  5. Soft Upsell to Recurring Giving: Encouraged to consider ongoing support.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Have a simple, fast donation process without unnecessary friction.
  • Feel an immediate emotional reward for their generosity.
  • Receive a clear, credible impact follow-up to reinforce trust.
  • Be nudged toward future involvement in a non-intrusive way.

6. Peer-to-Peer HID Fundraising Journey

User Journey Steps:

  1. Fundraiser Creation: Supporter sets up a personal campaign.
  2. Outreach & Sharing: Uses social media, email, and messaging to promote the fundraiser.
  3. Donations & Engagement: Tracks contributions, updates supporters.
  4. Milestone Recognition: Receives badges, progress updates.
  5. Final Celebration: Thank-you messages sent to contributors.
  6. Post-Campaign Transition: Encouraged to stay involved.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Easily create and share fundraisers with minimal effort.
  • Have built-in tools to drive donations and engagement.
  • Receive motivation and rewards to keep momentum going.
  • Have an easy way to close out campaigns while staying engaged with the platform.

7. Volunteer & Community Contributor HID Journey

User Journey Steps:

  1. Interest Discovery: Learns about volunteering opportunities.
  2. Application & Approval: Submits interest and gets approved.
  3. Onboarding & Training: Receives resources for their role.
  4. Active Contribution: Completes volunteer activities (mentoring, content creation, moderating, etc.).
  5. Recognition & Encouragement: Receives feedback and appreciation.
  6. Long-Term Engagement: Offered continued roles or increased responsibilities.

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):

  • Easily find and apply for volunteer roles.
  • Have a clear onboarding process to understand responsibilities.
  • Feel a sense of purpose and impact in their contributions.
  • Be recognized and appreciated for their efforts.
  • Have a path to continued involvement in the community.

🧁 Appendix: Additional Research Exploration for HID Journeys

Understanding these additional considerations helps refine each journey to enhance engagement, reduce friction, and maximize long-term supporter retention. Tailored solutions ensure each supporter type receives a meaningful, impactful experience.

1. New HID Onboarding Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • Users may hesitate at the account creation stage if too much personal information is requested.
  • The first donation is a critical moment; reinforcing trust with transparency is key.
  • Supporters may require an easy way to return to their onboarding tutorial.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Confusion about donation impact.
  • Difficulty navigating onboarding steps.
  • Overwhelming number of causes to choose from.

Solutions:

  • Interactive walkthroughs.
  • Tiered onboarding experience based on user engagement level.
  • Simplified donation explanations with visual impact reports.

2. Recurring HID Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • Regular engagement correlates with continued giving.
  • Emotional connection with impact updates increases donor retention.
  • Personalized nudges help maintain engagement without feeling intrusive.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Lack of visibility into how donations are used.
  • Fatigue from frequent donation requests.
  • Limited ability to track personal giving history.

Solutions:

  • A personal donor dashboard for tracking contributions.
  • Transparent breakdowns of fund allocations.
  • Monthly storytelling emails showcasing impact.

3. High-Value HID Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • High-value donors expect a more personal touch.
  • Exclusive experiences reinforce their connection to the cause.
  • Legacy-building opportunities can encourage increased involvement.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Standardized communications may feel impersonal.
  • Unclear differentiation between standard and high-value supporter experiences.
  • Lack of influence over funded projects.

Solutions:

  • Dedicated account managers for high-value donors.
  • Invitation-only events and personalized video updates.
  • Tailored funding options with project-specific reports.

4. Lapsed HID Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • Supporters may lapse due to financial constraints or loss of interest.
  • Re-engagement strategies work best when they tap into past emotional connections.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Feeling overwhelmed by past donation expectations.
  • Lack of compelling reason to return.
  • Unclear re-engagement pathways.

Solutions:

  • Personalized messages referencing past contributions.
  • Special time-sensitive matching funds or initiatives.
  • Option to contribute in non-monetary ways (e.g., volunteering).

5. One-Time HID Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • Many one-time donors may be emotionally moved by a specific event or crisis.
  • The challenge is to transition them into recurring supporters.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Lack of follow-up after donation.
  • Feeling their contribution is insignificant compared to recurring donors.
  • Unclear next steps beyond their one-time action.

Solutions:

  • Immediate post-donation thank-you with real-time impact updates.
  • Subtle invitations to support again without pressure.
  • Opportunities to engage beyond donations (e.g., advocacy, social sharing).

6. Peer-to-Peer HID Fundraising Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • Fundraisers need easy-to-use tools to maximize their outreach.
  • Social proof (friends/family donating) increases engagement.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Difficulty setting up and customizing fundraising pages.
  • Uncertainty about how to engage potential donors.
  • Lack of visible campaign progress can reduce motivation.

Solutions:

  • Pre-built fundraising templates with customization options.
  • Gamification features (badges, milestones, progress trackers).
  • Integrated social media sharing for wider reach.

7. Volunteer & Community Contributor HID Journey

Behavioral Insights:

  • Volunteers are motivated by impact and recognition.
  • Clear role definitions increase engagement and retention.

Potential Friction Points:

  • Lack of structure or clear tasks for volunteers.
  • Uncertainty about how their work contributes to the larger mission.
  • Difficulty finding relevant opportunities based on skills and interests.

Solutions:

  • Skill-based volunteer matching.
  • Tiered recognition system with badges and awards.
  • Structured feedback loops to show volunteers their impact.
