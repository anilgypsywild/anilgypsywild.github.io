Crazy 8s: SCP/FCP Partnership Landing Page Concepts

(For Church-to-Church Experience)

1. Partnership Profile & Vision Page

Purpose: Anchor identity and shared calling.
Concept:

  • Hero header with both churches’ names (SCP + FCP) and “United in Christ for Every Child.”
  • Vision statement block editable by RM/RS or SCP lead.
  • Key facts panel: # children sponsored, region, years partnered.
  • Timeline of partnership milestones (“First Compassion Sunday,” “First child letter exchange”).
  • CTA: “Update our Vision Statement.”

2. Progress Updates Page

Purpose: Keep the relationship alive with tangible, current information.
Concept:

  • Feed of visual updates from FCP projects (images, notes, prayer points).
  • Progress meters for pledged vs. achieved sponsorships or church goals.
  • Optional “Send encouragement” or “Leave a comment” feature for SCPs.
  • RM/RS dashboard view: recent communications, update frequency, response tracking.
  • CTA: “Share this Update with Your Congregation.”

3. Request Compassion Offerings Page

Purpose: Provide a simple tool for churches to request ministry resources or collaboration.
Concept:

  • Form-based request center with dropdowns: Events, Campaign Materials, Speakers, Child Packets.
  • Short autofill fields: church size, audience, preferred theme.
  • AI-style recommendation: “Churches like yours recently hosted a Compassion Sunday.”
  • Dynamic acknowledgment banner: “Thank you — your Compassion contact will respond within 48 hrs.”
  • CTA: “Submit Offering Request.”

4. Church Impacts Page

Purpose: Show measurable transformation with emotional and data depth.
Concept:

  • Map-based visualization: “Your partnership is blessing X children in Y community.”
  • Story cards featuring photos or brief FCP testimonies.
  • Impact summary bar chart (modeled on your MaxDiff-style visual) with metrics like “Children sponsored,” “Meals provided,” “Local volunteers mobilized.”
  • Rotating “Impact Story of the Month.”
  • CTA: “Celebrate Your Church’s Reach.”

5. Prayer Requests Page

Purpose: Build mutual intercession between churches.
Concept:

  • Digital prayer wall with real-time posts from FCPs and SCPs.
  • Filter options: “Needs,” “Answered Prayers,” “Community Recovery,” “Children’s Education.”
  • Animated counter: “X prayers lifted this month.”
  • Option to click “I prayed” — increments a visible tally to encourage ongoing engagement.
  • CTA: “Add a Prayer.”

6. Testimonial Celebrations Page

Purpose: Highlight stories of hope and faith to inspire others.
Concept:

  • Video wall: short 30–60 sec clips from pastors, children, or donors.
  • Story categories: “New Partnerships,” “Milestone Moments,” “Answered Prayers.”
  • Optional “Nominate a Story” button for RMs or FCP leads.
  • Integrates easily into Compassion’s social content or donor newsletters.
  • CTA: “Share Your Testimony.”

7. Unified Partnership Home Hub

Purpose: Provide one central home for SCP/FCP collaboration.
Concept:

  • Six tiles linking to all above pages: Profile, Updates, Offerings, Impact, Prayer, Testimonials.
  • Personalized greeting (“Welcome Pastor John — your FCP partner is Iglesia Esperanza”).
  • Quick metrics card: “Children sponsored this quarter: 18,” “Next Compassion Sunday: March 2.”
  • Seamless single sign-on experience for staff or partner churches.
  • CTA: “Go to Dashboard.”

8. Celebration & Renewal Page

Purpose: Reinforce long-term engagement and faith-centered growth.
Concept:

  • Year-in-review video montage auto-generated from church’s partnership data.
  • Section to renew or update the partnership vision for next year.
  • Reflection prompts: “What did God do through your partnership this year?”
  • Button to schedule next RM/RS check-in or event planning call.
  • CTA: “Plan the Next Chapter.”
