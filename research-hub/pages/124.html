Supporter Product Research Collaboration

A working and traceable collaboration space between Supporter Product Management and UX Research
Prepared by: Anil Wild, Senior Research Analyst (research team)
For visibility: Kayleight PM (Causes), [name omitted] (UX Director)

Purpose of This Page 🎯

This GitLab page exists to formalize how Product and Research collaborate on Compassion Causes. It provides:

  • A transparent record of all onboarding, mentorship, product guidance, and research support
  • A single source of truth for questions, decisions, and milestones
  • Auditability and proof of research contribution across quarters
  • A governance structure for how research requests are evaluated and prioritized

This page also ensures that our collaboration time is used responsibly, tied to product outcomes, and visible to leadership.

Why This Page Exists

To onboard PMs into standard Research practices, this workspace enables:

  1. A structured onboarding path for a new PM to UX and Research
  2. A clear governance model so research time is not spent on unscoped or unaligned work
  3. Alignment with expectations: research must drive product decisions, not ad hoc curiosity
  4. Visibility into how research collaboration contributes to velocity, clarity, and planning

This workspace explicitly supports team transparency, operational maturity, and leadership oversight.

Research Governance Model

All research needs to pass through this structure to be valid, scorable, and resourced.

  • Key Principle If a request is not tied to a product milestone, engineering dependency, or revenue or trust impact, it cannot enter the research queue.

This is to protect resources and ensure research contributes measurable value.

Collaboration Expectations

To support a healthy partnership and protect research capacity:

1. All research requests must use the Research Intake Form
This is non negotiable. It ensures:

  • Prioritization based on product impact
  • Leadership visibility
  • Resource planning
  • Prevention of non actionable studies 🧯

2. GitLab is our system of record
Teams and email threads disappear. This page preserves context, decisions, and effort.

3. Research requires lead time
Usability tests, surveys, or mixed methods studies require planning. Urgent unscoped requests dilute quality.

4. PM provides product context and milestones
To justify a study, I need clarity on:

  1. What decision this research will inform
  2. When that decision must be made
  3. Who owns the downstream execution
  4. What success looks like

This supports the research team’s data informed culture.

5. Research will always return insights tied to Product
We will not deliver academic or disconnected findings. Everything is tied to roadmap, user benefit, or strategic clarity.
