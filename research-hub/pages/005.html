🗒️ MaxDiff Survey — Version A (Full Instructions)

Intro Text (put at top of the form)

`Thank you for participating.

In this survey, you will see a series of short screens.
Each screen shows five possible motivators for giving to Compassion Causes.

For each screen:

  • Select the ONE statement that motivates you the most (Best)
  • Select the ONE statement that motivates you the least (Worst)

Please answer based on your personal preference. There are no right or wrong answers. `

Question Format

Each screen = two required multiple-choice questions with the same five options:

  • QxA (Best): “Which of these motivates you the MOST?”
  • QxB (Worst): “Which of these motivates you the LEAST?”

Both must be answered before moving on.
