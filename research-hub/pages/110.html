📘 Retro: How Our Past Research Could Map to UCD Framework

This is UCD based retro and future-fit alignment using completed major research projects in practice. It contrasts how these projects actually unfolded versus how they could ideally follow the UCD process.

🔄 Comparison Table: Actual vs. Ideal UCD Alignment

🔁 High-Level UCD Retro with “Redo Vision” per Project

1. Supporter Information-Architecture (IA) & Navigation Usability

  • What We Did:
    Post-launch test with 10 users. Findings reactive. No early nav audit, no IA benchmark.
  • UCD Redo (7 weeks total):
    • Discovery (1.5w): Benchmark IA (CharityWater, GoFundMe), run nav CSAT pulse
    • Define (2w): Open/closed card sort, tree test variants, stakeholder IA hypotheses
    • Design (2w): Mid-fi IA prototype, run Maze test or unmoderated click test
    • Build (1.5w): Intercept survey + CTR tracking for nav click success
  • What We Missed:
    Clear structure for HID donors, early nav labeling validation
  • With UCD:
    Launch-ready IA tested across segments, less confusion, higher engagement in first 30 days

2. Community Platform – FCP Beta Role-Based Analysis

  • What We Did:
    Role-based themes derived after launch, post-hoc quant/qual analysis. No MVP feedback loops per role.
  • UCD Redo (7 weeks total):
    • Discovery: Map PD, HW, Acct workflows; role surveys (CSAT, Trust)
    • Define: Co-create success metrics per role; test offline/draft UX concepts
    • Design: Task-based testing split by role; highlight friction & delights
    • Build: Role-specific XP baselines + post-release CSAT deltas
  • What We Missed:
    Pre-launch feedback loops from the most critical roles
  • With UCD:
    Tailored onboarding, draft mode tested before rollout, fewer missed expectations

3. Causes Pre-Production UX Baseline

  • What We Did:
    Baseline collected successfully, but metrics were unclear until late.
  • UCD Redo (7 weeks total):
    • Discovery: Flow friction benchmarks from Amplitude, competitor audit
    • Define: Map to HEART + SUPR-Q before testing, align with donation KPIs
    • Design: Lo-fi clickthroughs tested for donor trust, allocation comprehension
    • Build: A/B test “urgent vs impact-first” designs post-launch
  • What We Missed:
    Early decision-making confidence in metrics; better MVP guardrails
  • With UCD:
    Tighter story-flow alignment with user trust → fewer revisions post-launch

4. Supporter Personas (High Impact Donors)

  • What We Did:
    Survey-based personas after product scope was already set.
  • UCD Redo (6–7 weeks):
    • Discovery: PM interviews, draft “proto-personas” from assumptions
    • Define: MaxDiff study to validate traits, segment via clustering
    • Design: Flow review for 3 top personas; test messaging/impact language
    • Build: Survey or analytics tagging tied to persona behavior in product
  • What We Missed:
    Behavioral validation, messaging alignment, PM use of personas for shaping product experiences.
  • With UCD:
    Personas adopted by design + marketing. Data-backed strategy vs surface-level doc.

5. Mobile UX Evaluation (Supporter Ops)

  • What We Did:
    Ran one round of testing after mobile MVP locked. Feedback came too late.
  • UCD Redo (6 weeks):
    • Discovery: Survey for mobile users w/ friction logging (low CSAT, slow flows)
    • Define: Test low-data edge cases, MVP workflows (e.g., bad signal)
    • Design: Tap target tests, scroll fatigue testing, mid-fi evals
    • Build: Intercept for mobile-specific bugs, rerun SUS
  • What We Missed:
    Mobile context (field realities), scorecard by device
  • With UCD:
    Earlier mobile-first improvements → engineering focus better targeted

6. Community FCP Survey & Qual Study

  • What We Did:
    Ran independently of roadmap; high quality but no early stakeholder alignment.
  • UCD Redo (7 weeks):
    • Discovery: Align with [name omitted]’s goals + SDS ethical triggers
    • Define: Segment survey questions by spiritual, emotional, behavioral impact
    • Design: Test early content types (offline photos, approval cycles)
    • Build: Launch timing triggers → measure donor reactions to FCP media
  • What We Missed:
    Strategic alignment to roadmap; no feature test-prep
  • With UCD:
    Trusted roadmap-ready research tied to release cadence

🧠 Meta Takeaways

Pattern Fix with UCD
Research as validation → UCD makes it co-creation + prioritization
Personas not used → Personas tested + tied to flows
Metrics chosen late → Metrics defined in Define, tracked in Build
Stakeholders bypassing research → Discovery gets their input up front
Time = too rushed → UCD doesn’t slow things — it front-loads learning

🧩 Summary: Key Gaps UCD Would Fix

Gap How UCD Process Helps
Research happening post-design Early Discovery + Define phases prioritize user input
Metrics misaligned with UX goals Scorecards + HEART ensure consistent measurement
Personas detached from flows Define phase uses personas in journey, flow, IA decisions
Insights ignored in sprints RITE + pre/post Build testing loops them in
Surveys not aligned to triggers Define stage maps intercept logic to journey moments

✅ Next Steps: How We’ll Use This Going Forward

  1. All research briefs will use the UCD lifecycle template (Discovery → Build).
  2. PMs + UX must co-review success metrics in Define phase before user testing begins.
  3. Research readouts will now align findings to UCD stages, showing “where we are” in the cycle.
