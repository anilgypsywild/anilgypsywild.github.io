1. Research Brief: Push Notification Eval – Community App

What is the Problem We Are Solving?

Supporters currently encounter the mobile push notification prompt without any explanation or context appearing abruptly during profile setup. This creates confusion about its purpose and leads to a risk of users disabling notifications prematurely, reducing long-term engagement with field updates.

Why Are We Solving This Now?

Push notifications are the primary channel through which supporters receive meaningful updates from the field. However, due to usability concerns, we’ve temporarily disabled them during onboarding. With more supporters being added in the coming weeks, we must validate and improve the primer screen now—so we can safely reintroduce notifications into the live app experience.

What Will We Do With the Results?

  • Validate whether users understand the purpose and value of notifications.
  • Identify optimal timing within the onboarding flow.
  • Refine the screen’s wording to improve clarity and opt-in behavior.
  • Use the findings to inform final UI design and enable the notification flow by end of month.

What Do We Know Now?

  • Notifications were turned off due to premature opt-outs during initial testing.
  • Supporters were confused by the system-level prompt that appeared without context.
  • Notification interactions are a core value-driver for the Community experience, especially for users unfamiliar with how Compassion updates work.
  • No current users have disabled notifications—but only because the feature was paused at launch.

2. UserZoom Script

Study Introduction

Thank you for participating in this usability study for the Compassion Community App. We’re exploring a new screen that introduces push notifications—an important feature that keeps supporters informed about field updates and responses from the community.

How Your Feedback Helps

Your feedback will help us improve when and how we present this screen so that new users understand its purpose, feel confident opting in, and stay engaged with the Compassion Community platform. By optimizing clarity, timing, and usability, we can increase supporter retention and interaction.

Usability Tasks with Success Criteria

Task 1: Verbiage Clarity – First Impressions

Instruction:
Imagine you’re setting up the Compassion Community App for the first time. Read the notification screen and tell us what it’s asking you to do.

Metrics Captured:

  • Task success rate: ≥80% accurate interpretation
  • Time on task: ≤15 seconds
  • Confidence rating (1–5): Mean ≥4
  • CSAT (1–7): Mean ≥6

Open-ended Q:
What do you think this screen is asking you to do? What, if anything, was unclear?

Task 2: Action Interpretation – Understanding the Button

Instruction:
Now, imagine you tap “Allow Notifications.” What do you expect will happen next?

Metrics Captured:

  • Task success rate: ≥80% expectation matches actual UX
  • Error rate: <20% false assumptions (e.g., immediate alerts)
  • Confidence rating (1–5)
  • CSAT

Open-ended Q:
What made you think that would happen?

Task 3: Timing Expectation – When Should This Screen Appear?

Instruction:
When setting up a new app, when would you expect to see this kind of notification permission screen?

Metrics Captured:

  • Timing classification (e.g., early, mid, post-onboarding)
  • Task success rate (alignment with design intent): ≥70%
  • Open feedback themes

Open-ended Q:
Why does that timing feel appropriate to you?

Task 4: Comparative Placement Preference

Instruction:
Which timing do you prefer for seeing this screen?
A) Before profile setup
B) After profile setup

Metrics Captured:

  • Preference frequency (target ≥60% consensus for one option)
  • Confidence rating (1–5)
  • CSAT

Open-ended Q:
What makes that option better for you?

Task 5: Behavioral Intent – Would You Opt In?

Instruction:
If this were a real app, would you tap “Allow Notifications” or “Skip”?

Metrics Captured:

  • Click behavior: Target ≥70% opt-in rate
  • Time to decision: Mean ≤10 seconds
  • Confidence rating

Open-ended Q:
What influenced your decision?

Task 6: Overall Feedback and Suggestions

Instruction:
Now that you’ve interacted with the screen, what suggestions do you have for improving it?

Metrics Captured:

  • Qualitative themes (wording, visual, placement)
  • Frequency count of common improvement suggestions

Open-ended Q:
What would you change about this screen to make it better?

Post-Test: SUPR-Q Items (7-point Likert)

Please rate your experience with this screen and overall interaction using the following:

  1. I found the experience easy to use.
  2. The interface was easy to navigate.
  3. I felt confident using the screen.
  4. I trust this app to handle my information appropriately.
  5. I would recommend this app to a friend or colleague.
  6. I felt the screen content was relevant and clear.
  7. Overall, I am satisfied with the experience.

Note: SUPR-Q composite score will be calculated using normalized item averages based on Sauro & Lewis's framework (2016).
