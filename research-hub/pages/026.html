Usability Testing for Compassion Causes

A Baseline Study with a Catch Trial

Introduction to Participants

Thank you for participating in this usability study for Compassion Causes. Your time and feedback are greatly appreciated. Our goal is to assess how users interact with the platform, ensuring it is clear, trustworthy, and easy to use. Your insights will help us enhance the experience for donors who want to support causes aligned with their values.

Compassion International's mission is to release children from poverty in Jesus’ name. Through this study, we aim to strengthen the platform's ability to connect donors with meaningful causes that support this mission.

We appreciate your help in shaping a platform that honors generosity and service. Thank you again for your participation!

Usability Tasks for Unmoderated User Testing (UserZoom)

Each task will help us better understand how users interact with the platform. Please follow the instructions carefully, and if you encounter any challenges, know that your feedback is extremely valuable in helping us improve the experience.

Task 1: Finding and Selecting a Cause

📌 Context: You are interested in donating but would like to find a cause that aligns with your values.

✅ Instruction: Please search for a cause using the provided filters and select one that resonates with you.

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Time on Task (Efficiency)
  • Task Success Rate (Completion)
  • Click Paths & Search Behavior (Navigation)
  • CSAT Score (Satisfaction)

Task 2: Understanding Cause Information & Donation Allocation

📌 Context: Before donating, you want to ensure that your funds are allocated appropriately.

✅ Instruction: Please review a selected cause’s description and verify its donation breakdown.

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Task Success Rate (Can users find allocation details?)
  • SUPR-Q Information Clarity Score
  • Trust Confidence Score (≥75% confidence in allocation transparency)

Task 3: Completing a Donation

📌 Context: You have found a cause you want to support and are ready to donate.

✅ Instruction: Please complete the donation process by selecting a donation amount and payment method.

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Task Completion Rate (≥90%)
  • Time on Task (Checkout Speed)
  • CSAT Score (Checkout Experience)
  • Payment Confidence Score (≥75%)

Task 4: Navigating and Using Filters

📌 Context: You want to explore different categories of causes using filtering options.

✅ Instruction: Please use the platform’s filtering system to find a cause in a specific category (e.g., Education, Health, Emergency Relief).

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Time on Task (Ease of Navigation)
  • Click Tracking (Are filters used effectively?)
  • CSAT Score (Navigation & Filter Clarity)

Task 5: Post-Donation Confirmation & Tracking Impact

📌 Context: You have donated and now want to see how your contribution is making an impact.

✅ Instruction: Please check for updates on the cause you donated to, such as success stories or financial reports.

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Engagement Rate (Do users check updates?)
  • CSAT Score (Follow-up Experience)
  • Repeat Donor Rate Prediction (Likelihood to donate again)

Task 6: Understanding Donor Testimonials & Trust Indicators

📌 Context: Before donating, you want to see if other people trust this platform.

✅ Instruction: Please locate and read through donor testimonials or any verification markers that indicate the platform’s legitimacy.

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Trust Confidence Score (≥75%)
  • Engagement Rate (How many users interact with testimonials?)
  • NPS Score (Likelihood to recommend platform)

Task 7: (Catch Trial) Searching for a Non-Existent Cause

📌 Context: You are looking for a cause that funds clean drinking water in urban communities. You’ve heard that such a cause exists on Compassion Causes, and you want to find it to make a donation.

✅ Instruction: Please use the search and filtering options to find a cause related to urban water sanitation projects.

(Note: No such cause exists on the platform.)

📊 Metrics Captured:

  • Time on Task (How long users persist before abandoning the search)
  • Error Rate (Attempts to refine search or incorrect cause selection)
  • Frustration Indicators (Number of back-and-forth navigations, excessive scrolling, or rage clicks)
  • CSAT Score (Self-reported satisfaction post-task, assessing perceived difficulty and platform confidence)
  • Exit Behavior (Do users assume they made a mistake, blame the system, or seek external help?)

Appendix

Catch Trial Rationale for Causes Usability Test

Why Include a Catch Trial?

A Catch Trial introduces a controlled failure scenario in usability testing to evaluate how users respond to unexpected difficulties. This helps us understand key pain points and usability challenges that might not surface in a standard test.

Key Benefits for Compassion Causes:

  1. Identifying User Frustration Points:
    • Understanding how users react when they fail to complete a task helps identify navigation bottlenecks and UI flaws.
    • Example: If users struggle to find a cause that does not exist, it reveals search and filtering limitations.
  2. Assessing Problem-Solving Behaviors:
    • Users often try alternative approaches when they encounter issues. Tracking click paths, retries, and time spent helps optimize platform usability.
    • Example: If users repeatedly search for a missing feature, it indicates a lack of discoverability.
  3. Benchmarking Sentiment & Retention Risks:
    • Failed experiences can lead to frustration, loss of trust, and drop-offs. Measuring engagement before and after failure highlights critical risk points for donor retention.
    • Example: If users abandon the platform after encountering difficulty, it signals a need for better guidance or system messaging.

Strategic Use in Causes Testing

By incorporating Catch Trials, we gain accurate diagnostic insights into how resilient and intuitive the platform is. This data informs specific UX optimizations to enhance donor confidence, reduce drop-offs, and improve overall satisfaction. 🚀
