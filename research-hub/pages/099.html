Applying SUPR-Q to Measure SUS at Compassion

⚕️ Why It Matters

Compassion is focused on making sure our digital platforms are smooth, intuitive, and engaging. To get there, we’re using the Standardized User Experience Percentile Rank Questionnaire (SUPR-Q) in tandem with the System Usability Scale (SUS). This gives us a clear, data-backed view of usability, trust, design, and engagement.

Breaking Down SUPR-Q

SUPR-Q is an 8-question framework designed to evaluate four major areas of user experience:

  • Usability: Can users navigate and complete tasks without frustration?
  • Credibility: Do users find the information reliable and trustworthy?
  • Appearance: Does the platform look professional and well-designed?
  • Loyalty: Will users come back and recommend it to others?

Each category has structured rating scales to ensure accurate measurement and benchmarking.

8 SUPR-Q Questions

1. Usability (5-Point Likert Scale)

  • This platform is easy to use. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
  • Navigation is straightforward. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)

2. Credibility (5-Point Likert Scale)

  • The content feels credible. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
  • I trust the information provided here. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)

3. Appearance (5-Point Likert Scale)

  • The platform has an appealing design. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
  • The visual layout is clean and professional. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)

4. Loyalty

  • Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) (7-Point Likert Scale): I will likely use this platform again. (1 = Extremely Dissatisfied, 7 = Extremely Satisfied)
  • Net Promoter Score (NPS) (0-10 Scale): How likely are you to recommend this platform? (0 = Not at all likely, 10 = Extremely Likely)

Appendix: Calculating a 100-Point Scale

Each SUPR-Q dimension is transformed into a standardized 0-100 scale using the following:

  1. Convert each response into a percentage:
    • 5-Point Likert: ((Response - 1) / 4) * 100
    • 7-Point CSAT: ((Response - 1) / 6) * 100
    • 0-10 NPS: Response * 10
  2. Compute the mean score for each category (Usability, Credibility, Appearance, Loyalty)
  3. Aggregate overall SUPR-Q Score by averaging all categories

Summative Scores for Trend Analysis

  • Usability Score = Average of Usability responses (scaled to 100)
  • Credibility Score = Average of Credibility responses (scaled to 100)
  • Appearance Score = Average of Appearance responses (scaled to 100)
  • Loyalty Score = (CSAT Score + NPS Score) / 2
  • Overall SUPR-Q Score = (Usability + Credibility + Appearance + Loyalty) / 4

🗺️ Interpretation Guide

👷 For Product Managers:

  • Scores below 50 indicate critical usability issues requiring immediate action.
  • Scores between 50-75 show areas needing refinement but are functional.
  • Scores above 75 suggest strong usability, but continuous improvements are necessary.

For Designers:

  • Appearance scores under 60 suggest the need for design revisions and A/B testing.
  • Scores between 60-80 highlight areas where users appreciate the design but may still have pain points.
  • Scores above 80 indicate a well-received design that aligns with user expectations.

🥼 For Researchers:

  • Monitor trends across multiple assessments to identify recurring usability or credibility concerns.
  • Compare SUPR-Q data against qualitative insights to gain a holistic understanding.

👾 For C-Level Executives:

  • Scores above 75 demonstrate a strong user experience that aligns with company goals.
  • Scores below 50 may indicate risks to retention, engagement, and overall brand trustworthiness.
  • NPS trends are a key indicator of customer loyalty and long-term growth potential.

🎯 Final Thoughts

By implementing this refined SUPR-Q approach, can measure usability effectively, track UX trends over time, and make data-backed decisions that enhance the user experience.

Case-Study

Applying SUPR-Q to FCP Beta

To improve the FCP Community App survey, we can integrate the Standardized User Experience Percentile Rank Questionnaire (SUPR-Q) methodology. This will provide a structured evaluation of usability, credibility, appearance, and loyalty, ensuring a comprehensive assessment of the app’s user experience.

Modifying the Scale Questions for FCP Beta

1. Usability (5-Point Likert Scale)

Current Questions:

  • The app’s features and functions meet my requirements. (1-5 Likert)
  • The app is easy to use. (1-5 Likert)

Modified for SUPR-Q Alignment:

  • This app is easy to use. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
  • Navigation within the app is straightforward. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)

2. Credibility (5-Point Likert Scale)

Current Questions:

  • The app aligns with our mission and values. (1-7 Likert)
  • I feel confident about the app's intentions, actions, and outputs. (1-7 Likert)
  • The app upholds our organization’s integrity. (1-7 Likert)

Modified for SUPR-Q Alignment:

  • The information provided in the app is trustworthy. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
  • The app provides accurate and reliable information. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)

3. Appearance (5-Point Likert Scale)

Current Questions:

  • The app has an appealing design. (1-7 Likert)
  • The visual layout is clean and professional. (1-7 Likert)

Modified for SUPR-Q Alignment:

  • The app is visually appealing. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
  • The app’s design is clean and well-organized. (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)

4. Loyalty

Current Questions:

  • How likely are you to recommend this app to other FCPs? (0-10 NPS scale)
  • How likely are you to continue using the app after the beta test? (1-5 Likert)

Modified for SUPR-Q Alignment:

  • Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) (7-Point Likert Scale): How satisfied are you with the overall experience of the app? (1 = Extremely Dissatisfied, 7 = Extremely Satisfied)
  • Net Promoter Score (NPS) (0-10 Scale): How likely are you to recommend this app to a colleague or another FCP? (0 = Not at all likely, 10 = Extremely Likely)

Implementation Steps for FCP Beta

  1. Administer the SUPR-Q adapted survey at the end of the beta test.
  2. Analyze results by transforming responses into a 0-100 scale.
  3. Monitor trends over time by comparing scores from iterative surveys.
  4. Refine the app’s usability, credibility, and appearance based on user feedback.
  5. Enhance retention and satisfaction through targeted improvements.

Conclusion

By applying SUPR-Q to the FCP Beta survey, we create a data-driven method for evaluating user experience, ensuring the app aligns with the needs of its users. This structured approach allows us to track trends, prioritize improvements, and build a user-friendly platform that enhances engagement and trust.

SUPR-Q Computation

Steps to Calculate SUPR-Q Scores for FCP Beta

To calculate the final SUPR-Q score, we break it down into sections and convert Likert scale responses into a 0-100 scale.

1. Convert Raw Likert Responses to a 0-100 Scale

Each Likert response is transformed using the formula:

$\text{Scaled Score} = \left(\frac{\text{Response} - \text{Min Value}}{\text{Max Value} - \text{Min Value}}\right) \times 100$

  • 5-Point Scale (Usability, Credibility, Appearance)

    $\left(\frac{\text{Response} - 1}{5 - 1}\right) \times 100$

  • 7-Point Scale (CSAT)

    $\left(\frac{\text{Response} - 1}{7 - 1}\right) \times 100$

  • NPS (0-10 Scale)

    Response × 10

2. Calculate Section-Wise Scores

For each section, compute the average score of the two questions.

$\text{Section Score} = \frac{\text{Scaled Score for Q1} + \text{Scaled Score for Q2}}{2}$

3. Calculate Loyalty Score

Loyalty is based on CSAT (7-point scale) and NPS (0-10 scale).

$\text{Loyalty Score} = \frac{\text{CSAT Score} + \text{NPS Score}}{2}$

4. Calculate the Overall SUPR-Q Score

The final score is the average of all sections:

$\text{SUPR-Q Score} = \frac{\text{Usability Score} + \text{Credibility Score} + \text{Appearance Score} + \text{Loyalty Score}}{4}$
