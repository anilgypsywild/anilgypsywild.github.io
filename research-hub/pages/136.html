Research Questions

  • Descriptive (Most Concrete) Understand what, when, where, or how frequently events of interest occur
  • comparative Understand the difference between groups or things
  • Perceptual Understand user attitudes, interpretations, etc
  • Motivational Understand why users do what they do
  • Foundational (Most Exploratory) Understand a broad unexplored area

Deliverables

Low-Fi to High-Fi

- Insight Decks

Typical Research Process

  • Scoping (1-2 weeks)
    • Define the question or topic to explore
    • Approach:
      • Research Request Intake Form
      • Define key questions
      • Identify criteria for user recruitment
      • Identify next steps with expected research insights
      • Add above content to research-brief
  • Preparation (2-4 weeks)
    • Move from questions to research study
      • Cross-functional kick off meeting to align on objectives, methods, milestones
      • Recruit users for study or experiment
      • Create research materials: guides, surveys, prototypes, protocols, etc.
  • Run experiment (2-3 weeks)
    • Deploy tools to gather data of interest
      • Review existing research
      • Survey
      • Run concept test
      • Run usability test
      • In-depth interviews and coding feedback into themes
      • Ethnographic sessions (field visits)
  • Deliver insights (2-4 weeks)
    • Transform research data into coherent, actionable, shareable recommendations
      • Synthesize various insights into informational hierarchies
      • Identify key themes and tensions
      • Tailor insights to meet the goals of the product and developer teams
      • Schedule follow-ups to track and document research contributions to UX improvements via Gitlab issues

Timeline influencers

  • International audience, add 3-5 weeks Translate materials, fine tune for cultural sensitivities, adjust for holidays, etc.
  • Niche customer segment, add 1-2 weeks Users are harder to find and connect and requires more time to recruit
  • Foundational research, add 1-2 months Multiple methods, fuzzy research signals, unknown areas, need for ethnography, analytics and high-fidelity research approaches

XFN Collaborator, how do I work with you?

  • Submit research requests with our intake form to start a "I Wish I Knew..." (IWIK) session
  • Provide feedback on user recruitment, research questions, next steps with insights, etc.
  • Attend live research online or in the field
  • Attend research share out sessions
  • Help socialize user insights by leveraging and using them in strategy docs, roadmaps, etc.

Guidlines for participating in research sessions

  • Be curious to learn about the user/tester and build rapport early
    • Brief explanation about your role at CVG
    • Share housekeeping info: confidentiality, privacy, comfort, etc.
  • Focus on questions that support the goals and objectives of the task/work
  • Dig into specific topics
  • Conclude with gratitude for their heart and intellect to help us improve UX of our products.
