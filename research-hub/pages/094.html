Current SUPR-Q Scorecard

This scorecard presents SUPR-Q (Standardized User Experience Percentile Rank Questionnaire) results for Compassion Causes, evaluating usability, trust, appearance, loyalty, and NPS (Net Promoter Score).

Overall Performance Summary

  • Usability & Task Completion: Significant improvements over research cycles, with usability reaching 86.2.
  • Trust & Transparency: Trust scores grew from 72% to 85%, indicating improved donor confidence.
  • Loyalty & Retention: Rising NPS and donor engagement metrics highlight strong long-term potential.
  • Mobile Experience Issues: SO Mobile still experiences high task completion time, requiring further optimization.

Research Recommendations

1️⃣ Prioritize Mobile Experience Enhancements

  • Insight: SO Mobile scores lowest in usability (75.71) and has the highest task time (10m 48s).
  • Recommendation: Conduct focused usability tests on mobile workflows to identify key bottlenecks in donation processing and navigation.

2️⃣ Strengthen Trust & Transparency Features

  • Insight: Trust scores grew to 85%, but SO Mobile lags at 69%.
  • Recommendation: Improve trust signals by implementing real-time impact tracking, clearer fund allocation reports, and more donor testimonials.

3️⃣ Boost Retention Through Personalization & Loyalty Programs

  • Insight: NPS has improved to 86%, showing potential for stronger donor engagement.
  • Recommendation: Develop personalized donor engagement strategies, such as a subscription-based giving model, impact dashboards, and donor milestone rewards.

4️⃣ Improve Cause Discovery & Navigation

  • Insight: Drop-off rates remain high in cause selection workflows.
  • Recommendation: Optimize search, filtering, and recommendation algorithms to enhance discoverability and engagement.

5️⃣ Address Donor Experience Gaps with A/B Testing

  • Insight: A/B tests indicate that clearer donation sliders and visual urgency cues improve conversion rates.
  • Recommendation: Iterate on donation UI elements and conduct further testing to validate optimal layout, CTAs, and incentive structures.

Final Thoughts

Tracking SUPR-Q trends over time provides critical insights for executive decision-making. By leveraging these findings, Compassion Causes leadership can make data-driven, high-impact product decisions while continuously refining the donor experience. 🚀
