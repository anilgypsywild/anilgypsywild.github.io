Lyssna Script: SCP-FCP Portal Concept Test

Project Name: SCP-FCP Pastor-to-Pastor Portal Concept Validation
Lyssna Study link: TBD...

Section A: Warm-Up & Context

Instructions to Moderator: Read aloud the script. Encourage open conversation.

  1. Welcome & context (read):

“Thank you for meeting with us today. We’re exploring a new tool from Compassion that helps two partner churches work together directly: sharing prayer requests, tracking missions, and building authentic relationships—not just transactions.
This is an early concept. We will show you screen-images and ask for feedback. There are no right or wrong answers—please be as honest as possible. We are recording this session for our research team. Is that okay with you?”

  1. Baseline questions:
    • Q1: “Can you describe how you currently stay connected with partner churches or pastors—locally or internationally?” Open text
    • Q2: “When someone in your church needs urgent prayer or support, how do you share that request today?” Open text
    • Q3: “Do you use platforms like Facebook, WhatsApp, or TikTok for your ministry work?”
      • “Which ones?” Open text
      • “Do you find them helpful or distracting? Why?” Open text

Section B: Screen-by-Screen Walkthrough

Screen 1: Dashboard Overview

Moderator shows screen-image.

Pre-explanation questions:

  1. “Just looking at this page, what do you think this tool is for?” Open text
  2. “Who do you think would use this page in your ministry?” Open text

Go ahead explanation:

“This page is the home dashboard for two partnered churches. It shows what you are working on together, allows you to request prayer, share updates, access resources, and view impact.”

Follow-up Likert items:

  • Q3: “How meaningful is what you see on this page for doing real ministry work in Jesus’ name?”
    • 1 = Not meaningful at all
    • 2 = Slightly meaningful
    • 3 = Moderately meaningful
    • 4 = Very meaningful
    • 5 = Extremely meaningful
      (Tag: [Meaningfulness])
  • Q4: “Could you see yourself opening this dashboard in your ministry work?”
    • 1 = No, never
    • 2 = Unlikely
    • 3 = Maybe
    • 4 = Likely
    • 5 = Yes, definitely
      (Tag: [Adoption Intention])

Screen 2: Pastor Profile

Moderator shows screen-image.

Pre-explanation questions:

  1. “Tell me in your own words what this screen is showing you.” Open text
  2. “Does this give you enough personal context about the other pastor? Or is something missing?” Open text

Go ahead explanation:

“The idea here is that you would see key information about your partner pastor: their vision, church context, what they’re working on. You won’t have to rely only on a relationship-manager intermediary.”

Follow-up Likert items:

  • Q3: “How personally connected do you feel to this other pastor after seeing this screen?”
    • 1 = Not connected at all
    • 2 = Slightly connected
    • 3 = Moderately connected
    • 4 = Very connected
    • 5 = Deeply connected
      (Tag: [Relational Trust])
  • Q4: “Would this level of information build trust between your church and the partner church?”
    • 1 = Not at all
    • 2 = A little
    • 3 = Somewhat
    • 4 = Quite a bit
    • 5 = Very much
      (Tag: [Relational Trust])

Screen 3: Shared Mission Focus

Moderator shows screen-image.

Pre-explanation questions:

  1. “When you read this, how clear is the shared mission between the two churches?”
    • Very clear / Somewhat clear / Not clear — Select & comment
  2. “How urgent or important does this feel to you personally?”
    • Very urgent / Somewhat urgent / Not urgent — Select & comment

Go ahead explanation:

“This screen shows the specific ministry focus your two churches will partner on — e.g., restoration of abused children and families. It defines 3–4 things to work on together.”

Follow-up Likert items:

  • Q3: “If this tool existed, would it change anything about how you pray, preach, or mobilize volunteers?”
    • 1 = Definitely not
    • 2 = Probably not
    • 3 = Maybe
    • 4 = Probably yes
    • 5 = Definitely yes
      (Tag: [Ministry Impact])
  • Q4: “How spiritually important is this page to you?”
    • 1 = Not spiritually important
    • 2 = Slightly spiritually important
    • 3 = Moderately spiritually important
    • 4 = Very spiritually important
    • 5 = Extremely spiritually important
      (Tag: [Spiritual Resonance])

Screen 4: Resource Exchange

Moderator shows screen-image.

Pre-explanation questions:

  1. “Do you understand what is being asked of you on this page?”
    • Yes / Somewhat / No — Select & comment
  2. “Would your church leadership know how to respond to this page?”
    • We could respond ourselves
    • We would need staff/missions team
    • We would not know what to do — Select & comment
  3. “Do you feel overwhelmed by the number of requests or is it reasonable?”
    • Overwhelmed / Reasonable / Too light — Select & comment

Go ahead explanation:

“This page shows what the partner church (FCP) already has (e.g., building, children’s capacity, Bible‐study framework) and what they are inviting the sponsoring church (SCP) to help with.”

Follow-up Likert items (TLX light):

  • Q4: “How mentally difficult would it be for you to act on this page in real life?”
    • 1 = Very easy
    • 2 = Easy
    • 3 = Medium
    • 4 = Hard
    • 5 = Very hard
      (Tag: [Cognitive Load])
  • Q5: “How frustrating would it feel to you to work through this page?”
    • 1 = Not frustrating at all
    • 2 = Slightly frustrating
    • 3 = Neutral
    • 4 = Quite frustrating
    • 5 = Very frustrating
      (Tag: [Frustration])

Screen 5: Prayer Request Flow

Moderator shows screen-image.

Pre-explanation questions:

  1. “In your own words, what can you do here?” Open text

Go ahead explanation:

“This page is for making or receiving prayer requests between partner churches, marking status (pending, in progress, done), and optionally announcing the request in your congregation.”

Follow-up Likert items:

  • Q2: “How spiritually powerful is this feature for you personally?”
    • 1 = Not powerful
    • 2 = Slightly powerful
    • 3 = Moderately powerful
    • 4 = Very powerful
    • 5 = Extremely powerful
      (Tag: [Spiritual Resonance])
  • Q3: “Would you actually use this in your ministry in the next six months if it were real?”
    • 1 = Definitely not
    • 2 = Probably not
    • 3 = Maybe
    • 4 = Probably yes
    • 5 = Definitely yes
      (Tag: [Adoption Intention])
  • Q4: “Where would you currently do something like this?” Multiple choice
    • Facebook group
    • WhatsApp
    • Text message
    • Phone call
    • We cannot do this easily now
    • Other: ______
  • Q5: “Do Facebook, TikTok or Instagram feel like the right place for this kind of request?”
    • Yes / No — Select & comment why or why not
      (Tag: [Platform Fit])

Screen 6: Impact Dashboard

Moderator shows screen-image.

Pre-explanation questions:

  1. “Tell me what this dashboard is telling you in plain words.” Open text
  2. “Would this information help you report back to your church leadership or congregation?”
    • Yes / Maybe / No — Select & comment
    • “How would you use it? (e.g., sermon, newsletter, missions report)?” Open text

Follow-explanation:

“This dashboard tracks impact: number of children reached, progress updates, testimonies, resources used. It gives both partner churches and Compassion visibility in near real-time.”

Follow-up questions:

  • Q3: “How often do you think you would look at this dashboard if it were real?”
    • 1 = Daily
    • 2 = A few times each week
    • 3 = Once a week
    • 4 = Once a month
    • 5 = Rarely or never
      (Tag: [Adoption Frequency])
  • Q4: “Does seeing this help you feel that Compassion is being a good steward of ministry resources?”
    • 1 = Strong no
    • 2 = No
    • 3 = Somewhat
    • 4 = Yes
    • 5 = Strong yes
      (Tag: [Stewardship Perception])

Section C: Closing Questions

  1. “Compared to platforms like Facebook, TikTok, or GoFundMe – how would you describe the feeling of using this tool?”
    • More peaceful
    • About the same
    • More chaotic
      Follow-up: Why do you say that? Open text
      (Tag: [Emotional Comparison])
  2. “Does this tool help you live out your calling in Christ?”
    • 1 = Strong no
    • 2 = No
    • 3 = Somewhat
    • 4 = Yes
    • 5 = Strong yes
      (Tag: [Spiritual Alignment])

  3. “Would you recommend Compassion invest money to build this?”
    • Build it as is

  4. “In one word, how would you describe this experience?” Open text
    (Tag: [One Word Summary])

Section D: Demographics & Context

At end (optional):

  • Your role in your church (pastor, staff, missions, volunteer)
  • Size of your church (members / attendance)
  • Church location / country
  • Number of partner churches you currently work with
  • Are you a Compassion Partner church? (Yes/No)
  • What technologies you use for ministry today (list)

Section E: Thanks & Next Steps

“Thank you for taking time today. Your feedback will shape how Compassion proceeds with this tool, and how we build it to serve pastors and partner churches in line with Christ’s calling. If you think now of anything else you wish you said, please just email me and we’ll incorporate it.”

End recording

Additional Notes to Moderator:

  • After each Likert item, ask “Why did you choose that number?” to capture verbal rationale useful for qualitative thematic coding.
  • Record any spontaneously mentioned emotional words: e.g., “felt peaceful,” “felt disconnected,” “felt empowered,” etc. These will give us the ‘emotional resonance’ quotes.
  • Watch for signs of fatigue: if participant passive, move to closing.

Appendix

A1. Likert Scale Definitions (for clarity in instrument):

  1. Meaningfulness (1–5): 1 = Not meaningful at all; …; 5 = Extremely meaningful
  2. Adoption Intention (1–5): 1 = No, never; …; 5 = Yes, definitely
  3. Relational Trust (1–5): 1 = Not connected at all / Not at all; …; 5 = Very connected / Very much
  4. Spiritual Resonance / Spiritual Importance (1–5): analogous
  5. Cognitive Load & Frustration (1–5): scaled so that higher numbers = more difficult / more frustrated
  6. Adoption Frequency (1–5): 1 = Daily … 5 = Rarely or never
  7. Stewardship Perception (1–5): 1 = Strong no … 5 = Strong yes
  8. Spiritual Alignment (1–5): 1 = Strong no … 5 = Strong yes
