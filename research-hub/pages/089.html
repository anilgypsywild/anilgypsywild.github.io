📓 What MaxDiff Is

MaxDiff also called Best Worst Scaling is a quantitative method where respondents repeatedly select the most and least important items from sets, forcing tradeoffs instead of allowing inflated ratings.

It produces stable, rank ordered preference utilities that quantify true motivational weight and reduce scale bias.

[IV] AI Assisted Workflow Evaluation Program

SUS, NASA TLX, and Capacity Signals

Timeframe: June 2025 to January 2026

Primary Studies
• Guatemala FCP Usability and Workload Study June 2025: [reference omitted]
• Thailand AI Assisted Workflow Study October 2025: [reference omitted]
• FCP Capacity, Confidence, and Digital Self Efficacy January 2026: [reference omitted]

Why NASA TLX Was Critical

  • System Usability Scale measures perceived usability. It does not measure cognitive strain.
  • NASA Task Load Index measures mental demand, effort, and perceived workload.
  • In AI assisted workflows where guidance, rewriting, and interpretation occur, cognitive load is the primary behavioral risk signal.

Reference: [reference omitted]

Study 1

Guatemala FCP Usability and Workload

June 2025

Design

40 plus participants
Task based moderated testing
Measured SUS and NASA TLX in-situ

Quantitative Results

Top Performing Prototypes

Flow SUS TLX Interpretation
AI ChatBot Prototype 86.4 to 87.5 30 to 33 High usability, low mental burden
Cause Upload Prototype 87.5 30 Guided flow reduces effort

Underperforming Flows

Flow SUS TLX Signal
Original Learning Below 75 Above 60 High friction and overload
Foundations Baseline Low 70s Above 60 Cognitive fatigue risk

Key Insight

Some flows showed good SUS but elevated TLX around 52 to 60.
Meaning usability alone masked cognitive strain.

🔥 This established a core principle: SUS above 80 is insufficient if TLX remains above 50.

Qualitative Themes

From aligned feedback in the report

• Lower anxiety in AI assisted flows
• Higher confidence in guided structures
• Confusion and fatigue in legacy Learning modules
• Perceived burden from disconnected steps

Contribution of Study 1

•Introduceddual metric requirement SUS plus TLX
• Demonstrated AI can reduce workload when guidance is structured
• Identified cognitive load as primary blocker in Cause creation
• Set target benchmark SUS greater than 85 and TLX less than 40

This became the baseline for future AI workflow evaluation.

Study 2

Thailand AI Assisted Workflow

October 2025

  • This study directly builds on Guatemala.
  • Guatemala established that workload reduction is measurable and critical.
  • Thailand formalized TLX as required metric in validation framework.

Baseline Indicators Prior to Intervention

From research brief:

Metric Baseline
CSAT 5.5 to 6 out of 7
NASA TLX Mental Demand 5 out of 7
Confidence 4 out of 7
NPS Below plus 20

Signal
Moderate satisfaction but elevated mental demand and low advocacy.

Measurement Framework Shift

Thailand explicitly mapped:

• UMUX Lite for usability and usefulness
• NASA TLX for workload
• Confidence rating for independence
• NPS for adoption intent
• Emotional tone for Delight

🔥 Critical contribution

  • Workload reduction threshold defined as at least 1 point TLX reduction.
  • Usability improvement threshold defined as 10 to 15 percent.

Trust and AI Specific Risk Identified

Qualitative findings

• AI improves speed but does not automatically increase trust
• Users fear AI language may not reflect authentic voice
• Risk of complexity disguised as simplicity

This reframes AI evaluation: AI must reduce workload without eroding authenticity.

🏅 Operational KPI Mapping

Thailand connected TLX and usability directly to Frontline KPIs

UX Metric Operational Translation
TLX reduction Higher timeliness and lower rework
UMUX improvement Improved submission quality
Confidence increase Greater independence
NPS increase Adoption and reuse

This moved TLX from research metric to operational decision signal.

This study builds on both prior studies.

  • This study examines perceived capability and empowerment longitudinally.

Conceptual Clarification

🔥 KPIs are not usability metrics.
They measure digital self efficacy and agency.

Suggested: Retrospective pre/post method used to mitigate response shift bias

Quantitative Instrument

Scale 1 to 7

Measured

• Confidence using phone or computer
• Ease of filling forms
• Understanding sustainability
• Community sharing confidence

Interpretation guidance from report

• Scores above 5 indicate positive change
• Distribution more meaningful than raw mean
• Do not numerically compare to SUS or TLX

[V] PM Onboarding to Research Practice

Standardized Collaboration Framework for Research Practices


Initial Implementation: Causes PM Onboarding Workspace
OKR Category: Cross Functional Collaboration and Research Governance

Executive Purpose

This onboarding document formalized how Product Managers collaborate with Research in practice.

It was designed to:

• Establish structured research governance
• Prevent last minute or unscoped research requests
• Tie research explicitly to roadmap milestones
• Improve collaboration maturity between Product and Research
• Create leadership visible traceability for research impact

This framework is reusable for any PM onboarding across Supporter Product.

Structural Improvements Introduced

1. Formal Research Governance Model

Explicit principle documented:

If a request is not tied to a product milestone, engineering dependency, revenue impact, or trust metric, it does not enter the research queue

Impact:

• Protected research bandwidth
• Reduced ad hoc exploratory asks
• Created measurable prioritization logic

2. Mandatory Research Intake Requirement

All research requests must use the Research Intake Form

This ensures:

• Defined product question
• Defined decision owner
• Defined timeline
• Defined success metric

Contribution:

Moved collaboration from informal conversation to scoped commitment.

3. Decision Based Framing

Before initiating research, PM must define:

• What decision will be made
• When that decision must occur
• Who executes the output
• What measurable success looks like

Contribution:

Eliminated research as curiosity or urgency driven reaction.
Shifted collaboration to decision support model.

4. GitLab as System of Record

Codified GitLab as collaboration archive

Purpose:

• Prevent context loss
• Maintain audit trail
• Support quarterly reporting
• Enable leadership visibility

Contribution:

Institutionalized transparency across quarters.

Embedded Research Orientation Delivered to PM

The onboarding document also included a structured research overview covering:

• Causes usability benchmarks including SUS, CSAT, NPS
• Persona and P2P sandbox quantitative survey
• Trust and transparency findings
• Generational behavioral differences
• Known friction points in navigation and labeling
• Roadmap aligned future studies

This reduced redundant questioning and provided immediate evidence base.

Collaboration Guardrails Defined

Research Requires Lead Time

Documented that:

• Usability and surveys require planning
• Mixed methods require structured design
• Urgent requests dilute quality

Contribution:

Set expectation boundary around research velocity.

Milestone Alignment Template Added

Template required:

• Feature or concept
• Decision needed
• Target date
• Dependencies
• Expected impact
• Intake submitted yes or no

Contribution:

Operationalized milestone to research mapping.

This framework now serves as:

• A reusable onboarding template for any new PM

It converts collaboration from reactive to structured.
