$ Executive Summary

  1. Delight: Both generations report positive emotions; Next-Gen especially values visual feedback and easy sharing.
  2. Doability: Near-universal task completion and low error rates. Advanced features are discoverable but could be surfaced earlier.
  3. Desire: Next-Gen shows slightly stronger intent to return and recommend, but passives remain an opportunity for growth.
  4. Dependability: Trust and perceived clarity are strong, but can improve with more visual proof and personalized follow-up.

Tldr;

Compassion Causes delivers a robust and emotionally rewarding user experience for both segments, with clear opportunities to deepen trust, foster advocacy, and refine advanced features for future engagement.
