🚧 Draft Script: Live-Events Concept Experience Validation Research

🎯 Purpose & Context

The Live Events Research study aims to evaluate the redesigned Compassion + For King & Country (FK&C) concert experience through CVG's 4D UX lens: Doability, Dependability, Delight, and Desire.

Specifically, this moderated interview study will assess how concert-goers respond emotionally, behaviorally, and spiritually to different moments within the sponsorship journey from pre-concert exposure to post-concert engagement.

This effort supports the strategic goals of Compassion’s Live Events and Supporter Engagement teams by:

  • Validating core hypotheses about sponsorship motivation and digital interaction efficacy
  • Understanding audience reactions to exclusive content and sponsorship messaging
  • Measuring trust, clarity, emotional resonance, and perceived value across the experience

Findings from these interviews will directly inform design decisions, content prioritization, and concept refinements before scaling the FK&C sponsorship experience to wider audiences.

Method: 1:1 Moderated Interviews
Sample Size: 8–12 participants
Duration: 45–60 minutes per session

🎬 Section 1: Pre-Concert Digital Engagement

Moment / Topic Courtney’s Version Suggested Version Rationale
Artist Perks “What types of content would you be most interested in receiving?” “What kinds of exclusive content would be meaningful enough to motivate you to sponsor a child?” Aligns directly with H2 (perks increase urgency) and sharpens behavioral focus.
Giveaway Email Not addressed directly “Does receiving a giveaway or email follow-up increase your interest or trust in the artist or organization?” Explores emotional priming and builds context for later decision points.
Concert Ticket Purchase Narrative only, no questions “What role, if any, did FK&C’s cause-related content play in your decision to attend the concert?” Ties initial engagement to motivation and early emotional triggers.

🎤 Section 2: In-Concert Experience

Moment / Topic Courtney’s Version Suggested Version Rationale
Digital vs. Hand-Raise “Which would more likely move you to action?” “Which method felt easiest, most emotional, or most persuasive? Why?” Expands H1 from convenience to emotional resonance; adds cognitive unpacking.
Pitch Emotional Impact “How does the pitch moment make you feel?” “Was the pitch inspiring, neutral, or uncomfortable? What caused those feelings?” Addresses H4, ensures clarity around guilt vs. inspiration.
Virtual Child Moment “What thoughts are going through your head?” “Did the child connection moment feel powerful or performative? Why?” More clearly addresses H3 – perception of exploitation vs. trust.
Sponsorship Action “Do the perks increase urgency or are they the sole reason you sponsor?” “Did you sponsor mainly because of the emotional moment, perks, or other reasons?” Distinguishes emotional vs. transactional motivation (ties to H2).

🗒️ Section 4: After Journey, Summary & Debrief Questions

Topic Courtney’s Version Suggested Version Rationale
Rank Moments “Rank from highest satisfaction to least” “Rank by emotional impact, likelihood to share, and clarity of message” Multi-dimensional ranking improves thematic synthesis.
Brand Awareness “Assess brand awareness of Compassion” “What did you believe about Compassion before and after? Did your perception shift?” Promotes richer before-after delta, essential for H4.
Additive Experience “Does pitch moment add value?” “Did this pitch elevate or interrupt your concert experience? Why?” Directly evaluates revised H4 (additive vs. ad).
Artist Connection “Do you feel more connected to FK&C?” “Did this experience deepen your trust or emotional connection with the band?” Supports H5, especially regarding perceived authenticity.
Sponsorship Understanding “Assess understanding before and after” “What’s your understanding now of what sponsoring a child through Compassion means?” Plain language encourages accurate recall and confirms H4 learning outcomes.
Distinction “What might make Compassion distinct?” “Which moment felt uniquely Compassion vs. something another org might do?” Behavioral + brand differentiation insight.
Open Feedback “What parts did you not like or didn’t make much difference?” Add: “What felt surprising or unexpectedly powerful?” w does that make you feel?

📎 Appendix A: Hypotheses Being Tested

Hypothesis ID

Statement

4D UX Pillar(s)
H1 The digital pitch experience (QR/text) is perceived as more convenient and emotionally resonant than the traditional hand-raise moment. Doability, Delight
H2 Offering fan perks (e.g., exclusive content) meaningfully increases urgency to sponsor a child. Desire, Delight
H3 A virtual sponsor-child connection moment is emotionally powerful and builds trust, not perceived as exploitative. Dependability, Delight
H4 The redesigned pitch moment increases understanding of child sponsorship and distinguishes Compassion from other organizations. Dependability, Delight

H4 (Revised)

The pitch enhances the concert experience, even for those who do not sponsor.

The redesigned pitch moment increases understanding of child sponsorship and distinguishes Compassion from other organizations

Delight, Dependability
H5 Concert-goers feel a stronger bond to their favorite artist when they see them support child sponsorship efforts. Dependability, Desire
H6 Access to exclusive artist content is desirable enough that concert-goers may take meaningful action (e.g., sponsor a child) to gain access. Desire
