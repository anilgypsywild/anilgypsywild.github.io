NPS Score Analysis [W.I.p]

Example Case-Study

We conducted an NPS survey to assess customer satisfaction and loyalty for Product-Fiction. NPS is a widely used metric to assess how likely customers are to recommend a product, providing valuable insights into customer sentiment and loyalty.

Objectives

  • Analyze NPS Scores: Categorize customer responses into Promoters, Passives, and Detractors.
  • Inform Improvements: Use insights to drive product and service improvements and better meet customer needs.

NPS Survey Data

Survey responses were on a scale of 0-10, yielding the following raw scores:
3, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 9, 9, 9, 9, 9, 9, 10, 10, 10, 10, 9, 9, 9, 9, 9, 10, 10, 9, 10.

NPS Scoring

Step 1: Classifying Responses

NPS divides customers into three categories

  • Promoters (9-10): Enthusiastic customers likely to recommend the product.
  • Passives (7-8): Satisfied but unenthusiastic customers.
  • Detractors (0-6): Unhappy customers who may harm the brand's reputation.
Step 2: Categorizing Raw Scores
  • Promoters (9-10): 19 responses (9: 10, 10: 9)
  • Passives (7-8): 6 responses (7: 6)
  • Detractors (0-6): 7 responses (3, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 6)
  • Promoters: 59%, Detractors: 22%; Passives: 19%
Step 3: Calculating NPS

The NPS score is calculated as follows:

NPS = ( no. of promoters/ total no. of responses x 100) - (no. of detractors/ total no. of responses x 100)

Plugging in our values:

  • Total Responses = 32
  • Promoters = 19; Passives = 6; Detractors = 7
  • = (19/32 x 100) - (7/32 x 100)
  • = 59.38 - 21.88;
  • = 37.5

Interpretation

  • Promoters: 59%, Detractors: 22%; Passives: 19%
  • NPS Score: 37.5 (Positive)
  • Indicates that Promoters outnumber Detractors, signaling good customer satisfaction and potential loyalty. While positive, the 22% detractors suggest areas for improvement.

Insights

  • Strengths: A majority of Promoters (59%) indicates strong product satisfaction and growth potential.
  • Opportunities for Improvement: Focus on Detractors (22%) to identify pain points and resolve issues, potentially transforming detractors into promoters.

Next Steps

  1. Follow-up with Detractors: Conduct surveys or interviews to identify the root causes of dissatisfaction.
  2. Product Improvement: Prioritize changes based on feedback to address detractor concerns.
  3. Monitor NPS Trends: Track NPS over time to measure the effectiveness of improvements and customer sentiment.

Executive Summary

An NPS of 37.5, with 59% Promoters, reflects strong customer support and satisfaction but also highlights areas for growth. By focusing on the 22% detractor feedback, we can enhance the product, reduce negative sentiment, and potentially increase the NPS over time.
