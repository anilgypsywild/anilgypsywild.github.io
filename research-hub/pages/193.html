Qualitative Themes

1. Trust-Related Themes

  • High trust in app performance:

    • “The app is transparent and easy to use, showing what we’re doing to donors.” – GH0347
    • “Uploading videos and photos increases donor confidence and trust in our work.” – GH0121

  • Reliability of communication tools:

    • “The app helps us quickly connect with supporters; I feel confident using it.” – GH0347

2. Positive Feedback

  • Ease of use and value:
    • “It’s user-friendly and allows us to showcase impact transparently.” – GH0347
    • “The app is a great way to interact directly with supporters.” – GH0121

3. Negative Feedback

  • Notifications missing:

    • “Scrolling to find comments wastes time. Notifications would make it faster.” – GH0121

  • High data consumption:

    • “The app uses too much data compared to other platforms.” – GH0347

Actionable UX Recommendations

  1. Implement Notifications:

    • Real-time notifications for comments, approvals, and updates.

  2. Reduce Data Usage:

    • Introduce a low-data mode and optimize media uploads.

  3. Batch Upload Media:

    • Allow batch uploads for photos and videos.

  4. Streamline Moderation:

    • Speed up the approval process for submitted posts.

Qualitative Themes

1. Trust-Related Themes

  • General trust but with connectivity concerns:
    • “I trust the app’s performance when the network is strong.” – GH0463
    • “The app makes work easy, but failed uploads are frustrating.” – GH0346

2. Positive Feedback

  • Improves communication and visibility:
    • “It helps us share our activities with supporters and learn from others.” – GH0346

3. Negative Feedback

  • Connectivity and failed uploads:

    • “Uploading videos often fails when the network is poor.” – GH0463

  • Moderation delays:

    • “Waiting for approval takes too long, slowing our work.” – GH0346

Actionable UX Recommendations

  1. Add Offline Features:

    • Enable offline drafts for posts and uploads.

  2. Optimize Upload Workflows:

    • Compress video uploads and add retry mechanisms.

  3. Improve Moderation Speeds:

    • Automate approvals where possible.

  4. Network Optimization:

    • Ensure the app works smoothly in low-bandwidth environments.

Actionable UX Recommendations

  1. Develop Role-Specific Reporting Tools:

    • Tailor features for financial and impact reporting for Accountants.

  2. Improve App Usability:

    • Enhance navigation and reduce the “official” feel to encourage engagement.

  3. Add More Engagement Features:

    • Consider introducing messaging, live feedback, and multimedia tools.

  4. Low-Data Enhancements:

    • Add options for low-data consumption similar to other roles.

Conclusion

  • The analysis reveals that Project Directors/Coordinators exhibit the highest satisfaction and engagement
  • Health Workers and Accountants face usability challenges.
  • Prioritizing offline functionality, notifications, improved uploads, and role-specific features will address core pain points and enhance adoption across roles.

Executive Summary

The section summarizes role-based user satisfaction, engagement, and usability of the Community App Beta. It highlights strengths, identifies pain points, and provides actionable recommendations for improving the app experience.

Key Insights

1. Project Directors/Coordinators (Role 1)

  • Strengths: - Highest satisfaction (6.5/7) and trust levels.
    - Strong NPS (+70) driven by app transparency and donor engagement.
    • Most engaged users with consistent uploads, reactions, and comments.
    • Pain Points:
      • High data consumption for uploads.
    • Key Quote:
      “Uploading photos and videos builds donor confidence. The app is a game changer.” – GH0121

2. Project Health Workers (Role 2)

  • Strengths:
    • Good satisfaction (6.2/7) and moderate trust (5.8/7).
    • Active engagement despite upload issues (3 uploads on average).
  • Pain Points: - Failed uploads due to poor network connectivity.
    • Usability improvements needed (SUS: 50.0).
    • Key Quote:
      “Failed uploads are frustrating. Offline options would help a lot.” – GH0463

3. Project Accountants (Role 3)

  • Strengths:
    • Moderate satisfaction (5.8/7) with app alignment to mission.
  • Pain Points:
    • Lowest engagement levels across all roles.
    • Usability challenges and lack of specific features for reporting (SUS: 52.5).
  • Key Quote:
    “It feels too official. Adding lively features would encourage us to engage more.” – GH0987

Actionable UX Recommendations

1. Optimize Data Usage and Offline Functionality

  • Introduce a low-data mode and offline retry options for uploads to improve accessibility for Roles 2 and 3.

2. Real-Time Notifications

  • Implement notifications for comments, uploads, and approvals to drive engagement and responsiveness.

3. Role-Specific Enhancements

  • Health Workers (Role 2):
    • Focus on improving upload success rates and offline workflows.
  • Accountants (Role 3):
    • Add financial reporting tools and more engaging, user-friendly features.

4. Improve Usability

  • Streamline navigation, simplify workflows, and enhance onboarding to increase SUS scores across all roles.

Summary Snapshot

Focus Area Key Findings Recommendations
Satisfaction High for Role 1, moderate for others. Target usability and trust improvements.
Engagement Role 1 leads; Role 3 needs stronger support. Add role-specific tools to encourage usage.
Trust & Usability Lower trust in Roles 2 & 3 due to failures. Address network, offline, and SUS pain points.
