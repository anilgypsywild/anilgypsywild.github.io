How Research Answers [name omitted]’s Beta Launch Questions

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Will the Supporters Use the Product Regularly (Adoption)?

✅ How We Answer This:

  • Tracking Recurring Logins & Session Frequency using Mixpanel Events:
    • Session_Start, Dashboard_Viewed, Content_Interacted, Return_Visit
  • Comparing active users vs. one-time users to identify retention trends.
  • Time-on-platform analysis → How long do users spend per session?
  • Engagement thresholds → % of users returning after first session.

📊 Deliverable: Monthly adoption report with % of repeat users and retention curve.

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Establish Baseline Measures of Supporter Engagement

✅ How We Answer This:
We capture ALL engagement behaviors using Mixpanel event tracking:

Engagement Metric Tracked By
Views per post Post_Viewed count
Reactions (likes, emoji responses) Reaction_Clicked
Comments & Replies Comment_Posted, Reply_Posted
Downloads Download_Clicked
Shares Share_Clicked, Share_Completed
% Notifications Clicked Notification_Received, Notification_Clicked

📊 Deliverable: Engagement dashboard with real-time data visualization.

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Establish Baseline for “Time to Value” (Engagement Speed)

✅ How We Answer This:

  • Tracking “First Engagement” after Login
    • Time from First Login → First Reaction, Comment, or Share
  • Segmenting by Content Type
    • Which content leads to fastest engagement?
  • Comparing New vs. Returning Users
    • Are first-time users engaging faster/slower than expected?

📊 Deliverable: Time-to-Value heatmap showing engagement speed across content types.

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Which Types of Content Drive Engagement & Satisfaction?

✅ How We Answer This:

  • Content Performance Tracking:
    • Engagement Rate = (Reactions + Comments + Shares) / Views
    • Sorting by Content Type: Stories, Impact Reports, Data-Driven Metrics, Videos, Testimonials.
  • Post-Engagement Feedback:
    • “What type of content do you find most compelling?” (Multiple Choice)
    • “Why did you engage with this content?” (Short Answer)

📊 Deliverable: Engagement leaderboard → Top-performing content & insights.

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Determine CSAT (Customer Satisfaction)

✅ How We Answer This:

  • CSAT Survey (Likert Scale, 1-7)
    • Overall Satisfaction
    • Product Usability (SUS Score)
    • Content Satisfaction → Stories, Evidence of Impact.
    • Quality of Communications → Translations, Readability.
  • Behavioral Validation
    • Do high-CSAT users engage more?

📊 Deliverable: CSAT Report with correlations between satisfaction & behavior.

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Attitudinal Insights (Trust, Impact, Connection)

✅ How We Answer This:
We collect structured attitudinal data via short Likert surveys & open text feedback:

Attitude Metric Survey Question & Data Collection
Feeling of Connection "Do you feel connected to the community?" (1-7 scale)
Content Timeliness "Does content arrive at the right time?" (1-7 scale)
Perceived Impact "Do you feel your donations make an impact?" (1-7 scale)
Satisfaction in Contributions "Do you feel fulfilled in supporting this cause?" (1-7 scale)
Trust in Donations "Do you trust your donations are used wisely?" (1-7 scale)

📊 Deliverable: Trust & Connection Index, Cross-referenced with Engagement Metrics.

🔑 [name omitted]’s Question: Correlation Between CSAT & Engagement

✅ How We Answer This:

  • Statistical Analysis:
    • Spearman Correlation between CSAT scores & Engagement Levels (Views, Reactions, Comments).
    • Regression Model to predict likelihood of high engagement based on CSAT.
  • Engagement Clustering:
    • High CSAT, Low Engagement? → Platform friction.
    • Low CSAT, High Engagement? → Content is engaging but product UX needs improvement.

📊 Deliverable: Predictive Model → CSAT-Engagement Trends Over Time.
