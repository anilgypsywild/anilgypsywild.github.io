Closed Card Sort Specification

Outcome Based Cause Categorization
Phase 2 Validation ([name omitted] and [name omitted] Request)

I. Study Purpose

Validate whether supporters can consistently categorize causes into five predefined outcome level buckets requested by Product and Design. This study tests category clarity and mental model alignment under constraint, not category discovery.

Category Hypotheses (Fixed)

These five categories are explicitly provided by Product and Design and represent testable hypotheses loosely derived from the open card sort.

Category Working Definition
Education Supports learning, literacy, and school environments
Training & Skill Building Builds practical or vocational skills
Income Generation Enables earning power or economic self sufficiency
Safety Protects children and communities from harm
Basic Needs Meets essential needs for health, survival, and dignity

These categories are intentionally outcome focused and mutually exclusive for testing purposes.

II. Lyssna Setup

Participant Instructions (Lyssna)

Instruction Text

You will see a list of causes that support children and communities living in extreme poverty. For each cause, select the category that best represents the primary outcome or benefit it provides. There are no right or wrong answers. Choose the category that makes the most sense to you.

Category Description Prompt ([name omitted]'s Request)

After completing the card sort, participants will see five short prompts, one per category.

Prompt Template

“For causes in the [Category Name] category, how do you think children benefit from these causes?”

Response constraint: 10–15 words
Type: Short text response

This captures participant authored outcome language to inform final platform labels.
