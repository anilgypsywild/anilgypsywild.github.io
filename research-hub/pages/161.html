Research article

Objective:

Conduct a quick large sample test on UserZoom to better understand how users perceive/interact with the Impact Timeline section, and identify their preference between Horizontal and Vertical low-fidelity design prototypes.

Recommendation (post-test): Vertical Version

Participants:

  • Sample size: 50 (statistically significant)
  • Recruitment: Target users based on the following screener on UserZoom
    • Screener: Participants must be Christian and must have donated at least once to a charitable cause.

Methodology:

  • AB-Test of Design Prototypes Feedback on UserZoom

Procedure

1. Pre-Test Survey (1 min):

  • Ask about participants’ familiarity with Compassion’s platforms and apps.

2. Usability Test (A/B Testing):

Scenario Context:

Imagine you are using the Compassion Community app to stay updated on the real-world impact of your $25,000 annual donation. The app provides a clear and meaningful way to engage with the church partners and communities you are supporting. You are being asked to interact with two different timeline designs (vertical and horizontal) to help us determine which one works best.

  • Tasks Explore the timeline to locate specific updates (e.g., “12 youth baptized on Dec 17,” “87 families had better harvest results on Dec 13”).
    • Task 1: Please click around on the section of the timeline that provides an update about the "12 youth who were baptized.
    • Task 2: Please click around on the section of the timeline that provides an update about the "87 families who have better harvest results
A/B Preference Comparison:
  • Ask participants directly:
    • Which timeline layout do you prefer overall: vertical or horizontal? Why?
    • Which layout feels more intuitive for finding and understanding updates?

3. Post-Test Survey

  • For each prototype:
    • UMUX-Lite: "The app meets my needs effectively" (agree/disagree, 7-point scale).
  • A/B Comparison:
    • Explicit preference for vertical vs. horizontal.
    • Suggestions for improvement or additional features.

Quant + Qual Research

Usability Metrics + Why?

  1. UMUX-Lite (7-point scale):
    • "The timeline is easy to use."
    • "The timeline meets my needs effectively."
  2. Preference Ranking:
    • After testing both layouts, ask participants to rank them in terms of usability
  3. The Why? for the preference rating

Research Insights

Horizontal Version HeatMap Vertical Version HeatMap

Key Findings

1. Overall Preference and Usability

  • 85% of participants prefer the Vertical Timeline, while only 15% prefer the Horizontal Timeline.
    • Chi-Squared Test: χ2 = 14.16, p = 0.00017, df = 1; Conclusion: Statistically significant preference for the Vertical Timeline (p < 0.01).
  • Vertical Timeline SUS Score: 103.1 (Exceptional usability) is statistically significantly higher than Horizontal Timeline SUS Score: 67.0 (Below average usability benchmark of 68)
  • Vertical Timelines is stat. sig. easier to use than the Horizontal timeline
    • Ease of Use Question Responses
      • Vertical: [36 (5), 14 (4), 2 (3), 1 (2), 0 (1)]; Horizontal: [9 (5), 9 (4), 20 (3), 8 (2), 6 (1)]
      • Statistical Test Results: χ2 = 42.88, p < 0.001, df = 4

2. Heatmap Analysis

  • Vertical Timeline: Clear interaction patterns around target areas (e.g., "87 families have better harvest results") with minimal wasted clicks.
  • Horizontal Timeline: Dispersed clicks, suggesting confusion.

3. Qualitative Themes

Top Quotes

Here are the top 3 quotes that clearly highlight the preference for the Vertical Timeline over the Horizontal Timeline:

  1. "I prefer the vertical design because it allows me to view more information at a go compared to the horizontal design."
    • Highlights the efficiency of the vertical timeline in presenting more data without requiring additional navigation.
  2. "It’s extremely easy to understand in a format that flows from one to the next."
    • Emphasizes the natural flow and intuitive structure of the vertical timeline, making it easier to follow.
  3. "You can see more information on the screen without having to scroll."
    • Demonstrates the advantage of the vertical timeline in minimizing effort by presenting key information upfront.

Frequencies of Themes by the top quotes

To provide quantitative backing for decision-makers.

  1. Efficiency and Information Density:
    • Frequency: 15+ mentions
    • Representative Quote: "I prefer the vertical design because it allows me to view more information at a go compared to the horizontal design."
  2. Flow and Intuitive Design:
    • Frequency: 10+ mentions
    • Representative Quote: "It’s extremely easy to understand in a format that flows from one to the next."
  3. Reduced Interaction Effort:
    • Frequency: 8+ mentions
    • Representative Quote: "You can see more information on the screen without having to scroll."

These numbers highlight how frequently users articulated these themes, making the case for prioritizing these aspects in the vertical timeline design.

Recommendation: Go with Vertical Timeline

The Vertical Timeline outperforms the Horizontal Timeline in every measurable aspect—preference, usability, and immediate adoption is recommended. The user quotes capture the primary advantages of the vertical timeline: efficiency, intuitive design, and reduced interaction effort.
