Research Brief

Causes Intercept Survey Study

Objective: Intercept Survey Study to Increase Trust and Confirmation Satisfaction for Current Gen Donors in Causes Donation Workflow
Audience: [name omitted] (Product Owner), [name omitted] (Design), David Shareer (Engineering), [name omitted] (UX Director)

1. Research Questions

What is the problem we are trying to solve


Benchmark data show that Current Gen donors finish donations easily (Q1: 83% “very easy”) yet are less confident that their gift is used as described and less satisfied with confirmation.

  • Q2: Current Gen mean confidence = 5.4 with 60% high confidence; 21% low confidence. Next Gen mean confidence = 6.2 with only 2% low.
  • Q10: Current Gen mean confirmation satisfaction = 4.1 with 29% high satisfaction; Next Gen = 4.6 with 42% high.

Why solve this problem now

  • Current Gen donors give larger gifts (median $35; 40% donate $50+). Even small trust gains can yield outsized revenue impact.
  • NPS for Current Gen is 2, compared to 8 for Next Gen. Closing the trust and confirmation gap can shift detractors into promoters.
  • Aligns with CVG’s agile research culture: rapid intercept testing will surface trust drivers in time for end-of-year giving campaigns.

What do we know about the problem

  • Discovery is efficient and direct.
  • Primary motivators for Current Gen: transparency (77%), faith alignment (60%), personal connection (64%).
  • Confirmation and follow-up are perceived as impersonal and transactional.
  • Current Gen willingness to pay higher amounts is conditional on trust signals being clear and confirmation feeling meaningful.

2. Research Goals and Metrics

Goals

  1. Use Lyssna intercept surveys inside Causes to measure in-the-moment motivators and barriers for Current Gen donors.
  2. Validate interventions such as allocation indicators, testimonials, and real-time banners at CTA.
  3. Validate confirmation redesign (personalized thank-you, impact cues, follow-up promise).
  4. Link intercept data to behavioral events in Google Analytics for triangulated evidence.
  5. Frame insights and metrics in CVG’s 4D UX Scorecard and align activities with Strawman UCD requirements.

4D UX Scorecard Metrics

  • Doability
    • Donation completion rates
    • Time on task (discovery, checkout)
    • Error rates and abandonment
  • Dependability
    • Confidence ratings (7-pt)
    • CSAT on confirmation
    • NPS (recommendation intent)
  • Delight
    • Feeling Scale adjectives (excited, reassured, doubtful)
    • Open-ended intercept comments on trust and gratitude
    • SUPR-Q trust subscale
  • Desire
    • Repeat donations within 60 days
    • Referral clicks (impact story shares)
    • Save-favorite cause usage

3. Research Methods

Primary Method: Intercept surveys (must-have per Strawman)

  • Placement 1: Confirmation page (trust, gratitude, CSAT).
  • Placement 2: Cause detail dwell ≥60s or exit intent (drivers of hesitation).
  • Placement 3: No results page (frustration, alternatives).

Intercept flow

  • Consent → 5 MaxDiff statements (Best/Worst) → Trust/clarity rating → CSAT (if confirmation intercept) → Open text optional.
  • MaxDiff statement pool weighted toward trust, clarity, and faith alignment for Current Gen.

Complementary methods

  • Usability testing (Lookback): Validate allocation indicator and confirmation copy.
  • A/B testing (GA/Optimizely): Test allocation proof vs baseline; warm vs cold confirmation.
  • Focus group (6–8 Current Gen donors): Explore meaning of trust, gratitude, and faith alignment.

Sampling

  • Segment: Current Gen flagged in CRM (fallback proxies: repeat donors, higher gift tiers).
  • N=150 intercept completes in 2 weeks (±8% margin).
  • 10 usability test participants; 1 focus group.

Data analysis

  • MaxDiff utilities: count-based; logit model if N>200.
  • Proportion tests: high confidence, high CSAT.
  • Confidence intervals for mean change.
  • Link intercept_completed IDs to GA custom events for behavior correlation.

4. Hypotheses

Product Hypothesis

  • Allocation indicator + testimonial near CTA will raise Current Gen high confidence share from 60% → ≥70%.

Design Hypothesis

  • Personalized confirmation with impact statement and gratitude will raise Current Gen high CSAT from 29% → ≥45%.

A/B Hypothesis 1

  • Suggested searches and filters will reduce no-result frustration and sustain Current Gen discovery “very easy” at ≥80%.

A/B Hypothesis 2

  • Highlighting $50+ tier will increase higher-tier selections (baseline 40% → ≥50%).

Mixed Hypothesis

  • Combined trust proof + warm confirmation will increase Current Gen NPS from 2 → ≥8.

7. Appendix

MaxDiff Statement Pool (weighted for Current Gen)

Trust & Transparency

  • I clearly understand where my money is going
  • I trust Compassion to use my donation wisely
  • I know I will receive updates about the impact of my donation

Impact & Urgency

  • The cause feels urgent and needs support right away
  • I can see a specific project or child I am supporting
  • A progress bar helps me see impact clearly
  • I can make a tangible impact through my donation

Faith & Community Alignment

  • Supporting this cause aligns with my Christian faith and values
  • The cause feels authentically connected to the local community

Emotion & Gratitude

  • The story or images move me emotionally
  • Compassion thanks me in a meaningful way after I give

Ease & Choice

  • The donation process is simple and quick
  • I have the option to give once
  • I can choose how much I want to donate

Engineering Intercept Rules

  • Confirmation: trigger after 3s; once per user per 2 weeks.
  • Cause detail: trigger at dwell ≥60s or exit intent; exclude users shown confirmation intercept in last 14 days.
  • No results: trigger on first occurrence per week.

GA Event Instrumentation

  • Standard: page_view, view_item, begin_donation, add_donation_info, donation_complete.
  • Custom: select_cause, select_amount, click_cta_donate, open_filter, apply_filter, no_results_shown, save_favorite, share_referral_clicked, intercept_shown, intercept_completed.
    User properties: Current Gen vs Next Gen, donor history, variant.

Integration with Strawman Requirements

  1. Discovery: Must have exploratory studies and benchmark reuse. Intercept here provides immediate donor perception at cause detail.
  2. Define: Must have problem statement and success metrics. Intercept delivers direct evidence of donor trust and clarity needs.
  3. Design: Must have usability testing of prototypes. Intercept insights inform allocation and confirmation prototypes validated in Lookback.
  4. Build: Must validate launch criteria with event instrumentation. GA events tied to intercept ensure readiness.
  5. Post-Launch: Must validate metrics against benchmark. Intercept + GA repeat donation and CSAT form post-launch evaluation.

Summary

This research centers intercept surveys (Eg. Lysnna) as the primary approach for Causes. It directly addresses trust and confirmation gaps identified in benchmarking, connects MaxDiff statements to in-the-moment donor motivations, frames metrics in CVG’s 4D Scorecard, and fulfills Strawman requirements across Discovery, Define, Design, Build, and Post-Launch.
