Supporter Research Overview

This section contains all core research efforts focused on understanding and improving the experience of Compassion's supporters, especially High-Impact Donors (HIDs). It includes usability studies, diary studies, and longitudinal beta research plans structured around the Supporter Value Proposition (SPVP). Several multi-method strategies are documented here, guiding work from daily in situ tasks to high-fidelity evaluations.

High-Impact Donor (HID) Research

A major focus of this archive is psychographic and behavioral segmentation of HIDs. You'll find early persona drafts, survey strategies, naming studies, and interview syntheses to better model HID mindsets and motivations. These documents inform targeted engagement efforts and align research with donation drivers specific to high-capacity donors.

Usability & Beta Testing

There’s an emphasis on direct feedback through usability tasks, contextual interviews, and iterative design feedback. Studies range from app icon preference tests and navigation benchmarks to A/B evaluations of new features like the impact timeline. All tests aim to quantify supporter trust, satisfaction, and clarity of experience.

Community Platform Research

This includes UX and behavioral research on Compassion’s Community platform. It explores how supporters engage with content, react to returned sponsor materials, and form lasting emotional connections through digital touchpoints. Strategy briefs and scripts help guide ongoing improvements based on supporter behavior and feedback.

Live Events & Notifications

Live Events concept testing and push notification evaluations extend the research into real-time and emotionally charged supporter experiences. These efforts help ensure alignment between supporter expectations and digital delivery mechanisms across both planned and spontaneous interactions.
