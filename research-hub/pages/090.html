Understanding Research Delivery: Scope, Timelines, and Execution Reality

🎯 Purpose

This document provides transparency into

  1. how research requests translate into actual work
  2. the lifecycle involved in delivering high quality evidence-based insights
  3. a comparison between typical timelines and actual delivery across several initiatives within research team

The goal is to build shared understanding of research scope, sequencing, and capacity so expectations across teams remain aligned and decisions remain evidence informed.

🗒️ Problem Statement

  • Between early January and early February, multiple research initiatives were initiated across analytics, survey research, and information architecture.
  • Each request represented a full research lifecycle rather than a single task.

The comparison below shows how standard timelines compare to actual delivery under compressed schedules.

🚋 Research Requests & What They Entail

Requested Work vs Actual Research Activities

Requested Outcome Research Activities Required
Funnel analysis using analytics data Data validation, event mapping verification, funnel definition, segmentation analysis, interpretation, synthesis, reporting
Lifecycle trust survey Research framing, construct definition, instrument design, pilot testing, QA, field monitoring, data cleaning, statistical analysis, synthesis

Closed card sort validation Hypothesis generation, category definition, validation design, agreement analysis, decision workshop
Rapid insights for decisions Insight synthesis, stakeholder readouts, clarification sessions, decision documentation

🍨 The Research Lifecycle

For context,

Each study typically follows a multi stage lifecycle to ensure insights are reliable and actionable.

Lifecycle Stage Description
Alignment Clarifying decisions, defining research questions
Design Creating instruments or study structures
Validation Pilot testing and revisions
Fielding Data collection window
Analysis Cleaning, modeling, interpretation
Synthesis Translating findings into insights
Decision Support Stakeholder discussions and recommendations
Integration Supporting implementation and follow up
