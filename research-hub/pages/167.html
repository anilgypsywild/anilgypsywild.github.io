🎥 Support Platform - High-Impact Donor (HID) User Story

This user story ensures clarity, measurability, and alignment with Product hypothesis.

Problem Statement:

As a High-Impact Donor (HID), I want to receive compelling, narrative-driven updates and build a deeper connection with the Frontline Church Partners (FCPs) and communities I support, so that I can clearly see the tangible, measurable impact of my donations and feel confident in my continued commitment.

Goal:

To reinforce and maximize my long-term commitment to giving, either through:

  • Increased retention (consistent, ongoing donations over time)
  • Higher financial contributions (larger or more frequent donations)
  • Deeper engagement (involvement in advocacy, sharing impact with others, influencing new donors)

Success Metrics (Aligned with Hypothesis):

  1. Engagement & Sentiment Tracking:
  2. Survey-based emotional impact assessments before and after key updates
  3. Net Promoter Score (NPS) or donor satisfaction scores over time
  4. Financial & Time-Based Commitment Metrics:
  5. Retention Rate: % of HIDs who continue giving after X months
  6. Annual Donation Growth: Increase in average giving amount per HID
  7. Increase in Pledge Commitments: % of donors committing to a long-term giving plan
  8. Prospecting & Expansion Metrics:
  9. Increase in peer-referrals & shared impact stories
  10. More internal/external recruitment of new HIDs through active donors

Key Experience Priorities:

  1. Timely, authentic, and compelling impact updates
  2. Visual storytelling & direct community interactions to reinforce giving behavior
  3. Clear, transparent progress tracking of financial contributions

📊 User Journey & Core Tasks

Primary Users:

  1. High-Impact Donors (HIDs) – Primary user, seeking impact visibility and emotional reinforcement
  2. Supporter Care Partners (SCPs) – Secondary user, facilitating and ensuring engagement touchpoints
  3. FCP Leaders & Staff – Providing updates, maintaining communication loop

Main Tasks & Interactions:

  1. Sign In & Personalization
  2. Quick access to relevant, high-impact updates based on personalized giving history
  3. See a Curated Impact Feed
    1. Activity feed populated with narrative-driven updates from FCPs
  4. Dynamic impact statements, showing real-time progress of supported programs
  5. Engage with Impact Stories
  6. Ability to interact with updates (likes, comments, reactions)
  7. Option to deepen engagement (e.g., messaging FCP leaders, attending live updates)
  8. Track Donations & Outcomes
    1. View a timeline of financial contributions vs. program milestones achieved
    2. Visual representation of direct impact per dollar donated
  9. Expand Commitment & Advocacy
  10. Option to increase or adjust giving frequency based on impact perception
  11. Ability to share impact stories within personal networks to inspire new donors

🔢 Connecting the User Journey to Hypothesis-Driven Research

  • Aligns directly with the research-backed hypothesis that tangible, narrative-driven updates increase donor retention & financial commitment.

    Bridges engagement to financial impact tracking, reinforcing long-term giving behavior.

  • Incorporates behavioral levers (social proof, commitment reinforcement, feedback loops) to sustain motivation and advocacy.

Hypothesis for Supporter Platform – High-Impact Donors (HIDs)

Hypothesis:

Providing compelling narratives and tangible, data-driven outcomes to Supporters will lead to a measurable increase in lifetime giving, both in retention rates (continued giving over time) and donation volume per donor, by reinforcing their commitment to the mission through sustained engagement and impact visibility.

Testable Predictions:

1. Retention Impact: Supporters who receive personalized, high-fidelity impact updates (e.g., visual storytelling, direct beneficiary updates) will have a higher donation retention rate over a 12-month period compared to those receiving generic updates.

2. Giving Volume Impact: Supporters who engage with narrative-driven updates and milestone-based impact tracking will increase their annual contribution by X%, relative to baseline giving trends.

3. Commitment Reinforcement: Supporters with high engagement touchpoints (e.g., responding to updates, interacting with impact reports) will demonstrate higher Net Promoter Scores (NPS) and lower churn rates than disengaged supporters.

Measurement Plan:

• Cohort Analysis: Compare retention and donation patterns across groups receiving highly visual, personalized impact updates vs. those receiving standard updates.

• A/B Testing: Test different formats of impact storytelling (video, text, real-time dashboards) to quantify which drives the most sustained engagement.

• Engagement Metrics: Track frequency of supporter interactions with impact updates and correlate with donation recurrence and growth.

Expected Outcome:

If the hypothesis holds, Supporters exposed to richer impact narratives and tangible updates will have higher retention rates (by X%), increased giving volume (by Y%), and greater engagement (by Z%), strengthening their long-term commitment to the mission.

🍹 Product Adoption Process

The Product Adoption Process outlines the sequential stages that users go through before fully adopting and integrating a product into their routine. Understanding these stages allows teams to optimize onboarding, engagement, and retention strategies effectively.

😋 Stages of Product Adoption

  1. Awareness
    • Users become aware of the product’s existence.
    • Marketing, word-of-mouth, and targeted outreach play a key role.
  2. Interest
    • Users actively search for more information about the product.
    • Content marketing, educational resources, and testimonials help drive curiosity.
  3. Evaluation
    • Users conduct a mental trial, considering if the product meets their needs.
    • This stage often involves comparisons with competitors and internal justification for use.
  4. Trial
    • Users engage in a limited usage of the product.
    • Free trials, demos, and beta programs are crucial in this stage.
  5. Activation
    • Users experience the value of the product firsthand.
    • Product walkthroughs, guided onboarding, and customer support enhance activation.
  6. Adoption
    • Users decide to fully integrate the product into their workflow or routine.
    • Continuous engagement, feature discovery, and value reinforcement ensure long-term retention.

📔 Summary & Strategic Considerations

  • Why It Matters: Understanding user behavior at each stage helps tailor engagement strategies and improve conversion rates.
  • Key Metrics to Track: Conversion rates at each stage, time-to-value, engagement patterns, and retention rates.
  • Optimization Strategies:
    • Reduce friction in Evaluation & Trial stages with intuitive onboarding.
    • Strengthen Activation with clear value demonstrations.
    • Reinforce Adoption with personalization, ongoing engagement, and customer success initiatives.

By aligning research and product strategies with the Product Adoption Process, teams can create a more data-driven, user-centric growth model for their platform.
