12-Week Onboarding & Research Plan for UX Researcher

Goal: Equip the new Researcher with the necessary skills, knowledge, and hands-on experience in UX research while making meaningful contributions to Causes (first half) and optionally Supporter (second half).

🎨 Weeks 1-2: Onboarding & Foundations

Focus: Understanding the product, using research processes, and gaining familiarity with product success goals.

🔍 Week 1: Product & Research Context

✅ Meetings & Setup:

  • Kickoff meeting: Walk through Compassion’s mission and research goals.
  • Meet key stakeholders ([name omitted], designers, PMs).
  • Get access to necessary tools (GitLab, Miro, Usability testing platforms).
  • Review existing research on Causes UX and user personas.

✅ Self-Study & Documentation Review:

  • Read through past Causes UX research and SUPR-Q framework.
  • Familiarize with Compassion’s approach to donor engagement & transparency.
  • Understand the Supporter side (optional for later weeks).

✅ Light Research Task:

  • Conduct a competitive analysis of donor platforms (e.g., GoFundMe, Charity Water).
  • Summarize usability strengths/weaknesses for presentation next week.

📈 Week 2: Research Planning & Usability Study Kickoff

✅ Causes UX Score Baseline (SUPR-Q Setup)

  • Finalize critical user journeys and usability tasks.
  • Prepare SUPR-Q survey and define success metrics.
  • Develop a research plan for baseline UX testing.

✅ Plan 1st Usability Study:

  • Recruit participants (5-8 users, representative donors).
  • Set up usability test script and testing environment.
  • Run pilot test and refine approach.

✅ Shadow Qualitative Research Sessions:

  • Observe and take notes on any ongoing donor interviews with a PM/Researcher/Designer
  • Learn how to conduct semi-structured interviews effectively.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Competitive analysis summary.
  • Draft research plan for usability study.

🥼 Weeks 3-4: Causes Usability Testing & Analysis

Focus: Conduct and analyze usability testing for Causes.

👟 Week 3: Execute Usability Testing

✅ Run Usability Testing on Causes

  • Conduct 5-8 usability tests using predefined tasks.
  • Capture usability issues, confusion points, and areas for improvement.
  • Begin compiling SUPR-Q score and qualitative insights.

✅ Synthesis & Early Findings

  • Identify high-impact usability issues to address with design team.
  • Draft UX recommendations based on findings.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Initial usability findings report (short & actionable).

🔈 Week 4: Full Analysis & Research Readout

✅ Analyze & Present Findings

  • Finalize SUPR-Q scoring & usability results.
  • Share top usability pain points & recommendations.
  • Present findings to product & design teams.

✅ Plan Next Study: Donor Qualitative Interviews

  • Identify key research questions (e.g., why donors contribute, what builds trust?).
  • Begin drafting interview guide.
  • Recruit 8-10 donor participants.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Causes UX Usability Report (full insights & SUPR-Q scores).
  • Interview guide draft for donor study.

🐾 Weeks 5-6: Donor Qualitative Research

Focus: Conduct user interviews to uncover donor motivations, trust-building factors, and UX friction points.

🗣️ Week 5: Conduct Donor Interviews

✅ Run 8-10 Interviews with Donors

  • Test assumptions from usability research.
  • Explore trust, transparency, and donation experience.
  • Identify barriers to contribution.

✅ Begin Analysis & Theming

  • Organize interview data into key themes.
  • Identify trends in trust factors, donation experience, and UX friction.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Raw interview notes & recordings.
  • Early synthesis of themes.

🍜 Week 6: Synthesis & Strategy Presentation

✅ Analyze & Summarize Donor Research

  • Identify 4-5 key donor personas based on insights.
  • Finalize pain points & trust-building strategies.
  • Provide recommendations for UX improvements.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Donor Research Report (summarizing trust & engagement insights).
  • Personas & Experience Map for donors.

🤝 Weeks 7-8: Design Collaboration & Iteration

Focus: Work with designers to refine solutions based on research findings.

🖌️ Week 7: UX Design Collaboration

✅ Work with Designers to Prototype Improvements

  • Explore usability fixes from testing & interviews.
  • Create low-fidelity wireframes (if needed) for concept validation.
  • Develop testable solutions.

✅ Plan Next Usability Test (Post-Design Iteration)

  • Test proposed UX refinements.
  • Recruit participants for follow-up validation study.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Refined UX recommendations with wireframes/mockups.

☑️ Week 8: Validate Design Changes

✅ Conduct Usability Testing on Design Iterations

  • Run another round of usability testing on updated designs.
  • Evaluate effectiveness of trust-enhancing and usability improvements.

✅ Deliverables:

  • Comparison Report: Before vs. After UX Changes.
  • Final UX insights for Causes team.

Weeks 9-12: (Optional) Supporter Research & Final Wrap-Up

Focus: Shift to Supporter UX research (if [name omitted] opts in) or refine final learnings from Causes UX.

📓 Week 9: Supporter Research Kickoff (Optional)

✅ Stakeholder Meetings & Research Questions

  • Understand Supporter platform goals.
  • Identify biggest UX gaps & research priorities.

✅ Plan Usability Study on Supporter Platform

  • Define key Supporter user journeys.
  • Develop usability test script.

🧪 Week 10: Execute Supporter Usability Testing

✅ Conduct usability testing on Supporter workflows.
✅ Analyze & present findings to product team.

🗺️ Week 11: Final Interview Study & Strategy

✅ Run qualitative interviews with Supporters (donors with larger contributions).
✅ Analyze and extract insights on engagement & motivation.

🎁 Week 12: Wrap-Up & Handoff

✅ Final Deliverables & Documentation:

  • Final UX Research Summary for both Causes & Supporter.
  • Recommendations for future UX improvements.
  • Presentation to leadership on key findings & next steps.

🚀 Outcome:

New Researcher will have:
✔️ Led usability tests, qualitative interviews, and UX scoring for Causes.
✔️ Developed data-backed recommendations to improve donor engagement.
✔️ Optionally contributed to Supporter UX research in the second half.
✔️ Created strong SUPR-Q baselines for long-term UX tracking.
