📢 Overview

This research initiative focuses on refining the High-Impact Donor (HID) persona, ensuring alignment with the Supporter Platform user experience, and integrating persona-driven insights into platform design and analytics.

Over the next 4–6 weeks, we will:

✅ Tighten the HID persona and develop sub-personas based on qualitative themes from [name omitted]’s contextual interviews.
✅ Gather and review all Supporter Platform design and user experience documents to ensure research alignment.
✅ Develop an HID persona identification survey to validate personas before platform rollout.
✅ Define user stories for the Supporter Platform, mapping them to personas.
✅ Create a persona-driven profile tagging system to track persona engagement and feature usage post-launch.

The goal is to bridge qualitative insights with data-driven persona tracking, allowing us to understand donor behaviors, refine engagement strategies, and optimize platform experiences.

🎯 Research Goals & Objectives

1️⃣ Refine High-Impact Donor (HID) Persona & Develop Sub-Personas

📌 Identify key motivations, behaviors, and engagement patterns from past research and [name omitted]’s Supporter High Impact Donor Contextual interviews.
📌 Develop sub-personas to better differentiate between different HID engagement styles.
📌 Ensure persona details are actionable and inform Supporter Platform design.

2️⃣ Align Research with Supporter Platform Development

📌 Gather all relevant design, experience, and research documentation to ensure research supports real user needs.
📌 Identify gaps in existing user research that require further validation.

3️⃣ Validate Personas Before Platform Rollout

📌 Design a survey to identify HID personas among real users before rollout.
📌 Ensure survey insights inform onboarding and profile setup.

4️⃣ Develop Persona-Driven User Stories

📌 Create persona-based user stories that align with platform goals.
📌 Ensure feature development is aligned with user needs.

5️⃣ Implement Persona-Driven Profile Tagging & Engagement Tracking

📌 Define a persona selection option within user profiles.
📌 Track persona-based user behavior to compare stated needs vs actual feature usage.

🟡 Phase 2: Survey Development & Persona Validation (Feb 26 – Mar 10)

📌 Task: Validate survey internally before rollout.
✅ Outcome: Ready-to-deploy survey for persona identification.

🟠 Phase 3: User Stories & Persona Mapping (Mar 11 – Mar 24)

📌 Task: Define persona-driven user stories for the Supporter Platform.
📌 Task: Ensure user stories align with persona motivations and engagement strategies.
✅ Outcome: Completed user stories, ready for integration into the platform roadmap.

🔵 Phase 4: Persona Tagging & Engagement Tracking (Mar 25 – Mar 31)

📌 Task: Design persona selection & tagging for user profiles.
📌 Task: Define tracking framework for persona-based engagement analysis.
✅ Outcome: Initial plan for persona-driven feature tracking.

📌 Expected Deliverables by March 31st

✔️ Refined High-Impact Donor persona with sub-personas.
✔️ Persona identification survey ready for rollout.
✔️ Persona-based user stories integrated into platform roadmap.
✔️ Persona tagging proposal for user profiles.
✔️ Engagement tracking framework to measure persona behavior post-launch.

🚂 Relevance to Supporter Platform Beta Rollout (April–May 2025)

📢 This work is highly critical to your planned rollout of the Supporter Platform beta for High-Impact Donors starting at the end of April and running through May.

🔹 The persona refinement and validation work will ensure that the beta rollout is tailored to the real behaviors, needs, and motivations of high-impact donors.
🔹 The HID identification survey will provide early insights into who these donors really are and how they engage with the platform before the beta launch.
🔹 The user stories and persona tagging system will help us track real user engagement, compare stated needs vs actual platform use, and iterate on features during and after the beta period.
🔹 By integrating persona selection into the profile setup, we can gather real data on donor behavior and optimize engagement strategies for the full platform launch.

This work will directly shape the rollout strategy, platform design, and post-launch engagement tracking, making the beta more data-driven and aligned with donor expectations.
