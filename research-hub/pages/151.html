🛝 Hifi Test Insights Slide Content

Slide 1: Welcome & Research Objectives

Content:

  • Research Focus Areas:
    • Notifications & engagement
    • Moderation clarity & trust
    • Impact timeline usability
  • Goal:
    • Improve user trust, discoverability, and engagement
    • Gather insights for future platform enhancements
  • Icebreaker Question: What do you think was the biggest pain point for users?

Slide 2: Moderation Messaging – User Trust & Clarity

Content:

  • User Feedback Insights:
    • 40% of users felt unclear or dissatisfied with rejection reasons
    • 67% want an “Edit & Resubmit” feature
    • 47% feel discouraged after a rejection
  • Recommendations:
    • Improve rejection messages with clearer explanations
    • Implement an inline guidance system for resubmission
    • Add an “Edit & Resubmit” feature
  • Discussion Prompt:
    • How would you feel if your comment was rejected without a clear reason?

Slide 3: Finding Impact Updates – Search & Filtering Gaps

Content:

  • User Behavior Patterns:
    • 47% of users rely on manual scrolling to find updates
    • 13% don’t know how to find updates at all
    • Lack of search & filters reduces engagement
  • Recommendations:
    • Introduce search & filtering options
    • Add clear navigation cues in the UI
    • Consider a “Saved for Later” feature
  • Discussion Prompt:
    • If you donated, what’s the first thing you’d look for in an impact update?

Slide 4: Impact Timeline – Clarity & Personalization Needs

Content:

  • User Insights:
    • 80% found the Impact page clear, but 13% struggled
    • 40% want updates tailored to their interests
    • 93% expect to tap for more details
  • Recommendations:
    • Highlight key statistics & milestones
    • Personalize updates based on donor preferences
    • Make filters & engagement options more intuitive
  • Discussion Prompt:
    • Do donors prefer big-picture stats or personal stories?

Slide 5: Engagement Features – What Users Expect

Content:

  • User Expectations:
    • 93% expect to tap for more details
    • 47% expect to comment, 20% expect to share
    • Users want more social & interactive features
  • Recommendations:
    • Improve engagement via reactions, comments, and shareable updates
    • Enhance notifications for impact updates
    • Test a pinned or saved updates feature
  • Discussion Prompt:
    • What would make you engage more—likes, reactions, or comments?

Slide 6: Notification Effectiveness – What Works & What Doesn’t

Content:

  • Notification Insights:
    • Users prioritize resolving rejected comments over checking replies
    • 60% want control over notification timing
    • Timeliness influences engagement, but users also want custom settings
  • Recommendations:
    • Allow custom notification settings
    • Clarify next steps for rejected comments
    • Test “smart notifications” that highlight relevant updates
  • Discussion Prompt:
    • What notification settings would you prefer: real-time, daily, or weekly digests?

Slide 7: Key Takeaways & Next Steps

Content:

  • Key Takeaways:
    • 📌 Fixing Moderation Messaging → Restores trust
    • 📌 Search & Filtering Enhancements → Improves discoverability
    • 📌 Refining Data Presentation → Ensures clarity
    • 📌 Personalization & Engagement Features → Boosts interaction
    • 📌 Notification Customization → Matches user preferences
  • Next Steps:
    • 🔹 PMs: Prioritize search, filtering, and personalization updates
    • 🔹 Designers: Improve UI clarity for moderation & engagement
    • 🔹 Engineers: Assess feasibility of notification customization & edit-resubmit
  • Final Discussion Prompt:
    • What’s the one feature you think will drive the biggest engagement boost?
