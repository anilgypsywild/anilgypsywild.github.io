Contextual Interview Script for Frontline Church Partners [WIP]

[[TOC]]

This script aims to explore the challenges and needs of FCPs in their work and identify opportunities to enhance their experience and effectiveness in engaging donors.

Introduction

  1. Introduce Yourself and Purpose
    • Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today.
    • I’m [Your Name], and I work with [Organization Name].
    • Our goal is to understand more about your daily work and how we can better support you and your community through our platforms and resources.
  2. Set Expectations
    • This conversation will take about [30–45 minutes].
    • We’ll cover a bit about your role, your interactions with the community, and your experience with engaging donors and using any resources we provide.
    • There are no right or wrong answers, so please feel free to share openly."
  3. Consent to Record (if applicable)
    • To make sure I capture all your insights accurately, is it okay if I record our conversation? Your responses will remain confidential.

Questions: Understanding the Role and Daily Work

  1. Background and Responsibilities
    • Can you tell me about your role and your main responsibilities within your community?
    • What would you say are your primary goals in your work here?
  2. Daily Activities and Community Interaction
    • Could you walk me through a typical day or week? What kinds of activities do you engage in to support your community?
    • How do you identify the needs of the children and families you work with?

Questions: Interaction with Donors and Platforms

  1. Engaging with Donors
    • How often do you interact with donors or communicate the needs of your community to them?
    • What types of information do you find are most helpful for donors to understand your community’s needs?
  2. Challenges in Donor Communication
    • Are there any specific challenges you face when communicating with or engaging donors?
    • What could make it easier for you to share the impact of donations with them?
  3. Use of Technology and Tools
    • Do you use any digital tools or platforms to track, report, or manage donations and resources?
    • How comfortable do you feel using these tools, and are there features you find especially helpful or difficult?

Questions: Feedback and Improvement Areas

  1. Impact Measurement and Reporting
    • How do you measure and report the impact of donations on your community?
    • What challenges do you encounter when tracking or sharing this impact?
  2. Support from the Organization
    • Are there specific resources or support from [Organization Name] that you rely on?
    • "How could we improve our support or resources to make your work easier or more effective?
  3. Suggestions for Improvement
    • If there was one change or new feature we could implement to make things easier for you, what would it be?
    • Are there additional ways you feel we could support you in connecting with donors or fulfilling your mission?

Closing

  1. Additional Thoughts
    • Is there anything else you’d like to share about your experience, either with your community or with our organization’s support?
  2. Thank You and Next Steps
    • Thank you for your insights and the important work you’re doing.
    • We’ll use this information to look for ways to better support you and your community.
    • If we have follow-up questions, would it be okay to reach out?
