ToDo: Supporter Platform Beta Launch – 12-Week Research Plan

Goal: Ensure high-impact donors (HIDs) have a seamless onboarding experience and remain engaged long-term by tracking their impact statements, visual updates, and interactions with the platform. The diary study will help fill gaps in onboarding goals, experience mapping, and ongoing engagement strategies.

🗓️ Week-by-Week Research Breakdown

Weeks 1-2: Research & Onboarding Strategy Development

🔹 Research Methods:

  • Stakeholder Interviews → [name omitted] & key team members to define high-level goals.
  • Competitive Analysis → Examine other philanthropy engagement platforms for onboarding best practices
  • Diary Study Kickoff → Start testing HID experiences to shape onboarding strategy.

🔹 Data Collected & How It’s Used:

  • Benchmarking Data → What works well in other platforms?
  • HID Expectations & Frustrations → Early insights from diary study participants.
  • Preliminary HID Journeys → Draft high-level HID engagement map.

➡️ Deliverable: Draft of onboarding script & first version of key HID user journeys.

Weeks 3-4: Onboarding & Early Engagement Testing

🔹 Research Methods:

  • Usability Testing (5-7 HIDs, rolling basis) → How intuitive is first-time use?
  • Diary Study Entries (Daily/Weekly) → Capture early friction points.
  • Onboarding Observation → [name omitted] introduces HIDs one by one, research team observes.

🔹 Data Collected & How It’s Used:

  • Time to First Interaction → How long before an HID checks their impact statement?
  • Onboarding Drop-off Data → Where do users get stuck?
  • Live Feedback from HIDs → Used to iterate onboarding flow in real-time.

➡️ Deliverable: Refined onboarding goals & flow + usability feedback incorporated into iteration 1.

Weeks 5-6: Impact Experience Deep Dive

🔹 Research Methods:

  • 1:1 HID Interviews (Post-Onboarding, 5-6 HIDs) → What information do they want first?
  • Heatmaps & Click Tracking (via Mixpanel) → What impact content do they interact with?
  • Midpoint Diary Study Review → Common patterns & feedback so far.

🔹 Data Collected & How It’s Used:

  • Engagement Heatmaps → Which pages matter most?
  • Qualitative Themes from Interviews → What makes them feel their money is well-used?
  • Iterated Personas for HIDs → Adjust Supporter engagement model based on real behavior.

➡️ Deliverable: First Impact Experience Report + Update to onboarding & engagement goals.

Weeks 7-8: Long-Term Retention & Influence Testing

🔹 Research Methods:

  • Retention Funnel Analysis (Mixpanel, 10 HIDs) → Do they keep coming back?
  • A/B Test Impact Update Delivery → What type of updates (videos, reports, dashboards) keep them engaged?
  • Usability Testing Round 2 → Improvements based on past learnings.

🔹 Data Collected & How It’s Used:

  • Retention Drivers & Drop-Off Points → Where do they disengage?
  • Preferred Content Formats → Used to improve notifications & updates.
  • Social Engagement Tracking → Do they share their impact stories?

➡️ Deliverable: Retention Report & refined content engagement strategy.

Weeks 9-10: Executive Engagement & Personalization Testing

🔹 Research Methods:

  • HID Personalized Outreach Experiment → Do personalized calls/messages improve engagement?
  • Sentiment Analysis on Impact Statements → Are the reports emotionally compelling?
  • Final Diary Study Reflection → Closing insights on what worked/didn’t.

🔹 Data Collected & How It’s Used:

  • Emotional Response to Impact Reports → Used to refine storytelling strategies.
  • HID Expectations vs. Reality Check → How does the platform match expectations?
  • High-Touch vs. Low-Touch Support → What level of interaction do HIDs want?

➡️ Deliverable: Final Persona Refinement + Executive Engagement Recommendations.

Weeks 11-12: Final Beta Readout & Next Steps

🔹 Research Methods:

  • Final UX Score Baseline (SUPR-Q Analysis) → Measuring the full platform experience.
  • Data-Backed Personas & Engagement Model Finalization → How do HIDs use the platform?
  • Stakeholder Workshop → Present findings to Compassion leadership.

🔹 Data Collected & How It’s Used:

  • Final Engagement & Satisfaction Metrics → Used to plan the full rollout.
  • Refined HID Personas & Strategies → Ensures future iterations are aligned.
  • Beta Summary Report → High-level outcomes & next steps.

➡️ Deliverable: Final Supporter Research Report + UX Strategy Recommendations.

This Week’s Action Items (March 4-8):

✅ Kickoff Stakeholder Interviews with [name omitted] (define HID onboarding goals).
✅ Start Diary Study Enrollment (ensure 3-4 HIDs begin tracking experience).
✅ Draft First HID Onboarding Script (to be tested in Week 3).
✅ Set Up Mixpanel Tracking for Impact Engagement Events (dashboard for early tracking).
✅ Competitive Analysis Report (Best Onboarding Practices for HIDs in Philanthropy).

Key Takeaways

  • There is no oboarding script, but the diary study will define it.
  • Focus is on engagement & impact tracking, not donations.
  • Every 2 weeks, iterate onboarding, retention, and content experience.
  • Mixpanel will track real behavior while diary study fills in gaps in expectations.
