Neighbour to Neighbor / Peer-to-Peer Fundraising on Compassion Causes

Objective: Use this doc to identify info relevant to research.

Main Problems

  • Fundraisers need to set collective goals for their community and track donations centrally.
  • They need to raise funds for multiple causes if exceeding the $10k cap.
  • Participants should receive updates automatically on the causes, reducing manual efforts.

Target Audiences

  • Influencers (micro to mega)
  • Supporting Churches (congregations, youth groups)
  • College Groups (chapels, clubs)
  • Donors (wanting to engage friends and family in collective giving)
  • Mass Supporters (average fundraisers and those supporting others’ causes)

Use Cases

  • Influencers like [name omitted], church groups, and colleges currently face issues using separate systems to set goals, track community contributions, and communicate cause updates. They need a solution to simplify collective fundraising efforts and donation tracking.

Why Peer-to-Peer Fundraising Fits Next-Gen

  • Next-gen donors want to feel part of something bigger, and influencers play a huge role in shaping their decisions. Peer-to-peer fundraising taps into this by allowing communities to rally around causes together, inspired by influencers or collective passion.

What is Peer-to-Peer Fundraising

  • Supporters can set up fundraisers alongside their community, track progress, and share it through social media. It harnesses collective giving power by enabling users to customize their fundraising pages with goals, bios, and community updates.

Competitors

  • Tiltify and DonorDrive are leaders in peer-to-peer software, often used by streamers. However, they lack the ability to fundraise for multiple causes, which is a key requirement for Compassion. Compassion's causes are more specific and cannot be integrated into these platforms.

Build vs. Buy

  • Existing platforms (DonorDrive, Tiltify) excel in gamification but don’t offer the necessary customization for multiple causes or detailed progress updates, making in-house development more suitable for Compassion’s needs.

Key User Stories

  • Fundraisers want an easy sign-up process, custom fundraising pages, and tools to track progress and thank donors automatically. They also need resources like toolkits and video content for easy sharing across social platforms.

Additional Features for Future

  • Teams, gamification (badges, leaderboards), more customization options, moderation tools, and legal compliance are potential areas for future enhancement.

Conclusion

Peer-to-peer fundraising offers a powerful way to engage next-gen supporters by allowing communities to collectively rally around causes they care about, leveraging the influence of social media and content creators.

Compassion must focus on building a tool that supports multiple causes, simplifies the user experience, and integrates essential features to scale fundraising efforts.
