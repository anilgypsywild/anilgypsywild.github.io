🚧 FCP: PERSONA SURVEY

📌 Objective

This survey aims to better understand how FCP staff in Ghana, Myanmar, Thailand, and Peru use the FCP Community App, their work challenges, and what improvements would make their experience better.

The survey takes less than 10 minutes and all responses will be confidential.

📋 Survey Questions

🟢 Section 1: Demographics & Work Context

📌 Purpose: Understand user backgrounds and work conditions.

  1. Where is your FCP located? (Dropdown: Ghana, Myanmar, Thailand, Peru, Other)
  2. What is your role at the FCP? (Select one)
    • Project Director/Coordinator
  3. How long have you been working at your FCP?
    • Less than 6 months
  4. What devices do you use for your work? (Select all that apply)
    • Smartphone
  5. How reliable is your internet connection? (Bandwidth & network constraints assessment)
    • Always stable
  6. What languages do you use at work? (Understanding language needs for localization)
    • English
  7. How do you prefer to provide feedback to Compassion International? (Select all that apply)
    • Paper-based surveys
  8. What challenges do you face when trying to provide feedback? (Select all that apply)
    • Internet is unreliable
  9. Would receiving surveys in your local language make it easier for you to respond?
    • Yes

🟡 Section 2: App Engagement & Usage

📌 Purpose: Identify how frequently and effectively users engage with the app given bandwidth constraints.

  1. How often do you use the FCP Community App?
  • Daily
  1. What are the main reasons you use the app? (Select all that apply)
  • Uploading reports or images
  1. Have you faced difficulties accessing or using the app due to low internet speeds?
  • Yes, often
  1. If the app had an offline mode, would that be helpful for your work? (Understanding offline feature demand)
  • Yes, very helpful

🔵 Section 3: Motivations & Work Challenges

📌 Purpose: Identify what drives engagement and barriers to using the app.

  1. What motivates you to use the FCP Community App? (Select all that apply)
  • It helps me communicate with my team
  1. What are the biggest challenges you face in your daily work? (Select all that apply)
  • Not enough time to complete all tasks

🟠 Section 4: Trust & Confidence in the App

📌 Purpose: Understand what builds and erodes trust in the platform.

  1. How confident are you that the app protects your data and information? (1-7 scale: 1 = Not at all confident → 7 = Extremely confident)
  2. Which factors make you trust the FCP Community App? (Select all that apply)
  • Transparency in updates and impact reporting
  1. What improvements would increase your trust in the app? (Select all that apply)
  • More updates on how my input is used

📝 Section 5: Open Feedback

📌 Purpose: Capture user insights beyond structured questions.

  1. If you could improve one thing about the FCP Community App, what would it be? (Select all that apply)
  • Faster load times
  1. Is there anything else you’d like us to know about your experience? (Optional open-ended field)
