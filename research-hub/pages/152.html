🍔 Overview

🍔 Overview

 Evaluate Push Notifications Evaluate Notification Feed Assess Content Moderation Clarity & Trust  Explore the Impact Timeline
  • This research proposal outlines the UZ.com user experience evaluation, scheduled to run from February 27 to March 3, 2025.
  • The study will follow the CVG Research Process to assess the Supporter App’s notifications, content moderation, and impact tracking features, ensuring alignment with donor expectations and usability best practices.

📜 Completed Research Report (03-03-2025)

📅 Study Timeline: Feb 17 – Feb 28, 2025

  • Feb 17 – Feb 27: Research scoping, user recruitment, finalizing study materials
  • Feb 20 – Feb 28: Conduct usability testing & initial data collection
  • Feb 25 – Feb 28: Midpoint synthesis, identify key usability gaps, begin follow-up testing
  • Feb 25 – Feb 28: Final data collection, post-test surveys, and stakeholder reporting
  • Feb 23- Feb 28: Analysis, reporting, GitLab documentation & presentation of insights

🔍 Study Goals

  1. Evaluate notification experience for clarity, engagement, and usability.
  2. Assess content moderation policies for transparency and user trust.
  3. Measure impact timeline comprehension and perceived donor value.
  4. Identify usability pain points and validate engagement pathways.

Methodology

👤 Participants:

  • Recruited faith-driven donors and supporters based on screener criteria.
  • Participants segmented by giving frequency and technology familiarity.
  • Total: 15 participants across initial usability tests + survey validation.

🛠️ Research Methods Applied:

  • Moderated usability testing (Tasks 1-4)
  • Survey questionnaire (Pre & post-test feedback)
  • Intercept surveys (In-app touchpoints for engagement validation)
  • Qualitative insights (Thematic coding from open-ended responses)

📊 Measurement Criteria:

  • Success rate & efficiency per task.
  • Perceived ease of use & clarity (Likert scale).
  • User confidence in app information (Post-test validation).
  • Engagement potential & behavior intent (Click tracking & open-ended responses).

Research Tasks

1️⃣ Push Notification Usability

📌 Objective: Evaluate clarity and actionability of notifications.
📋 Task: Participants will interact with and respond to different notification formats.
📊 Metrics: Success rate, perceived clarity.

2️⃣ Notification Feed Comprehension

📌 Objective: Assess how well users navigate and prioritize notifications.
📋 Task: Users will browse and locate important notifications in a simulated environment.
📊 Metrics: Success rate, ease-of-use rating, expected user actions.

3️⃣ Content Moderation Trust & Clarity

📌 Objective: Test whether rejection messages are clear and actionable.
📋 Task: Users will review a rejected comment and decide their next action.
📊 Metrics: Understanding of rejection reason, likelihood to appeal or disengage.

4️⃣ Impact Timeline Perceived Value

📌 Objective: Evaluate how effectively the impact timeline communicates donor impact.
📋 Task: Users will explore the timeline and respond to comprehension questions.
📊 Metrics: Perceived relevance, engagement potential, next-action intent.
