MaxDiff Survey Build Specification

Umoderated Survey in Lyssna

  • Estimated time: 8–10 minutes

SCREEN 1: Welcome Screen

Thank you for participating.

This short survey explores how supporters expect Compassion to respond when a cause cannot move forward as originally planned due to real world conditions.

There are no right or wrong answers. Please answer honestly based on what feels right to you.

SCREEN 2: Scenario Assignment (Randomized)

Lyssna feature: Randomize between multiple blocks

  • Create 4 separate blocks, each containing ONE scenario.
  • Set Lyssna to randomly show one block per participant.

Scenario Block A: Safety and Infrastructure

Imagine you supported a Compassion Cause to build a secure kitchen and gathering space so children can safely receive daily meals at a local church.

After fundraising begins, the church learns that the original construction site cannot be used due to unexpected zoning and safety restrictions. The community still needs support, but the original plan cannot move forward as designed.

Scenario Block B: Education and Skills

Imagine you supported a Compassion Cause to provide technology training and equipment for youth to develop job ready skills.

After fundraising begins, the local partner organization is no longer able to participate, delaying or changing how the program could be delivered.

Scenario Block C: Livelihood and Economic Empowerment

Imagine you supported a Compassion Cause focused on helping families start small businesses through farming or vocational training.

After fundraising begins, economic conditions change and the original business plan is no longer viable in that community.

Scenario Block D: Community and Youth Engagement

Imagine you supported a Compassion Cause to create a sports field or youth activity space to support community building and leadership development.

After fundraising begins, the project cannot move forward because land access permission is denied.

SCREEN 3: MaxDiff Task (Core)

Component: Best–Worst (MaxDiff)

Lyssna calls this: Best and Worst Choice

Instructions for Ranking Your Preferences

Based on the situation you just read, choose the option you would MOST prefer and the option you would LEAST prefer.

MaxDiff Items

  1. Automatically redirect my donation to a similar cause that meets the same need
  2. Ask me to choose a new cause before my donation is used
  3. Pause the donation and notify me so I can decide what happens next
  4. Refund my donation in full with a clear explanation
  5. Apply my donation to the same community in a revised way that addresses new needs
  6. Extend the cause timeline and continue fundraising when conditions improve
  7. Move my donation to a general Compassion fund supporting urgent needs
  8. Keep my donation allocated while the situation is reassessed and provide updates
  9. Cancel the cause entirely and return all funds
  10. Offer me the option to convert my donation into sponsorship or ongoing support

Lyssna settings for MaxDiff

  • Items per task: 4 or 5
  • Number of tasks per participant: 8–10
  • Randomize order: ON
  • Require both Best and Worst: ON

This is enough for stable utilities at n ≈ 50.

SCREEN 4: Trust Rating

Component: Rating scale

Question:

Based on the response you MOST preferred, how much would you trust Compassion to handle situations like this in the future?

Scale:
1 Very low trust
2 Low trust
3 Neutral
4 High trust
5 Very high trust

SCREEN 5: Giving Tier

Component: Multiple choice

Question:

In a typical year, about how much do you personally give to Compassion Causes?

Options:

  • Less than $25
  • $25 to $99
  • $100 to $249
  • $250 to $999
  • $1,000 to $3,999
  • $4,000 or more
  • Prefer not to say

SCREEN 6: Conditional Giving Impact

Branch Logic in Lyssna

  • If response ≤ $249 → show Screen 6A
  • If response ≥ $250 → show Screen 6B

Screen 6A: Low Tier Anchor

Component: Multiple choice

Question:

Imagine you planned to give about $10 to support this cause.

If Compassion handled the situation using the approach you MOST preferred, what would you do?

Options:

  • I would still give $10
  • I would give less than $10
  • I would increase my gift above $10
  • I would not give in this situation
  • I am not sure

Screen 6B: High Tier Anchor

Component: Multiple choice

Question:

Imagine you planned to give about $4,000 to support this cause.

If Compassion handled the situation using the approach you MOST preferred, what would you do?

Options:

  • I would still give $4,000
  • I would give less than $4,000
  • I would increase my gift above $4,000
  • I would not give in this situation
  • I am not sure

SCREEN 7: Future Intent

Component: Rating scale

Question:

Thinking about this situation overall, how likely would you be to support Compassion Causes again in the future?

Scale:
1 Very unlikely
2 Unlikely
3 Neutral
4 Likely
5 Very likely

SCREEN 8: Open End

Component: Open text

Question:

Is there anything Compassion should absolutely do or avoid when causes need to change or end?

Lysnna Data Outputs

  • MaxDiff utilities per action
  • Preference differences by scenario
  • Trust deltas by action
  • Giving impact by donor tier
  • Open text grounded in real scenarios
