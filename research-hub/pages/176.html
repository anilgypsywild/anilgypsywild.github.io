⏳ Objective:

Rapidly validate, refine, and optimize the Supporter Platform Beta for High-Impact Donors (HIDs) through multi-method UX research.

🔍 Research Overview

This research cycle ensures that usability, engagement, and retention are optimized before full-scale deployment.

Each week-long sprint captures key user insights through:

  • Surveys (SUPR-Q, CSAT, NPS)
  • Usability Testing (Moderated & Think-Aloud)
  • Behavioral Analytics (Feature Adoption, Drop-Offs, Retention)
  • A/B Testing (Personalization & Engagement Nudges)

📆 4-Week Research Breakdown

Each week builds on previous findings to accelerate iterative improvements.

Week 1: Onboarding & First Impressions

  • Goal: Ensure a frictionless first-time user experience.
  • What We Measure: Adoption rate, usability issues, and first engagement behaviors.

Methods:

  • SUPR-Q Survey (Usability & Appearance) – Assess ease of navigation, clarity, and onboarding friction.
  • Moderated Usability Testing (10 users) – Identify usability breakdowns in onboarding.
  • Behavioral Analytics (Drop-Off & Time-to-Value Tracking) – Measure how long it takes for users to engage.

Key Metrics:

  • Time-to-Value (TTV): Time from login → first engagement.
  • Drop-Off Rate: % of users abandoning onboarding.
  • SUPR-Q Usability & Appearance Scores: Benchmarking against UX industry standards.

Research Output:

  • Onboarding UX Fixes: Quick-win recommendations to optimize user flow.
  • Baseline TTV Data: Foundation for future engagement comparisons.

Week 2: Feature Engagement & Personalization

  • Goal: Understand what keeps users engaged and what drives feature adoption.
  • What We Measure: Which platform features add value vs. cause friction.

Methods:

  • SUPR-Q Survey (Credibility & Loyalty) – Assess trust in platform content.
  • A/B Testing (Personalized Notifications vs. Generic Alerts) – Measure effectiveness of engagement nudges.
  • User Interviews (Feature Perceived Value, 8 users) – Identify most/least useful features.
  • Behavioral Analytics (Feature Click-Through Rates & Interaction Frequency) – Track engagement levels.

Key Metrics:

  • Click-Through Rate (CTR): Engagement with notifications.
  • Feature Utilization Rate: % of users engaging with core features.
  • SUPR-Q Credibility Score: User trust in donor impact updates.
  • Qualitative Sentiment Analysis: Identify which features users love vs. ignore.

Research Output:

  • Data-driven feature prioritization: Which elements to refine, remove, or highlight.
  • Personalization recommendations: Optimize engagement nudges for HIDs.

Week 3: Retention & Long-Term Engagement

  • Goal: Identify key drivers of long-term user retention & churn risks.
  • What We Measure: Ongoing satisfaction and engagement fatigue signals.

Methods:

  • Longitudinal Survey (CSAT & Retention Tracking) – Gauge user satisfaction over time.
  • Behavioral Cohort Analysis – Identify engagement drop-off patterns.
  • Think-Aloud Interviews (8 users) – Assess emotional connection to the platform.

Key Metrics:

  • Retention Rate (Weekly Active Users, WAU): % of returning users.
  • CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score): User confidence in donation impact.
  • Engagement Fatigue Detection: Drop in interaction frequency over time.
  • SUPR-Q Loyalty Score: Willingness to continue using & recommend platform.

Research Output:

  • Churn risk predictors: Early warning signs of disengagement.
  • Retention strategy recommendations: Nudges to sustain engagement (e.g., content personalization, reminders).

Week 4: Beta Retrospective & Optimization

  • Goal: Final synthesis of findings before full launch.
  • What We Measure: Overall user sentiment, retention potential, and final optimizations.

Methods:

  • Final SUPR-Q Survey (Usability, Design, Credibility, Loyalty) – Holistic UX assessment.
  • Behavioral Audit (Pre vs. Post Engagement Analysis) – Identify improvement areas.
  • Stakeholder Workshop (PMs, UX, Engineering) – Convert research into design decisions.

Key Metrics:

  • Net Promoter Score (NPS): % of users likely to recommend.
  • Final Retention Rate: % of HIDs still active by Beta end.
  • SUPR-Q Aggregated Score: Overall UX performance.

Research Output:

  • Beta Report (Comprehensive UX Insights + Design Recommendations).
  • Final Feature Fix Prioritization (Usability, engagement optimizations).

🎗️ Final Takeaways

This 4-week research sprint delivers rapid, data-backed UX optimizations.

  • Each phase builds on the last, ensuring a continuous improvement loop before launch.
  • The approach balances quant & qual: Statistical rigor + deep user insights.
  • Final Outcome: A fully validated Supporter Platform Beta ready for scale.
