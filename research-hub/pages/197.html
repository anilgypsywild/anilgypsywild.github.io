Field Notes and Data Example: AI-Assisted Workflow Study (Thailand Pilot)

Project: AI-Assisted Workflow Study
Phase: Design → Validate
Date: October 2025
Participant ID: THA-P01
Role / Function: Frontline Church Partner
Session Duration: 42 minutes
Observer Name: PM

1. Session Context

Context Element Notes
Location / Platform Chiang Mai Partner Office
Workflow Type Shown Baseline → AI-Assisted Workflow
Prototype Type Static slides with guided narration
Technical Issues None
General Environment Shared workspace, warm and informal
Participant Mood at Start Calm, slightly nervous

2. Baseline Workflow Observation

Observation Area Field Notes
Steps they describe in their own words “I usually copy from my last report and fix a few words.”
Where they pause or hesitate Long pause at the description box. Unsure what detail to include.
Verbatim quotes about confusion “Should I write this in English or Thai?”
Emotional cues Nervous smile, tapping pencil, quiet voice.
Workarounds or shortcuts Copies previous report text and changes child’s name.
Final task ease rating (1 = Very Easy, 5 = Very Hard) 4

5. Field Researcher Observations (Qualitative)

Aspect Notes
Where they seemed most engaged When the AI suggested text aligned with their voice.
Where they lost focus or interest During system confirmation screens.
How they described trust in AI Unsure—liked clarity, but questioned accountability.
Key emotional transitions Tense → Relieved → Slightly anxious.
Contradictions Said “it’s helpful,” but kept asking “is it allowed?”
Verbatim quote “It helps me, but I’m scared if it’s wrong.”

6. Quick Summary (Participant Level)

  • Overall Ease: 4
  • Overall Confidence: 4
  • Emotional Word: Confident
  • Would they use it again?: 8
  • Immediate Red Flags:
    • Trust issue

Takeaway:
Low cognitive load (NASA-TLX 3/7) and high usability (UMUX-Lite 4.5/6), but lingering trust barriers.

7. Site Summary (Chiang Mai, 4 Participants)

Metric Mean Range Interpretation
UMUX-Lite (0–6) 4.5 4–5 Consistently usable
Confidence (0–6) 4.0 3–5 Moderate independence
NASA-TLX (1–7 ↓ better) 3.0 2–4 Low workload
Likelihood to Reuse (0–10) 8.0 7–9 High adoption potential
Likelihood to Recommend (0–10) 8.0 7–9 Peer advocacy
Emotional Tone Calm, Confident, Neutral — Generally positive
Most Common Fix First “Show AI changes visually.” — Transparency issue

Summary:
Chiang Mai users found the AI workflow easy and low-stress but want clearer visibility into how AI modifies their words.

8. Roll-Up Across Three Sites

Metric Chiang Mai (n=4) Bangkok (n=3) Udon Thani (n=3) Interpretation
UMUX-Lite (0–6) 4.5 5.0 3.5 Urban sites adapt better
NASA-TLX (1–7 ↓ better) 3.0 2.0 5.0 Rural sites report strain
Confidence (0–6) 4.0 5.0 2.5 Lower autonomy in rural settings
Likelihood to Reuse (0–10) 8.0 9.0 5.0 Strong intent only in urban sites
Emotional Tone Calm, Confident Energized Neutral, Stressed Emotional range widens by context

Qualitative Signals

  • Bangkok: “Feels modern and easy.” (energized)
  • Chiang Mai: “Helpful but scary.” (trust gap)
  • Udon Thani: “Too hard, I don’t understand what AI wants.” (cognitive barrier)

9. Example ANOVA (NASA-TLX: Mental Demand)

Site Scores Mean
Chiang Mai 3, 4, 3, 2 3.0
Bangkok 2, 2, 2 2.0
Udon Thani 6, 5, 4 5.0

Result: F(2, 7) = 9.6, p < .01

Interpretation:
Cognitive load differs significantly by site.
Udon Thani users find the AI-assisted workflow mentally harder.
Design action → Simplify prompts, localize guidance, and add visual AI edit tracking.
