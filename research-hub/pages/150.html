Research article

🏆 Executive Summary

  • This report presents findings from a research study on UserZoom for the Supporter Platform.
  • Specifically, the study evaluated how effectively the timeline communicates impact updates to users, identifies usability barriers, and highlights opportunities for improving engagement, clarity, and personalization.

Implications for Product

1

Moderation Messaging Clarity

40% of users were uncertain or dissatisfied with rejection reasons.

67% want an “Edit & Resubmit” feature.

Lack of clarity in content moderation decreases user trust and discourages engagement.

Addressing this can improve user confidence in the system.

 Implement clearer rejection messages and allow users to edit and resubmit easily.

Design an inline feedback system that explains why a comment was rejected and offers guidance on how to fix it.

Finding Specific Updates (Search & Filtering)

47% rely on manual scrolling;

13% don’t know how to find updates.

Discoverability issues frustrate users and reduce the efficiency of engagement with the platform.

Adding search and filters will make impact updates more accessible.

 Improve search and filtering functionality for impact updates. Introduce a clear, intuitive search and filter UI.

3

Impact Timeline Clarity

 80% found the Impact page easy to understand, but 13% struggled. While most users find the page understandable, improving clarity can ensure that no users are left behind. Simplify and enhance data presentation to make impact updates clearer.

Prioritize high-level impact numbers and specific milestones in the UI.

4

Relevance of Updates (Personalization)

 40% of users want different updates that better match their interests.

Personalization can significantly enhance user engagement and satisfaction, making updates more meaningful.

Provide tailored updates based on user preferences.

 Introduce personalization options that allow users to see updates relevant to their contributions.

5

User Engagement Features

93% expect to tap for more details, but only 20% engage with sharing or filtering.

Users want to interact with content, but current options do not encourage engagement.

Adding social and interactive elements can increase participation.

Improve engagement through interactive elements like reactions, comments, and sharing.

Key Takeaways & Next Steps

  • Fixing Moderation Messaging is the top priority to restore trust and clarity in the comment rejection process.
  • Search & Filtering Enhancements are essential to improve discoverability of impact updates.
  • Refining Data Presentation will ensure all users clearly understand the impact updates.
  • Personalization & Engagement Features should be implemented incrementally to increase user relevance and participation.

📓 Insights by Tasks

1️⃣ Push Notification Usability

📌 Objective: Evaluate clarity and actionability of notifications.
📋 Task: Participants will interact with and respond to different notification formats.
📊 Metrics: Success rate, time to interaction, perceived clarity.

Research Insights

#

 Question Insights
The majority of users clearly understood the meaning of the notifications, indicating that the wording and messaging are effective.
  • Users prioritized resolving issues with their comments over checking replies or general activity.
  • This suggests that negative feedback notifications (e.g., rejected comments) may have a stronger engagement pull.
  • The primary drivers for notification engagement are curiosity about responses and the need to resolve issues.
  • This highlights the importance of clarity in notification wording and actionability.
The notifications effectively communicate their purpose, reducing cognitive load and friction in user interaction.

Expectations align well with intended outcomes, but some users (13%) may benefit from clearer next steps regarding rejected comments.
  • While most users engage regardless of timestamps, a significant portion prioritizes recent notifications.
  • Timeliness influences engagement but is not a major barrier.
  • Time of delivery affects engagement, with 60% preferring to control when they receive notifications.
  • Consider custom notification settings or delivery time optimization.

Overall Takeaways

  1. Notification Clarity is Strong: All users understood the notifications, confirming effective wording and messaging.
  2. High Engagement with Problematic Notifications: Users prioritize rejected comment notifications over replies or general activity, suggesting negative feedback drives immediate action.
  3. Users Expect Clear Next Steps: While expectations generally align, a small gap exists in clarifying rejection reasons and actions.
  4. Timeliness Matters to Some Users: Nearly half of participants engage regardless of timestamps, but recent notifications receive higher engagement.
  5. Timing Control Preferences Exist: 60% of users want control over notification timing, suggesting potential value in scheduled delivery options.

2️⃣ Notification Feed Comprehension

📌 Objective: Assess how well users navigate and prioritize notifications.
📋 Task: Users will browse and locate important notifications in a simulated environment.
📊 Metrics: Success rate, ease-of-use rating, expected user actions.

Research Insights

#

 Question Insights

Notifications are generally clear, but a small subset of users (20%) encountered minor issues.

Grouping is well-received, with most users finding it useful for organization.

Users expect clear interaction controls, particularly for reading, dismissing, and marking notifications.

The notification wording is effective, ensuring clarity.

Users want a way to save or filter important notifications for later retrieval.

The notification feed design is effective, but some users (13%) see room for better organization.

Users want more control over notification management, particularly read/unread states and filtering/sorting options.

Overall Takeaways

  1. Notifications Are Clear: 100% of users found the notifications easy to understand, reinforcing strong message clarity.
  2. Grouping is Effective: 87% prefer the “This Week” vs. “Older” grouping, showing it helps with organization.
  3. Expected Interactions: Users expect to tap, dismiss, and mark notifications as read, reinforcing the importance of clear interaction controls.
  4. Saved/Pinned Option Needed: 60% of users want a way to save important notifications, suggesting an enhancement for pinned notifications.
  5. Design is Generally Effective: 87% found it easy to scan, but 13% suggested better organization.

3️⃣ Content Moderation Trust & Clarity

📌 Objective: Test whether rejection messages are clear and actionable.
📋 Task: Users will review a rejected comment and decide their next action.
📊 Metrics: Understanding of rejection reason, likelihood to appeal or disengage.

Research Insights

#

 Question Insights
While a many (40%) understood the rejection reason clearly, few, 33%, (5/15) found it unclear or needed more context.

While 60% either fully or somewhat agreed with the rejection, 20% disagreed, highlighting a need for greater transparency in moderation decisions.
Users primarily want more details (33%) and a way to contact support (27%) to better understand the rejection.

Most users (53%) are willing to adjust their comments, but a significant portion (33%) would reach out for clarification.
While most users remain engaged, 47% experience some level of discouragement, indicating the importance of clear communication to maintain user trust.

The top two desired improvements (67%) are clearer explanations and an easy way to edit & resubmit comments.

Overall Takeaways

  1. Moderation Clarity Needs Improvement: 33% of users found the rejection reasons unclear, and 60% either fully or somewhat agreed with the reasoning, leaving 40% uncertain or dissatisfied.
  2. Users Want Transparency & Guidance: The top-requested improvement (67%) is a clearer explanation of why comments were rejected, suggesting more detailed messaging or inline feedback could help.
  3. Willingness to Engage Remains Strong but Somewhat Affected: While 53% of users said rejection messages had no impact on engagement, 47% felt some level of discouragement, indicating moderation messages must be handled carefully.
  4. A Direct Edit & Resubmit Feature is Highly Desired: 67% want a way to edit and resubmit from the rejection message, suggesting this could reduce frustration and friction in the process.
  5. Appeal Options Could Improve Trust: 40% want an appeal or clarification option, reinforcing the need for accessible communication channels for users to understand and resolve rejections.

Recommendations:

  1. Improve transparency by offering detailed rejection reasons,
  2. Provide a direct “Edit & Resubmit” feature
  3. Consider an optional appeal/clarification system to maintain user trust and encourage continued engagement.

4️⃣ Impact Timeline Perceived Value

📌 Objective: Evaluate how effectively the impact timeline communicates donor impact.
📋 Task: Users will explore the timeline and respond to comprehension questions.
📊 Metrics: Perceived relevance, engagement potential, next-action intent.

Research Insights

#

 Question Insights
The majority (80%) found the Impact page easy to understand, with some users (13%) struggling with parts of it. Additional clarity in data presentation or explanations could improve comprehension.
  • Users are primarily drawn to high-level impact numbers and specific milestones, while categorized updates and timeline dates receive less attention.
  • Prioritizing big-picture statistics and highlighted achievements may enhance engagement.
  • Almost half of users rely on manual scrolling, which suggests search and filtering options should be more prominent or improved.
  • Some users (13%) don’t know how they would find updates, pointing to a need for better UI cues for discoverability.
  • While no one found the updates completely irrelevant, 40% of users would prefer different updates.
  • Personalization or filtering options could help tailor updates to individual user interests.
  • Most users expect to interact with updates, particularly by tapping for more details (93%).
  • However, commenting (47%) and sharing (20%) are secondary expectations.
  • The low engagement with filtering (20%) suggests it may need a more intuitive placement or clearer purpose.
  • Users want personalized updates (33%) and more interactivity (27%).
  • A small number (7%) also value visual elements, suggesting that adding images or videos might enhance engagement.

Overall Takeaways

  1. Prioritize key statistics & milestones in the UI.
  2. Improve discoverability of impact updates with clearer filtering.
  3. Introduce interactive features (comments, reactions, Q&A).
  4. Explore personalization options for tailored updates.
