MaxDiff Question Set Report: Compassion Causes – Donor Motivations

  • Audience: Research and product teams
  • Prepared by: [account removed]

1. Objective

Identify and prioritize the key motivations driving first-time and repeat donations on the Compassion Causes platform using MaxDiff scaling.

2. Screener Questions

These screener Q's will allow us to segment respondents into four analysis groups: CurrentGen, NextGen, High-Impact Donors, Casual Supporters

Q1. Age Group
Which of the following best describes your age?

  • 18–24
  • 25–34
  • 35–44
  • 45–54
  • 55+
    (Use 18–34 for NextGen; 35+ for CurrentGen.)

Q2. Giving Frequency
In the past 12 months, how often have you donated to any charitable cause?

  • Weekly
  • Monthly
  • A few times a year
  • Once a year or less
  • Never
    (Higher frequency + larger gift size → proxy for High-Impact Donors; lower frequency → Casual Supporters.)

Q3. Typical Gift Size
When you donate, what is your typical donation amount?

  • Less than $50
  • 50–199
  • 200–499
  • $500 or more
    (Use $500+ as the High-Impact Donor cutoff.)

Q4. Giving History with Compassion Causes
Have you donated to Compassion Causes before?

  • Yes, many times (repeat donor)
  • Yes, once (first-time donor)
  • No, not yet
    (Lets you split first-time vs. repeat giving in analysis.)

Q5. Role / Context (optional, for richer clustering)
Which of the following best describes you?

  • I mostly donate as an individual
  • I often give or fundraise with my church or community group
  • I often promote or advocate for causes on social media
  • I am a student or young professional still exploring how I give

Mapping to Segments

  • NextGen vs CurrentGen → From Q1.
  • High-Impact Donor vs Casual → From Q2 + Q3 (frequency + typical size).
  • First-time vs Repeat → From Q4.
  • Extra richness → Q5 helps align with Influencer, Church, Student personas in N2N research.

4. Proposed Question Pool

Each of these statements will be presented across randomized sets of 4–5 items. Participants will select the most motivating and least motivating reason.

Transparency & Trust

  1. I clearly understood where my money was going.
  2. I trust Compassion to use my gift wisely.
  3. I believe the donation will reach the intended community.
  4. I could see how my donation made a difference (impact evidence).

Urgency & Need

  1. The need felt urgent and timely.
  2. I knew donations would make a difference right away.
  3. The cause was close to being fully funded.

Faith Alignment

  1. It aligns with my Christian faith and values.
  2. Giving felt like part of my spiritual practice.

Emotional Connection

  1. I felt personally connected to the cause or community.
  2. The story or images made me feel moved.
  3. I wanted to help because I could imagine myself or my family in their place.

Specificity & Tangibility

  1. I could see a specific project or child I was supporting.
  2. The progress bar or percentage funded showed me impact clearly.
  3. I knew exactly how my donation amount would be used.

Community & Influence

  1. I saw others in my network supporting it.
  2. I wanted to be part of something larger than myself.
  3. I wanted to be recognized or thanked for contributing.
  4. Influencers, churches, or groups I trust supported it.

Ease of Giving

  1. The donation process was simple and quick.
  2. The “Donate” button was clear and easy to find.
  3. I could donate on my phone without difficulty.

Representation & Authenticity

  1. I saw flags, maps, or local cultural context.
  2. The cause felt authentically connected to the local community.

Follow-up & Relationship

  1. I knew I would receive updates about the impact of my gift.
  2. Compassion said thank you in a meaningful way.
  3. I felt like part of an ongoing relationship, not just a one-time transaction.

Choice & Flexibility

  1. I had the option to give once or set up recurring gifts.
  2. I could choose how much to donate.
  3. I could support more than one cause at the same time.

5. Survey Instructions (for participants)

“You will see a series of screens, each showing 4–5 possible reasons someone might choose to donate to a cause. For each set, please select the reason that would motivate you the most and the reason that would motivate you the least.”

6. Max-Diff Statistical Design Details

  • Full set: 30 factors.
  • Balanced exposure: each factor shown an equal number of times across respondents.
  • 4–5 items per screen.
  • Randomized block design ensures coverage and minimizes order bias.

Max-Diff Deliverables

  • Relative importance scores for all 30 factors.
  • Ranked hierarchy of donor motivations (overall).
  • Segment-level results: CurrentGen vs. NextGen vs. High-Impact Donors vs. casual supporters.
  • Top motivators for first-time vs. repeat giving.
  • Recommendations for design, product, and messaging priorities.
