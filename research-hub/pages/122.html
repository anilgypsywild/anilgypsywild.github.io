HID Persona Presentation Plan

1. Objectives of the Presentation

Core Message:

  • Personas are dynamic, data-driven models of user behavior, not static marketing constructs.
  • Understanding user psychographics and motivations is essential for product success.
  • Personas must be continuously refined and validated using both qualitative and quantitative data.

Key Topics:

  1. Defining Personas
    • Distinction between UX personas and marketing personas
    • The limitations of fictional personas (e.g., ‘Donor Dave’)
  2. The Strategic Value of Personas
    • Facilitating alignment across teams
    • Driving data-informed product decisions
    • Enhancing engagement through user-driven insights
  3. Methodology for Persona Development
    • Data sources: Interviews, surveys, behavioral analytics
    • Methods: Segmentation, clustering, validation
  4. Maintaining Persona Relevance
    • Continuous data integration and updates
    • Feedback loops from research, support, and engagement metrics
    • Evolution of personas as user behaviors shift
  5. Practical Applications
    • Case studies demonstrating persona-driven decision-making
    • Relevance of HID personas in supporter-side initiatives

2. Presentation Approach

Formats & Tools:

  • Slide Deck (PowerPoint/Google Slides): Structured flow with visuals and supporting research.
  • Miro Board: Interactive session to challenge misconceptions and map persona evolution.
  • Live Case Study: Demonstration of a persona’s direct impact on product strategy.
  • Q&A Session: Open discussion to address concerns and clarify applications.

3. Anticipated Challenges & Strategies for Addressing Them

Potential Misconceptions:

  • Confusion between UX personas and marketing personas.
  • Resistance to the idea of personas as evolving models.
  • Uncertainty regarding the practical application of personas in decision-making.

Mitigation Strategies:

  • Use real-world, data-backed examples to demonstrate persona validity.
  • Highlight the risks of ignoring user insights (e.g., failed engagement, low retention).
  • Provide a structured framework for integrating personas into daily workflows.

4. Division of Responsibilities

Section Presenter Content Overview
Introduction & Importance Meme Context-setting and problem definition
Defining Personas Anil Explanation of UX personas vs. marketing personas
HID Persona Development Anil Overview of methodology, data sources, and clustering approach
Maintaining Persona Relevance Anil Best practices for ongoing persona refinement
Persona Application in Strategy Meme Case studies and practical implementation
Q&A & Discussion Both Open floor for audience engagement

5. Key Questions for Consideration

  • How can we shift the team’s perception of personas from static to dynamic models?
  • Which case studies or real-world applications will resonate most with the audience?
  • Would a supplementary guide help attendees integrate personas into their workflow?
  • How do we address skepticism from those who see personas as optional?
  • What follow-up actions will reinforce this learning session?

6. Next Steps & Execution Plan

  1. Develop initial slide deck with clear visuals and supporting data.
  2. Create an interactive Miro board for live engagement.
  3. Finalize role assignments and refine speaking points.
  4. Conduct a dry run to ensure clarity and flow.
  5. Deliver the presentation and collect post-session feedback for continuous improvement.
