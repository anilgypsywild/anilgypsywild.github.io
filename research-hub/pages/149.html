📧 1. Purpose

This research supports the Community Supporter Platform by: optimizing email design for clarity, emotional resonance, and actionability. The research-to-design cycle aligns with the research team’s UCD model and measures against our 4D UX framework (Doability, Dependability, Delight, Desire).

🧪 3. Research Questions & Hypotheses

Bucket

Research Question

E.g. Hypothesis

Metric(s)
Behavioral Can users locate and interact with the CTA quickly? 85% will locate and click CTA within 5 seconds Time-to-click, success rate
Attitudinal Is the email clear and trustworthy? ≥70% will rate clarity ≥4 on a 5-point Likert scale Likert rating, CSAT
Emotional Does the email evoke emotional connection or inspiration? Story-driven layout will yield higher positive sentiment Sentiment tags, open-text emotion
Adoption Will users act on the email or return later? Higher comprehension will increase intent to engage Engagement intent score, scroll depth
