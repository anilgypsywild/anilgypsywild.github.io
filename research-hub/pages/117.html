Research article

Goal

Construct a complete tiered model for using the 4D UX framework across multiple studies—Causes and Community Platform (FCP & Supporters)—showing how to:

  1. Apply 4D across different research scopes
  2. Produce comparative scorecards at varying fidelity
  3. Roll it up for exec review without losing actionable depth or mission clarity

Research Overview: 3 + 3 Studies by Platform & User Group

Platform User Group Study 1 Study 2 Study 3
Causes Mixed (SO, FF) Overall UX Core Workflows Giving/Sharing Experience
Community FCPs Overall UX Posting/Moderation Onboarding/Training
Community Supporters Overall UX Content Consumption Notifications/Feedback Loop

Each study should collect quantitative data (SUS, CSAT, NPS, Emotion, TTV) and qualitative insights (quotes, moments of frustration, emotional drivers), then translated into the 4D UX Framework:

4D UX Framework Applied Across Studies

4D Pillar Sample Question Example Metric Type of Insight
Doability Can users complete key flows with ease? Task success, TTV, SUS Friction, bottlenecks, confusion
Dependability Do they trust the system and feel it works reliably? NPS, CSAT, reliability rating Tech performance, policy clarity
Delight Is the experience emotionally fulfilling or frustrating? Emotion scale, sentiment Uplift, gratitude, spiritual impact
Desire Will they return, recommend, or deepen engagement? Repeat visit intent, NPS Loyalty, stickiness, inspiration

✅ Recommended Actions (Tied to Mission)

  • Fix Mobile UX (Causes/SO): Supporter Operations deserve workflows that feel as smooth as their mission to serve.
  • Strengthen Trust (Community FCPs): Clarify moderation & feedback to help FCPs feel heard and empowered.
  • Invest in Joy (Supporters): Emotional uplift correlates with donor loyalty—build on what’s working.

✝️ Mission Tie-In: How 4D Serves Compassion’s Goals

4D Spiritual Alignment
Doability “Make it easy for those who serve to focus on the children, not the tools.”
Dependability “Trust builds dignity—platforms should reflect Christ’s reliability and compassion.”
Delight “Every small joyful moment is an opportunity to reflect God’s love through digital interaction.”
Desire “Returning again and again to serve or give is a signal of faithful participation in the mission.”

🛠️ Implementation Plan

  1. Instrument each study with both quant (SUS, CSAT, NPS, Emotion, TTV) and qual (quotes, themes).
  2. Score per user group & study with the 4D table (like above).
  3. Add benchmark thresholds for each metric to highlight outliers.
  4. Roll up insights quarterly into a 1-slide 4D Scorecard for leadership.
  5. Tie each 4D pillar to spiritual framing, so the mission remains present in each finding.
