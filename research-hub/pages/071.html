Research Strategy for N2N '25

  • Research strategy for aligning research efforts with the Causes P2P Fundraising milestones from [name omitted].
  • This includes identifying key milestones and the type of research needed at each stage.
  • This strategy aligns with the P2P milestones and ensures that research efforts are targeted, actionable, and drive measurable impact.
  • It integrates usability testing, validation, and A/B testing at key milestones.

Milestones and Research Plan

1. Causes - P2P Fundraising (HiFi Testing)

  • Timeline: February-March 2025 (Already started)
  • Research Type: Summative usability testing + Benchmarking
  • Goal: Ensure high-fidelity P2P fundraising experience meets usability, trust, and engagement criteria.
  • Methods:
    • Usability Testing (moderated/unmoderated)
    • Benchmark Metrics (SUS, CSAT, NPS, Task Completion)
    • Pain Point Identification
  • Key Deliverables:
    • Final usability testing report on P2P fundraising experience

2. Causes - Adding Video from FCPs (Validation)

  • • Timeline: March 2025 (Not started)
  • • Research Type: User sentiment testing + A/B Testing
  • • Goal: Understand whether adding videos from FCPs increases donor engagement and conversion.
  • • Methods:
    • • A/B Testing: Fundraising page with vs. without video.
    • • Eye-tracking or clickstream analysis to measure engagement.
    • • Survey/Interviews to gauge emotional connection with video content.
  • • Key Deliverables:
    • Engagement metrics report

3. Causes - Design Enhancements (A/B Testing for Conversions)

  • • Timeline: Ongoing (Quarterly; started with [name omitted])
  • • Research Type: Continuous A/B testing & conversion optimization
  • • Goal: Improve key touchpoints (CTAs, urgency tags, UX design refinements).
  • • Methods:
    • • Conversion Funnel Analysis (identify drop-off points)
    • • A/B Testing on CTA Placement & Design
    • • Heatmaps & Clickstream Analysis
  • • Key Deliverables:
    • • Recommendations for donor engagement optimizations

4. Causes - Logged-In Experience (Lo to HiFi Testing)

  • • Timeline: April - June 2025 (Not started)
  • • Research Type: End-to-end UX testing + Task-based usability studies
  • • Goal: Optimize the logged-in donor journey for clarity and ease of use.
  • • Methods:
  • • Lo-Fi Prototype Testing (April)
  • • Hi-Fi Testing + Iterations (May-June)
  • • RITE (Rapid Iterative Testing & Evaluation) for quick iterations
  • • Key Deliverables:
  • • Research insights for personalized donor dashboards
  • • Usability findings on donor journey friction points

5. Causes - Email Journey Enhancements (Validation)

  • • Timeline: April - June 2025 (Not started)
  • • Research Type: Email A/B Testing + User Surveys
  • • Goal: Optimize email personalization & automation for donor retention.
  • • Methods:
  • • A/B Testing: Email templates (impact-focused vs. direct ask)
  • • Surveys: What drives donors to re-engage after an email?
  • • Click & Open Rate Analysis
  • • Key Deliverables:
  • • Engagement report on best-performing email designs

6. Causes - Multi-lingual Fundraising + New GPA

  • • Timeline: July - September 2025 (Not started)
  • • Research Type: Usability Testing + Qualitative/Quantitative Research
  • • Goal: Test multilingual experience & expand fundraising into new geographic regions.
  • • Methods:
  • • Usability Testing in Multiple Languages (translation accuracy)
  • • Qualitative Research (interviews with target audiences)
  • • Quantitative Surveys (measure trust & usability)
  • • Key Deliverables:
  • • Localization & language clarity insights
  • • Usability recommendations for GPA expansion

7. Causes - Recurring Giving (Validation)

  • • Timeline: October - December 2025 (Not started)
  • • Research Type: Behavioral Research + Subscription UX Testing
  • • Goal: Understand barriers & motivators for recurring donations.
  • • Methods:
  • • Behavioral Analytics (drop-off rates for recurring signup)
  • • A/B Testing: One-time vs. recurring ask placements
  • • User Interviews: Recurring donor motivations
  • • Key Deliverables:
  • • Report on why donors do/don’t opt-in to recurring giving
  • • Optimized recurring giving flow recommendations

8. Community FCP Standard Survey (Translation & Simplification)

  • • Timeline: End of March 2025 (Started with [name omitted])
  • • Research Type: Survey Refinement + UX Validation
  • • Goal: Ensure FCP Standard Survey is optimized for comprehension, accuracy, and localization.
  • • Methods:
  • • Cognitive Interviews (validate comprehension)
  • • Survey Usability Testing
  • • Translation Validation
  • • Key Deliverables:
  • • Finalized survey version for multilingual deployment
  • • Recommendations for simplifying FCP survey structure

Final Summary: Research Priorities by Quarter

Quarter Research Focus
Q1 (Jan-March 2025) HiFi Usability Testing (P2P Fundraising), Video Testing, A/B Testing for Design Enhancements
Q2 (April-June 2025) Logged-In Experience, Email Journey Enhancements, Iterative Testing for P2P Optimization
Q3 (July-Sept 2025) Multi-Lingual & GPA Fundraising, Usability Testing Across New Markets
Q4 (Oct-Dec 2025) Recurring Giving Optimization, Behavioral Research for Subscription Models

Next Steps

  1. 1. Feb-March: Complete HiFi usability testing on P2P Fundraising.
  2. 2. March: Kickoff video integration research (A/B testing).
  3. 3. April-June: Conduct P2P logged-in experience research & test email journey optimizations.
  4. 4. July-Sept: Launch multilingual fundraising usability research.
  5. 5. Oct-Dec: Conduct recurring giving behavioral studies.

Key Research Outcomes

  1. • Improve donor engagement, trust, and usability across P2P fundraising.
  2. • Optimize key design components through rapid A/B testing & usability studies.
  3. • Ensure seamless expansion into multilingual and GPA fundraising.
  4. • Drive higher conversion rates for recurring donations.
