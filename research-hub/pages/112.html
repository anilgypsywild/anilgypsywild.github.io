This guide outlines the UCD process used at CVG, with associated research goals, key deliverables, and tools. Use this to align Product, Design, and Research across project stages.

🔄 Overview of the CVG UCD Lifecycle

Stage Key Activities Research Focus Tools & Frameworks
1. Discovery Rapid ideation, tech viability, Product Requirements/FAQ briefs Competitive, exploratory, and gap analysis Miro, MindMap, Jobs-to-be-Done, Google HEART, Kano, Desk Research
2. Define Scoping, user flows, success metrics, low-fi prototypes Rapid qualitative tests, UX Wizard of Oz, early A/B Empathy Maps, Journey Maps, User Flows, Figma (low-fi), Tree Testing
3. Design Story creation, mid-/hi-fi wireframes, backend alignment Mid-fidelity qualitative validation Figma (interactive), Lookback, SUS, SUPR-Q, UX Scorecards
4. Build Iterative story testing, production-ready hi-fi flows, agile sprint development Pre-/post-launch UX testing, KPI tracking A/B Testing, RITE Testing, UX Scorecards, GoogleAnalytics, Optimizely, Mixpanel

📍 Notes on Research Integration

  • Discovery → Define: Use quant+qual to frame the opportunity and user problem.
  • Define → Design: Validate concepts with users early and often (Wiz of Oz, Lo-Fi).
  • Design → Build: Tight loop testing using SUS, SUPR-Q, or RITE helps de-risk delivery.
  • Post-Launch: Compare actual XP to baseline metrics; track long-term behavior via analytics.

🧩 Related CVG Practices

  • Use the ART Form to submit or track research alignment.
  • Reference our Scorecard Templates (SUPR-Q, HEART) for consistent KPI tracking.
  • Pair with your PM/UX to scope emotional, behavioral, and spiritual success metrics.
