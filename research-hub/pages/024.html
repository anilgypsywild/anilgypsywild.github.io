📊 Primary UX Metrics

🏆 Core Tasks to Baseline for Causes UX Scorecards

To establish CSAT, NPS, and SUPR-Q metrics for Compassion Causes, we need to baseline the following core tasks based on findings from the research documents. Below is a structured list of tasks along with the relevant usability metrics.

This structure ensures a quantifiable UX scorecard with clear baselines for CSAT, NPS, and SUPR-Q, addressing usability, engagement, and trust across Compassion Causes. 🚀

📊 Primary UX Metrics

  1. Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) – User satisfaction rating.
  2. Net Promoter Score (NPS) – User loyalty and likelihood to recommend.
  3. System Usability Scale (SUS) – Overall usability evaluation.
  4. SUPR-Q – Website/app quality perception, including trust and usability.

🔍 Baseline Core Tasks and Metrics

1️⃣ Cause Discovery & Selection

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Search and find a cause.
  • Filter and sort causes by category, urgency, or funding need.
  • Understand and interpret funding progress indicators.
  • Select a cause and proceed to donation checkout.

📊 Metrics:

  • Time on Task – How long users take to find a relevant cause.
  • Task Success Rate – Percentage of users who successfully find a cause.
  • Ease of Use – User rating of search and navigation.
  • SUPR-Q Trust Score – Perceived trust in donation transparency.

📈 Existing Issues:

  • Unclear funding allocation (90/10 vs. 95% split confusion) (Causes_ ThematicAnalysis UserConcerns, 2024 Dec – Causes_ThematicAnalysis_UserConcerns.pdf).
  • Urgent causes not clearly marked (Causes_ Improving Donor Engagement and Usability_ Insights on Urgent Tags, CTAs, and Cultural Representation [Jan '25] – Causes_Improving_Donor_Engagement.pdf).
  • Navigation difficulties with cause tiles (Causes_ Core Flows Usability Research Nov '24 – Causes_Core_Flows_Usability_Research.pdf).

2️⃣ Cause Information & Transparency

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Read and understand a cause’s impact statement.
  • Identify how donations are used.
  • Compare donation amounts (ascending vs. descending order).
  • Trust the cause and its financial transparency.

📊 Metrics:

  • Trust Confidence Score (≥75%) – Do users believe their donation is allocated correctly?
  • Time on Task – How long it takes to read and understand donation allocation.
  • SUPR-Q Information Clarity – Perceived transparency of donation breakdown.

📈 Existing Issues:

  • Users report confusion on how donations are allocated (Causes_ ThematicAnalysis UserConcerns, 2024 Dec – Causes_ThematicAnalysis_UserConcerns.pdf).
  • Lack of infographics or clear donation breakdowns (Causes_ Post Production Research Brief – Causes_Post_Production_Research_Brief.pdf).

3️⃣ Checkout & Payment

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Complete a donation.
  • Adjust donation amount using a slider bar.
  • Use different payment methods (credit card scanning vs. manual entry).
  • Receive confirmation and receipt.

📊 Metrics:

  • Task Completion Rate (≥90%) – Successful checkouts.
  • CSAT (≥6) – Satisfaction with the donation process.
  • SUPR-Q Usability Score – Ease of completing a donation.
  • Trust Survey (≥75%) – Confidence in where money is going.

📈 Existing Issues:

  • Unlabeled slider bars create confusion (Causes_ Post Production Research Brief – Causes_Post_Production_Research_Brief.pdf).
  • Lack of payment security transparency reduces trust (Causes_ ThematicAnalysis UserConcerns, 2024 Dec – Causes_ThematicAnalysis_UserConcerns.pdf).
  • Manual card entry slows process; scanning preferred (Causes_ Post Production Research Brief – Causes_Post_Production_Research_Brief.pdf).

4️⃣ Navigation & Usability

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Locate the donation button.
  • Navigate between cause categories.
  • Use filters effectively.
  • Find the FAQ/help section.

📊 Metrics:

  • Time on Task – Navigation speed.
  • CSAT (≥6) – Satisfaction with ease of navigation.
  • SUPR-Q Usability Score – Ease of use and accessibility.
  • Heatmap Analysis – Click and scroll tracking for UI issues.

📈 Existing Issues:

  • Inconsistent UI labels (e.g., "Add to Cart" instead of "Donate") (Causes_ ThematicAnalysis UserConcerns, 2024 Dec – Causes_ThematicAnalysis_UserConcerns.pdf).
  • Poor filter placement affects usability (Causes_ Core Flows Usability Research Nov '24 – Causes_Core_Flows_Usability_Research.pdf).

5️⃣ Social Proof & Trust

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Read reviews or testimonials.
  • See the latest donor activity.
  • Verify cause legitimacy (e.g., verified badges, endorsements).

📊 Metrics:

  • NPS (≥75% for trust) – Likelihood to recommend based on platform trust.
  • SUPR-Q Trust Score – Overall perception of legitimacy.
  • Engagement Rate – Clicks on testimonials, donor lists.

📈 Existing Issues:

  • Users hesitant to donate due to lack of clear impact updates (Causes_ ThematicAnalysis UserConcerns, 2024 Dec – Causes_ThematicAnalysis_UserConcerns.pdf).
  • No verification for donor credibility (N2N (P2P) Sandbox Survey Results, Dec '24 – N2N_P2P_Sandbox_Survey_Results.pdf).

6️⃣ Emotional Engagement & Persuasion

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Read a personal impact story.
  • Watch a video testimonial.
  • Engage with emotional triggers (e.g., urgent causes).
  • Decide to donate based on emotional connection.

📊 Metrics:

  • CSAT (≥6.5 on engagement) – Satisfaction with emotional appeal.
  • Engagement Rate – Interactions with stories, videos, images.
  • SUPR-Q Emotional Appeal – Does the content inspire action?

📈 Existing Issues:

  • Emotional triggers are weak for major donors (N2N (P2P) Sandbox Survey Results, Dec '24 – N2N_P2P_Sandbox_Survey_Results.pdf).
  • Video testimonials underutilized (N2N_ Personas Survey Plan Dec '24 – N2N_Personas_Survey_Plan.pdf).

7️⃣ Post-Donation Engagement

📌 Tasks to baseline:

  • Receive and open a thank-you email.
  • Track the impact of donations.
  • Share donation activity with friends.

📊 Metrics:

  • Engagement Rate – Email open and click-through rates.
  • CSAT (≥6.5) – Satisfaction with follow-up process.
  • Repeat Donor Rate – Percentage of returning donors.

📈 Existing Issues:

  • No automatic updates for fundraisers (N2N_ FundRaising Research Needs – N2N_FundRaising_Research_Needs.pdf).
  • Donors unsure if funds reach the intended cause (N2N_ FundRaising Research Needs – N2N_FundRaising_Research_Needs.pdf).

🔗 Next Steps

  1. Create a UX Scorecard with the above metrics for ongoing benchmarking.
  2. Conduct usability testing to validate baseline scores.
  3. Refine personas and engagement strategies based on observed pain points.
  4. Integrate A/B testing for checkout flows, navigation tweaks, and donor engagement strategies.
  5. Monitor key KPIs over time to track improvements.
