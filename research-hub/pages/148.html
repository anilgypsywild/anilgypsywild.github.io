Report: Mixed-Methods Research to Guide Causes Email Engagement Design

I. Overview

This study evaluated four Compassion Causes email templates using the 4D UX Scorecard and UCD process. 

By combining behavioral testing (click tracking, 5-second impression), survey insights, and UX benchmarks (SUS, CSAT, NPS, MWTP), we explored how email design affects clarity, engagement, trust, and giving intent.

Question

What action does this encourage? 62% said learn, 54% said donate - dual purpose works

MWTP (Marginal Willingness to Pay)

Donation Range

% Users
$25 (max) 34%
$10 or less 28%
$0 or "No Again" 16%
Mean MWTP $16.50

Themes from Rationale:

  • Budgeting Across Causes: Most split giving across orgs - test loyalty framing
  • Trust Building: Users start low, then give more with proven impact
  • Strategy: Some give based on calculated disposable income or giving schedule

Recommendations

  • Add emotional trust markers near CTA (e.g., child faces, short testimonial)
  • Clarify visual framing on preview copy (highlight "What this helps fund")
  • Test an inline progress bar or urgency meter

Template 2: About Compassion

Purpose & Flow

Intent: Introduce Compassion International’s mission to new or curious donors.
Design Goal: Build trust, generate click-through to sponsorship and resources.

5-Second Test Findings

Key Takeaway

Details
Recognized Brand

~40% identified Compassion directly
Core Association 40% noted helping children; 20% mentioned mission/faith
Email Type Identified as a primer for new users or an explainer

First Click Heatmap Summary

Area Clicked

Engagement

Interpretation
Top CTA Button (Hero) High Strong message-visual-CTA alignment
Resource Links (Learn More, Join Us) Medium Interest exists but under-performs visually
Stats Section Low Adds credibility but is not actionable
Footer CTA Low Passive fallback, not primary driver

Survey Results

Question

Top Insight
Perceived Goal? 70% said donation, 40% said sponsorship - strong overlap
Curiosity Score (1–7) 74% said 5–7, 36% gave 7 - strong pull to learn more
Clarity of next step 84% rated 5–7 - journey clarity is high

Open Text Insight

  • Many found the content trustworthy but slightly long.
  • Some users missed that more content existed below fold - consider scroll cues.
  • Users appreciated emotional tone, but wanted quicker donation path cues.

Recommendations

  • Improve visual separation in "Ways to Give" section - test icons or cards
  • Scroll cues needed: minor stickiness or progress indicator
  • Emphasize sponsorship path CTA more explicitly (e.g., badge or banner)

Template 3: Monthly Newsletter

Purpose & Flow

Design Goal: Showcase multiple causes grouped by theme or status (Urgent, Funded, Passion-Based).

5-Second Test Findings

Key Takeaway

Details
Purpose Clarity 50% mentioned "updates", 30% saw donation appeal - works as hybrid
Layout Fatigue Some users felt “scroll heavy” – indicates too many stacked blocks

Area Clicked

Survey Results

Question

Top Insight
Main Goal? 68% said donate, 52% said content
Willingness to explore? 78% rated 5–7, 34% gave 7 - high browsing intent
Layout feedback 90% said easy to scan, but open text: too many choices below

Recommendations

  • Test accordion format to reduce scroll fatigue (progressive disclosure)
  • Separate "Fully Funded" into a celebration format - better as confirmation email
  • Consider filters or personalization to group causes dynamically
