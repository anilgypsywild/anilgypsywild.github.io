Introduction and Context

At Compassion Causes, we are committed to empowering believers to live out their faith through impactful generosity and action. As we refine and expand our platform, understanding our users’ motivations, behaviors, and trust factors via persona-research is critical.

This persona research is designed to deliver actionable insights through a quantitative survey targeting key user segments, enabling us to build a platform that is emotionally resonant, trustworthy, and aligned with user needs.

Objective

The primary goal of this research is to gather data-driven insights that will:

  1. Identify and validate key user personas, including next-generation users, major donors, influencers, and faith-based organizations.
  2. Uncover the emotional and trust factors that drive generosity and platform loyalty.
  3. Inform design, engineering, and product decisions to optimize user experience, increase engagement, and foster deeper connections with our mission.

Target Personas

This survey will engage current and potential users across different roles, including:

  • Individual Contributors and Major Donors: To understand motivations, preferences, and trust indicators for giving.
  • Churches and Faith Organizations: To explore collaborative fundraising needs and tools.
  • College Groups and Young Influencers: To identify emerging trends in next-generation engagement and digital fundraising methods.

Methodology

A 15–20 minute online survey w will be deployed, covering the following key areas:

  1. User Demographics and Habits: Basic data on who our users are and how they interact with our platform.
  2. Fundraising Behaviors: Insights into their motivations, methods, and goals when supporting causes.
  3. Persona Validation: Specific questions targeting behaviors and preferences for each persona segment.
  4. Trust, Persuasion, and Emotional Connection: Factors influencing trust in the platform and emotional resonance with campaigns.
  5. Satisfaction and Feedback: Quantitative metrics like CSAT and NPS, along with open-ended feedback to identify improvement opportunities.

Proposed Deliverables

  • Persona Profiles: Data-backed refinement or expansion of our user personas.
  • Key Behavioral Metrics: Insights into what drives engagement, trust, and generosity.
  • Emotion and Trust Framework: A guide to creating emotionally resonant and trustworthy experiences on the platform.

Why Persona Research Matters

  1. Impact on Design:
    • Understanding user motivations will help our neighbours in UX-Design craft intuitive, inspiring interfaces.
    • Emotional resonance and storytelling preferences will inform content and visual design strategies.
  2. Impact on Engineering:
    • Persona-specific insights will guide our neighbours in Engineering in prioritizing features, like analytics for influencers or collaboration tools for churches.
    • Feedback on platform usability will drive performance improvements and scalability.
  3. Impact on Product Strategy:
    • Data-driven personas and segmentation will enable our Product neighbours to align roadmap priorities with user needs.
    • Key satisfaction metrics will provide benchmarks for measuring product success.
  4. Impact on Organizational Goals:
    • This research aligns with Compassion Causes’ mission by equipping the team with actionable insights to foster deeper connections with users and amplify the impact of their generosity.
