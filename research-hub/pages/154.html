Study Overview

Objective:

Validate the FK&C ideal concert-goer journey by testing the emotional, behavioral, and spiritual impact of different pitch and engagement moments across a fan's journey: before, during, and after the concert.

Recommended Research Method

Method

Why

Notes

1:1 Zoom Interviews (n = 8–12)

 Deep dive into emotional reactions and preferences Recruit across a mix of Compassion-aware and unaware fans. Use video to test reactions.

Journey Walkthrough with Scripted Storyboard

 Simulate experience for immersion Ask participants to close their eyes and walk through an audio-enhanced or narrated storyboard.

Follow-up Online Survey (optional)

 Rank concepts quantitatively Useful if you want to validate top features or rank engagement moments at scale later.

Key Hypotheses to Test

ID

Hypothesis
H1 The digital pitch experience (QR/text) is perceived as more convenient and emotionally resonant than the traditional hand-raise moment.
H2 Offering fan perks (e.g., exclusive content) meaningfully increases urgency to sponsor a child.
H3 A virtual sponsor-child connection moment is emotionally powerful and builds trust—not perceived as exploitative.
H4 The redesigned pitch moment increases understanding of child sponsorship and distinguishes Compassion from other organizations.
H5 Concert-goers feel a stronger bond to their favorite artist when they see them support child sponsorship efforts.

Interview Script Flow (Draft)

  • Warm-up / Context
    • When was your last Christian concert? Which artist?
    • What did you enjoy most about that experience?
  • Walkthrough Journey (Scripted Experience)
    • Ask participant to close their eyes and listen to a narrated or guided walk-through (e.g., entrance → concert pitch moment → QR/text → post-concert follow-up)
  • Engagement Reactions
    • What stood out emotionally?
    • Which moment would most likely lead you to sponsor a child?
    • Emoji rank or satisfaction rank of each moment.
  • Pitch Moment Deep Dive
    • How did the pitch make you feel?
    • Was there a feeling of guilt or pressure? Why or why not?
    • Which method (QR/text/hand raise) would most likely move you to action?
  • Perks and Artist Connection
    • What kind of content or perks would excite you from your favorite artist?
    • Would access to that motivate you to sponsor a child?
    • Do you feel more connected to the artist after this journey?
  • Sponsorship Understanding & Brand Distinction
    • What is your understanding of Compassion now?
    • How is Compassion different from other orgs, if at all?
    • Any moments that felt unclear, uncomfortable, or inauthentic?
  • Wrap-up
    • Open feedback: What did you like or not like?
    • Would you share this experience with others?

Recommendations for Execution

  • Use an audio-enhanced script or narrated video to simulate the journey (vs. just Miro visuals).
    • If using Miro, presenter mode + screen share with added voice-over can work.
  • After each journey moment, pause to ask how they feel and rate it.
  • Consider capturing non-verbal reactions on video.
  • Record sessions (with permission), tag key emotional and behavioral moments in synthesis.
