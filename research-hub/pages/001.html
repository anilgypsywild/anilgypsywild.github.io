📓 Research Brief: Compassion Causes – Donor Motivations (MaxDiff Study)

🎯 Objective

  • This research is to understand the drivers that lead to first-time and repeat donations for the Compassion Causes platform.
  • The study will focus on identifying and quantifying via MaxDiff (Best-Worst Scaling) the most important motivational factors influencing donor behavior.
  • The broader aim is to capture overall donor motivations and behaviors in order to inform product, design, and communication strategies.

🥼 Key Research Questions

  1. What are the primary factors that motivate a donor to give for the first time on the Compassion Causes platform?
  2. What factors encourage repeat donations and long-term engagement with Causes?
  3. Which platform experiences (e.g., urgency cues, cultural representation, storytelling, progress updates, peer/community influence) have the strongest impact on motivating giving behavior?
  4. How do these motivations differ across donor segments (CurrentGen, NextGen, High-Impact Donors, and casual supporters)?
  5. Which elements reduce the likelihood of donating or diminish trust in the platform?

🗒️ Methodology: MaxDiff Survey

⚖️ MaxDiff (Maximum Difference Scaling) is the sole method for this study. It is chosen because:

  • It forces trade-offs, producing a clear hierarchy of motivations.
  • It avoids bias from rating-scale inflation.
  • It provides relative importance scores for each motivation, directly actionable for design and product teams.

Statistical Design:

  • Participants will be shown sets of 4–5 motivation factors at a time.
  • For each set, they will select the “most motivating” and “least motivating” factor.
  • The design will ensure balanced exposure across all factors and respondents.
  • Output will yield importance scores for each motivation driver, comparable across segments.

💟 Motivation Factors to Test (Preliminary List)

Derived from prior briefs, proposals, and survey drafts. Final wording will be refined into survey-ready MaxDiff statements.

  1. Urgency of cause (e.g., “urgent” label or immediate need)
  2. Emotional storytelling and imagery
  3. Transparency about how funds are used
  4. Progress updates after donating
  5. Cultural representation (flags, maps, local context)
  6. Ease and simplicity of the donation process
  7. Trust in Compassion as an organization
  8. Clear and visible “Donate” call-to-action
  9. Ability to share causes with peers
  10. Seeing collective impact (community or peer-to-peer fundraising)
  11. Ability to support multiple causes
  12. Recognition or gratitude from the organization

🧩 Kanban Task Breakdown

Scoping and Design

  • Review and finalize motivation factor list
  • Convert factors into precise MaxDiff survey items
  • Program survey in Lyssna and R (or other survey platform)

Execution

  • Recruit respondents across donor groups (CurrentGen, NextGen, High-Impact Donors, casual supporters)
  • Administer survey with randomized MaxDiff design
  • Ensure balanced participation across segments

Analysis

  • Calculate relative importance scores for each motivation factor
  • Compare motivational hierarchies across donor groups
  • Identify top drivers of first-time giving vs. repeat giving
  • Highlight factors that decrease motivation or trust

Synthesis & Deliverables

  • Ranked list of donor motivations with importance scores
  • Segment-level comparisons (CurrentGen vs. NextGen vs. High-Impact Donors)
  • Clear recommendations for product, design, and communications based on top motivational drivers
  • Final report with data visualizations and prioritized insights

📊 Success Metrics

  • Completion of a robust MaxDiff dataset with statistically valid sample sizes across donor groups
  • Identification of top 5 motivational drivers overall and per donor segment
  • Actionable prioritization of motivational elements for product, design, and content strategy
