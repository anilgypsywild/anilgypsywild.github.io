In-Situ UX Research: Supporter App Feedback Form

Objective:

This form is designed to help you, a valued Supporter, explore the Supporter App naturally and share your feedback. Your insights will help us enhance the app to better serve you and the causes you support.

Please take your time as you navigate the app, and answer the questions based on your experience. There are no right or wrong answers—just your honest thoughts. Thank you for your generosity and for helping us improve this platform.

Step 1: First Impressions & Login

Instructions: Open the Supporter App and log in. Spend a moment looking around.

Reflection Questions:

  • What stands out to you when you first enter the app?
  • What do you think you can do from this screen?
  • Was it clear where to find information about your past donations?

Feedback Submission:

  • Quick SMS: "On a scale of 1-5, how clear was the homepage?"
  • Voice Memo (1 min): "Share your first impressions."

Step 2: Exploring Impact Stories

Instructions: Navigate to the ‘Impact’ section and browse through the updates. Select one that interests you.

Reflection Questions:

  • Does this story feel personal and connected to your giving?
  • Was the content (text, images, stats) engaging?
  • Would this kind of reporting encourage you to continue supporting?

Feedback Submission:

  • Screenshot + Text: "Share a screenshot of an impact story you liked or found confusing and a quick comment."
  • WhatsApp Text: "Would you donate again based on these stories? Yes/No + Why?"

Step 3: Notifications & Updates

Instructions: Check your notifications in the app.

Reflection Questions:

  • Do these updates feel helpful and relevant?
  • Which notification was the most meaningful to you?
  • Would you prefer to receive updates differently (e.g., email, weekly digest)?

Feedback Submission:

  • Quick SMS: "Thumbs up/down: Are notifications useful?"
  • Short Call (5 min): "Which update felt most relevant and why?"

Step 4: Commenting & Engaging with the Community

Instructions: Leave a comment on an impact post and observe the process. If your comment is blocked, review the message you receive.

Reflection Questions:

  • Was it easy to comment and engage?
  • If your comment wasn’t posted, was the reason clear to you?
  • Would you like to engage more with the community? What would make that easier?

Feedback Submission:

  • Quick SMS: "Rate ease of commenting 1-5."
  • Screenshot: "Send a screenshot of any moderation message you receive."

Step 5: Final Reflections & Wrap-Up

Instructions: Reflect on your overall experience using the app.

Reflection Questions:

  • How would you describe your overall experience?
  • What would make this app better for you as a Supporter?
  • Would you recommend this app to another Supporter? Why or why not?

Feedback Submission:

  • Final SMS: "Rate the app 1-10 and share 1 thing you liked & 1 thing to improve."
  • Voice Memo (2 min): "Summarize your thoughts on using this app."

Thank you for your valuable insights. Your generosity makes a difference, and your feedback helps us make the Supporter App better for you and others like you! May God bless you for your kindness and stewardship.

Appendix: Summary of Feedback Methods

Step Key Focus How to Share Feedback
First Impressions Homepage clarity & navigation Quick SMS or Voice Memo (1 min)
Exploring Impact Relevance & engagement Screenshot + Comment or WhatsApp Text
Notifications Usefulness & frequency Quick SMS or Short Call (5 min)
Community Engagement Commenting & moderation SMS Rating or Screenshot of moderation issue
Final Reflections Overall experience & recommendations Final SMS Rating or Voice Memo (2 min)

Why This Form Works for Supporters:

  1. Simple & Flexible – They can share feedback in the way that works best for you.
  2. Reflective & Meaningful – Feedback focuses on your user experience rather than rigid tasks.
  3. Lightweight & Actionable – Quick SMS, short calls, and screenshots keep it fast and easy.
  4. Designed for High Impact Donors – Ensures we hear from Supporters who care deeply about impact and giving.
