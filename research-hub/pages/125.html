Research ⏯️ Product Collaboration

A shared workspace for ongoing alignment between Product Management and Research

Welcome

This page will be our working space to track questions, research needs, decisions, and milestones related to Compassion Causes.

Using GitLab instead of Teams or email helps us

  • stay organized
  • maintain continuity
  • and document the work

required to bring Product initiatives from questions to validated insights.

This also gives both of us a consistent source of truth as you settle into the Product Manager role and ensures visibility into the research effort supporting product outcomes.

Purpose of This Page 🎯

This space will help us:

• Track your questions as you come up to speed
• Capture ongoing context for your product needs and roadmap priorities
• Document decisions and clarify research requirements
• Maintain a traceable record of collaboration and effort
• Keep research tied to product milestones, engineering deliverables, or measurable user impact 🌡️

This page will be living and continuously updated as we move forward.

Research Intake Expectations 🥼

To keep research aligned with Product, Design, Engineering, and Leadership:

All research requests must flow through the Research Intake Form.

This is essential for three reasons:

  1. Accountability and Resourcing
    Research requires time, recruitment, tooling, and analysis. I must be able to justify this effort to [name omitted] and the leadership team.
  2. Impact and Prioritization
    The form tells me what product milestone, feature, or engineering dependency the research must support.
    Without a clear milestone or decision point, research becomes activity without product impact, which is not acceptable for CVG.
  3. Portfolio Visibility
    The intake form allows us to track what studies are happening, who requested them, what product goal they support, and how findings influence roadmap decisions.
    This protects both of us and ensures the research is taken seriously.

If the request is not tied to a specific milestone, user problem, or decision the team must make, I cannot take it on.

Collaboration Model

How we will work best together:

• You add questions here as they come up
• We clarify which items require research and which require simple guidance
• Any research item that requires time, analysis, or participants must go through the Research Intake Form
• I will provide you with insights, briefings, and summaries aligned to your roadmap
• We will revisit this page weekly or biweekly to stay aligned

This creates transparency and avoids lost context across Teams threads or scattered emails.

Research Process Summary 🥪

If helpful, here is a quick view of Research standard workflow:

  1. Clarify the user problem and the product decision
  2. Align on milestone and success criteria
  3. Select method: usability, survey, A B testing, concept validation, or mixed methods
  4. Execute rapid RITE cycles when appropriate
  5. Deliver insights tied directly to roadmap decisions

This helps ensure research is actionable and not academic.
