About

Research that is methodologically rigorous and operationally useful

My work sits at the intersection of UX research, quantitative decision support, and product strategy. I build studies that go beyond insight theater by producing evidence teams can actually prioritize against.

Across the projects in this portfolio, the common thread is turning ambiguity into a usable system: a clearer conversion baseline, a more reliable persona architecture, a better trust model, or a stronger measurement framework.

What I optimize for

  • Research questions anchored to real product decisions
  • Methods matched to uncertainty rather than habit
  • Quant and qual used together, not in silos
  • Outputs that survive handoff into roadmap and design work

Toolkit

Methods I reach for when the decision matters

Usability and experience diagnosis

Task-based studies, friction analysis, baseline scorecards, and conversion breakpoints that reveal where journeys fail and why.

Quantitative survey design

MaxDiff, forced ranking, Likert modeling, segmentation, and preference measurement built to reduce bias and sharpen tradeoffs.

Persona and segmentation systems

Persona frameworks grounded in observed behavior, motivational structure, and measurable product implications rather than fiction.

Decision-ready synthesis

Readouts, prioritization frames, KPI mapping, and artifact design that help stakeholders act on research instead of admiring it.

Process

A repeatable path from ambiguity to action

01

Frame the decision

Clarify the business risk, the unanswered question, and what would actually change if the team learned something new.

02

Match the method to the uncertainty

Use usability testing, quantitative preference design, analytics, interviews, or blended approaches based on the decision at stake.

03

Collect both behavior and meaning

I prefer evidence that explains not only what users do but also why they hesitate, trust, abandon, or commit.

04

Translate research into operating artifacts

Persona maps, scorecards, prioritization tables, user stories, and roadmap-ready recommendations make the work durable.
